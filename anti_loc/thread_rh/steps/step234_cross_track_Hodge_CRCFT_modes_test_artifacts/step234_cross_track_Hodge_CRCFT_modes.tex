\section{Purpose}

This step tests whether the CRCFT modes derived on the RH track and BSD-verified at step 233 also classify the Hodge track's active cycle-column structure.

The three modes are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{TE}: closure is target-equivalent in the declared scope.
\item \textbf{CTMT}: closure reduces to carrier-native terminal computations.
\item \textbf{BF}: comparison or transport from a support carrier to the target fails without target-strength bridge content.
\end{itemize}

\section{Hodge residual readout}

The Hodge track defines
\[
V_{X,p}=\mathrm{Hdg}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q},\qquad
A_{X,p}=\mathrm{Alg}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\subseteq V_{X,p},
\]
and
\[
\XiH(X,p)=I-P_{A_{X,p}}.
\]
The faithful reformulation is
\[
\XiH(X,p)=0 \Longleftrightarrow A_{X,p}=V_{X,p}.
\]

For the active Fermat fourfold lane the scoped residual is
\[
\Xi_H^{\mathrm{std}}(X^4_{33},2)\ne 0,
\]
where the standard/induced Fermat dictionary misses a residual sector. Repair requires a nonstandard codimension-2 surface or correspondence column passing the cycle-column gates.

\section{Cycle-column gates}

The Hodge records list six gates:
\begin{enumerate}
\item algebraic codimension-2 surface on \(X^4_{33}\);
\item containment in the Fermat hypersurface;
\item Chow/cohomology cycle-class map;
\item Fermat character projector;
\item nonzero residual quotient column;
\item Hodge--Riemann metric/projection plus rational/cyclotomic descent.
\end{enumerate}
