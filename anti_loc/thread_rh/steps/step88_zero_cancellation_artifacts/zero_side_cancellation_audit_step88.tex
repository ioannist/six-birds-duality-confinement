\section{Purpose}

After the paired completed Weil feature gate, the active low-frequency obstruction is the legal zero of a paired shift/CND carrier.  If the completed paired symbol has
\[
\Psi(\xi)\sim c|\xi|^{2r}
\quad (\xi\to 0),
\]
then positivity does not imply coercivity.  A zero-side readout can be controlled by this carrier only if it either cancels at the legal zero with a uniform witness, or the carrier supplies an independent low-frequency source.

This note audits the first option: zero-side cancellation.

\section{Setup}

Let \(Y^{-}\) be the completed anti-invariant response space and suppose a translation-side paired carrier is diagonalized by a nonnegative symbol
\[
q_{\rm cmp}[f]=\int \Psi(\xi)|\widehat f(\xi)|^2\,d\xi,
\qquad
\Psi(\xi)\simeq c|\xi|^{2r}\text{ near }0 .
\]
Let \(g_y=V_Z^*y\) denote the representer of a zero-side anti-invariant readout recombination \(y\).  The local capacity contribution is
\[
\operatorname{Cap}_{0}(y)
\sim
\int_{|\xi|<\varepsilon}
\frac{|\widehat g_y(\xi)|^2}{\Psi(\xi)}\,d\xi .
\]

\section{Cancellation order is not enough without a uniform witness}

\begin{proposition}[Local cancellation threshold]
Suppose in local dimension \(d\),
\[
\Psi(\xi)\gtrsim |\xi|^{2r},
\qquad
|\widehat g_y(\xi)|\lesssim |\xi|^{s}.
\]
Then the local capacity integral is finite near the legal zero if
\[
s>r-\frac d2 .
\]
In the one-dimensional shift-zero case \(r=1,d=1\), this is
\[
s>\frac12 .
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
In polar coordinates the local integral is bounded by
\[
\int_0^\varepsilon \rho^{2s-2r}\rho^{d-1}\,d\rho,
\]
which converges precisely when \(2s-2r+d>0\).
\end{proof}

\begin{warning}[Pointwise cancellation is not a uniform membrane]
The statement \(\widehat g_y(0)=0\), or even first-order cancellation for each fixed \(y\), does not by itself give a uniform anti-localization membrane for a family of recombinations.  A sequence of probes may vanish at \(\xi=0\) while concentrating closer and closer to the legal zero, causing
\[
\int |\widehat g_y(\xi)|^2/\Psi(\xi)\,d\xi
\]
to diverge.
\end{warning}

\section{Uniform primitive gate}

The useful cancellation certificate is not merely a pointwise order statement.  It is a bounded factorization through the legal zero.

\begin{definition}[Uniform primitive / derivative factorization]
A zero-side readout family satisfies the uniform primitive gate if for every recombination \(y\) there exists \(h_y\) such that
\[
\widehat g_y(\xi)=\xi\,\widehat h_y(\xi)
\]
and
\[
\|h_y\|^2\le y^*\Theta_{\rm prim}y .
\]
Equivalently, \(V_Z^*\) factors as
\[
V_Z^*=D\,H,
\]
where \(D\) is the derivative multiplier \(i\xi\) and \(H\) is a bounded response-to-primitive map.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Derivative-factorized low-frequency capacity bound]
Assume
\[
\Psi(\xi)\ge c|\xi|^2
\]
near the legal zero and \(\Psi(\xi)\ge m(\xi)>0\) away from it.  If
\[
\widehat g_y(\xi)=\xi\widehat h_y(\xi)
\]
with
\[
\|h_y\|^2\le y^*\Theta_{\rm prim}y,
\]
then the low-frequency capacity contribution satisfies
\[
\int_{|\xi|<\varepsilon}
\frac{|\widehat g_y(\xi)|^2}{\Psi(\xi)}\,d\xi
\le
c^{-1}y^*\Theta_{\rm prim}y .
\]
If the high-frequency region is separately budgeted, then the full capacity is bounded.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On the low-frequency region,
\[
\frac{|\widehat g_y(\xi)|^2}{\Psi(\xi)}
=
\frac{|\xi|^2|\widehat h_y(\xi)|^2}{\Psi(\xi)}
\le
c^{-1}|\widehat h_y(\xi)|^2.
\]
Integrating gives the result.
\end{proof}

\section{Oddness is not a gap}

On the log side \(Jf(t)=\overline{f(-t)}\).  For real functions, anti-invariance \(Jf=-f\) means \(f\) is odd, hence \(\widehat f(0)=0\).  But this does not yield a uniform membrane.  Odd packets can concentrate arbitrarily close to \(\xi=0\).  For example
\[
\widehat g_\varepsilon(\xi)
=
N_\varepsilon \xi e^{-\xi^2/(2\varepsilon^2)}
\]
has \(\|g_\varepsilon\|_2=1\), but relative to \(\Psi(\xi)\sim \xi^2\),
\[
\int \frac{|\widehat g_\varepsilon(\xi)|^2}{\xi^2}\,d\xi
\simeq \varepsilon^{-2}.
\]
Thus anti-invariance supplies formal cancellation but not a uniform low-frequency budget.

\section{Zeta-specific status}

For the RH root-composite route, the separating readout
\[
\psi_-(s)=\Re(s)-\frac12
\]
detects off-critical zeros.  This is a fixed-locus separation record.  It is not the same as a low-frequency cancellation record for the log-side paired Weil carrier.

Therefore the zero-side cancellation path requires an additional operator statement:
\[
V_Z^*=D\,H
\quad\text{with }H\text{ bounded on the completed anti-invariant response space,}
\]
or an equivalent moment/primitive certificate.  Without this, the cancellation route remains support-only.

\section{Conclusion}

The zero-side cancellation alternative is viable only in its uniform primitive form.  Mere anti-invariance, oddness, or fixed-locus separation does not resolve the low-frequency legal zero of the paired Weil carrier.

The next viable alternative is therefore to test whether the zeta carrier supplies a source-coercivity ladder over the low-frequency anti-invariant sector, or to construct the required primitive factorization for \(V_Z\).
