
# Nonclaim boundary for Step 88

This step does not prove RH and does not prove the zeta zero-side readout has the required cancellation.

It proves only:

1. Low-frequency capacity is finite under a cancellation-order condition.
2. For a probe family, pointwise cancellation is not enough.
3. A uniform primitive / derivative factorization is a sufficient low-frequency cancellation certificate.
4. Anti-invariance or oddness alone does not produce a spectral gap.
5. The RH readout Re(s)-1/2 separates the critical line but does not by itself resolve the log-frequency legal zero.

Status of the zero-side cancellation route: promising only if a bounded primitive factorization for V_Z is constructed.
