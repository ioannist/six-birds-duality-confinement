# Step 38 — Root-Composite RH Obligation Theorem

## Goal

Assemble Steps 32–37 into one RH-facing theorem schema:

\[
\text{anti-linear duality}
+
\text{explicit-formula domination}
+
\text{character-source lower frame}
+
\text{anti-invariant budget collapse}
\Rightarrow
\text{zero confinement}.
\]

This is not an RH proof. It states the precise records that would make the root-composite anti-localization route prove zero confinement.

## Main theorem

Let \(J\) be an anti-linear involution and let \(Y^-\) be the anti-invariant response sector. Let \(Z\) be a zero/root ledger with anti-invariant readout

\[
V_Z:Y^-\to \ell^2(Z,w),
\]

and displacement matrix

\[
\mathsf A_Z=V_Z^*V_Z.
\]

Let the completed explicit-formula carrier currency be

\[
\mathsf K_{\mathrm{cmp}}=W_{\mathrm{cmp}}^*W_{\mathrm{cmp}}.
\]

If the following records are accepted:

\[
\mathsf A_Z\preceq \mathsf K_{\mathrm{cmp}},
\]

and

\[
\mathsf K_{\mathrm{cmp}}\preceq \Theta^-,
\]

then

\[
\mathsf A_Z\preceq \Theta^-.
\]

Consequently, for every \(\varepsilon>0\),

\[
\sum_{z:\|P_-\psi(z)\|\ge\varepsilon} w_z
\le
\frac{\operatorname{tr}\Theta^-}{\varepsilon^2}.
\]

If \(\Theta^-=0\), all visible root mass lies on the fixed locus of \(J\).

For RH,

\[
J(s)=1-\overline{s},
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Fix}(J)=\{s:\Re(s)=1/2\}.
\]

Thus the theorem says: if off-critical zero displacement is dominated by completed anti-invariant carrier currency, and that currency budget collapses, then zeros are confined to the critical line.

## Character-source corollary

If character-source records produce

\[
\mathsf K_{\mathrm{cmp}}\preceq \Lambda^{-1}\Theta_0^-,
\]

then

\[
\sum_{z:\|P_-\psi(z)\|\ge\varepsilon}w_z

\frac{\operatorname{tr}\Theta_0^-}{\Lambda\varepsilon^2}.
\]

If a strict-extension ladder has \(\Lambda_n\to\infty\), then positive-distance off-fixed mass tends to zero along the accepted ladder.

So explicit-formula defects, missing gamma/pole/support terms, and source-frame defects become explicit off-critical allowance.

## Layman interpretation

The critical line is the mirror of the completed zeta symmetry. An off-line zero has a measurable mirror imbalance. Step 38 says that if the completed explicit formula makes every such imbalance pay through the anti-invariant carrier currency, and if the root-composite character sources collapse that currency budget, then off-line zeros have no room to exist.

But this is still an obligation theorem. It does not construct the actual RH carrier, the explicit-formula contraction, or the character-source lower frame.
