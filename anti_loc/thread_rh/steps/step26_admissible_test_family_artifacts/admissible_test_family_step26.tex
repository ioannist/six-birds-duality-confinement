\section{Purpose}

Steps 23--25 upgraded scalar probe capacity to a finite predictive probe-family currency matrix.  This note adds the missing Six-Birds-facing gate: when is a mathematically bounded family an \emph{admissible anti-localization test family}?  The answer is not just the matrix inequality
\[
  \widehat K_j \Loewner \Theta.
\]
That inequality is the mathematical budget.  The admissibility gate also requires protocol honesty, readout agreement, route/transport records, all-six-channel statuses, and no-smuggling/no-overreading records.

The point of this note is deliberately formal.  We do not simulate a Six Birds substrate.  We define the test-family record and prove the consequences of the record.

\section{Predictive family currency recap}

At refinement depth $j$, let
\[
  \Gamma_j:E_j\to H_j,
  \qquad
  C_j\succeq0,
  \qquad
  C_{\Gamma,j}:=\Gamma_j^*C_j\Gamma_j,
\]
where $\Ran\Gamma_j$ is the exact packaged carrier.  Let
\[
  L_j:H_j\to Y_j
\]
be a finite-dimensional native probe family and let
\[
  R_j:Y_j\to Y_*
\]
transport responses into a common response space.  The transported predictive family currency matrix is
\[
  \widehat K_j
  :=
  R_jL_j\Gamma_j C_{\Gamma,j}^\dagger\Gamma_j^*L_j^*R_j^*.
\]
Null-mode legality means
\[
  \Ker C_{\Gamma,j}\subseteq \Ker(R_jL_j\Gamma_j).
\]
Under this condition, for every recombination $y\in Y_*$,
\[
  y^*\widehat K_jy
  =
  \Cap_{C_j}\bigl(y^*R_jL_j;\Ran\Gamma_j\bigr).
\]
Predictive family anti-localization is the common budget
\[
  \widehat K_j\Loewner\Theta
  \qquad
  \text{for all accepted }j.
\]

\section{The anti-localization test-family record}

\begin{definition}[Anti-localization test-family record]
An anti-localization test-family record is a tuple
\[
\mathsf T
=
\bigl(
\mathsf{Cl},\,J,\,\{H_j,E_j,\Gamma_j,C_j,L_j,R_j\}_{j\in J},\,
Y_*,\Theta,\mathsf P,\mathsf V,\mathsf A,\mathsf S,\mathsf N
\bigr),
\]
where:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(T\arabic*)]
\item $\mathsf{Cl}$ is the formed closure/layer record.  Anti-localization is asserted only inside this formed closure.
\item $J$ is the accepted refinement/staging index set.
\item $(H_j,E_j,\Gamma_j,C_j)$ is the exact packaged carrier and audit energy at depth $j$.
\item $L_j:H_j\to Y_j$ is the declared native probe family at depth $j$.
\item $R_j:Y_j\to Y_*$ is the declared response transport to a common response space.
\item $\Theta\succeq0$ is the declared predictive family budget on $Y_*$.
\item $\mathsf P$ is the protocol/route record, including route-union declarations when several routes are claimed.
\item $\mathsf V$ is the visibility/suppression record.
\item $\mathsf A$ is the audit record.
\item $\mathsf S$ records statuses for all six primitive channels $P_1,\dots,P_6$.
\item $\mathsf N$ is the nonclaim record forbidding substrate-level literalization, undeclared probes, diagonal-only overreads, and current-only overreads.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Accepted anti-localization test family]
A record $\mathsf T$ is an accepted anti-localization test family if the following gates pass.
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.8cm]
\item[G0: formed closure] $\mathsf{Cl}$ is accepted.  Non-closed artifacts are outside scope.
\item[G1: exact package] each $(\Gamma_j,C_j)$ is declared on the exact packaged carrier, not a public shadow.
\item[G2: null legality] $\Ker C_{\Gamma,j}\subseteq\Ker(R_jL_j\Gamma_j)$ for every accepted $j$.
\item[G3: family declaration] the whole native response space $Y_*$ is declared; recombinations $y\in Y_*$ are in scope.
\item[G4: matrix budget] $\widehat K_j\Loewner\Theta$ for all accepted $j$.
\item[G5: protocol honesty] all route/protocol variants claimed by the statement are either included in one route-union/block family budget or explicitly excluded with a nonclaim.
\item[G6: readout agreement] probes claimed to be the same public readout have controlled difference-capacity after transport.
\item[G7: predictive stability] the transports $R_j$ and the common budget $\Theta$ are stable under refinement, or the defect is recorded and absorbed in the budget.
\item[G8: all-six-channel record] $P_1,\dots,P_6$ have explicit statuses; missing $P_1,P_5,P_6$ records downgrade the claim from accepted to support-only.
\item[G9: no-smuggling/no-overreading] $L_j,\Gamma_j,R_j,\Theta$ are selected upstream of the test and do not depend on successful $S_6$ margin evaluation; public shadows are not literalized as lawful witnesses.
\end{description}
\end{definition}

\section{The accepted test-family theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Accepted family anti-localization]
If $\mathsf T$ is an accepted anti-localization test family, then every native predictive recombination has bounded capacity:
\[
  \sup_{j\in J}
  \Cap_{C_j}\bigl(y^*R_jL_j;\Ran\Gamma_j\bigr)
  \le
  y^*\Theta y
  \qquad
  (y\in Y_*).
\]
Consequently there are no native predictive recombination needles inside the formed closure.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By null legality and the probe-family capacity identity,
\[
  \Cap_{C_j}\bigl(y^*R_jL_j;\Ran\Gamma_j\bigr)
  = y^*\widehat K_jy.
\]
By the matrix budget gate $\widehat K_j\Loewner\Theta$, this is at most $y^*\Theta y$ for every accepted $j$.  Taking the supremum over $j$ gives the claim.  The assertion is limited to native recombinations $y\in Y_*$ and to the formed closure recorded in $\mathsf{Cl}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Closure-only scope]
This theorem deliberately says nothing about raw substrates, unformed artifacts, hidden probes, or public shadows.  A non-closed object may contain needles; that is not a counterexample.  The theorem is a no-native-needle result for formed closures/layers only.
\end{remark}

\section{Protocol honesty as a block-family theorem}

A protocol-honest claim must not certify each route separately and then overread those diagonal certificates as a union certificate.  The correct object is a block currency matrix.

Let a common exact carrier $(\Gamma,C)$ be equipped with protocol-indexed transported probe families
\[
  \widetilde L^p:=R^pL^p:H\to Y_p,
  \qquad p\in\mathcal P.
\]
Stack them:
\[
  \mathbb L u := (\widetilde L^p u)_{p\in\mathcal P}
  \in
  \mathbb Y:=\bigoplus_{p\in\mathcal P}Y_p.
\]
Then the route/protocol block currency is
\[
  \mathbb K
  :=
  \mathbb L\Gamma C_\Gamma^\dagger\Gamma^*\mathbb L^*.
\]
Its $(p,q)$ block is
\[
  K^{pq}=\widetilde L^p\Gamma C_\Gamma^\dagger\Gamma^*(\widetilde L^q)^*.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Protocol-block anti-localization]
Assume null legality for the stacked family.  Then for every mixed protocol recombination $y=(y_p)_{p\in\mathcal P}\in\mathbb Y$,
\[
  \Cap_C(y^*\mathbb L;\Ran\Gamma)
  =y^*\mathbb K y.
\]
Thus a protocol-union claim is accepted only from a block budget
\[
  \mathbb K\Loewner\mathbb\Theta,
\]
which implies
\[
  \Cap_C(y^*\mathbb L;\Ran\Gamma)
  \le y^*\mathbb\Theta y.
\]
Diagonal route budgets $K^{pp}\Loewner\Theta_p$ alone do not certify mixed-protocol recombinations.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
This is the probe-family capacity theorem applied to the stacked family $\mathbb L$.  The warning about diagonal route budgets follows because the quadratic form $y^*\mathbb Ky$ depends on the off-diagonal blocks $K^{pq}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{example}[Diagonal protocol checks do not control the route union]
Let $H=\mathbb C$, $C=1$, $\Gamma=1$, and let $r$ protocols all use the same scalar probe $\widetilde L^p u=u$.  Then $K^{pp}=1$ for every $p$, so every route-local scalar certificate passes at level $1$.  But
\[
  \mathbb K=\mathbf 1_r\mathbf 1_r^*,
\]
whose top eigenvalue is $r$.  The normalized all-ones recombination has capacity $r$.  Hence diagonal protocol checks understate the union danger by an arbitrary factor.
\end{example}

\section{Readout agreement and transfer of budgets}

Protocol honesty also requires that different probes claiming to represent the same public readout agree after transport.  The agreement should be measured in capacity, not merely pointwise notation.

\begin{definition}[Difference-capacity readout defect]
For two transported families $\widetilde L^p,\widetilde L^q:H\to Y$ on the same exact carrier, define
\[
  D^{pq}:=(\widetilde L^q-\widetilde L^p)\Gamma C_\Gamma^\dagger\Gamma^*(\widetilde L^q-\widetilde L^p)^*.
\]
We call $D^{pq}$ the readout-agreement currency defect.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Budget transfer under readout agreement]
Let
\[
  K^p=\widetilde L^p\Gamma C_\Gamma^\dagger\Gamma^*(\widetilde L^p)^*.
\]
If $K^p\Loewner\Theta_p$ and $D^{pq}\Loewner\Delta_{pq}$, then for every $t>0$,
\[
  K^q
  \Loewner
  (1+t)\Theta_p+(1+t^{-1})\Delta_{pq}.
\]
In particular, a budget can be transferred from protocol $p$ to protocol $q$ only with an explicit readout-agreement defect charge.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write $\widetilde L^q=\widetilde L^p+E^{pq}$, where $E^{pq}=\widetilde L^q-\widetilde L^p$.  For any response vector $y$,
\[
  \|(C_\Gamma^\dagger)^{1/2}\Gamma^*(\widetilde L^q)^*y\|^2
  =
  \|a+b\|^2,
\]
where
\[
  a=(C_\Gamma^\dagger)^{1/2}\Gamma^*(\widetilde L^p)^*y,
  \qquad
  b=(C_\Gamma^\dagger)^{1/2}\Gamma^*(E^{pq})^*y.
\]
The inequality
\[
  \|a+b\|^2\le(1+t)\|a\|^2+(1+t^{-1})\|b\|^2
\]
gives
\[
  y^*K^qy\le(1+t)y^*K^py+(1+t^{-1})y^*D^{pq}y.
\]
Using $K^p\Loewner\Theta_p$ and $D^{pq}\Loewner\Delta_{pq}$ proves the Loewner inequality.
\end{proof}

\section{No-smuggling and no-overreading gates}

\begin{definition}[Upstream selection]
Let $\mathcal U$ denote the upstream-visible data: formed closure, exact carrier, declared probe family, response transports, threshold/refinement records, and public readout instruments.  A selection rule for $(\Gamma,L,R,\Theta)$ is upstream if it is measurable with respect to $\mathcal U$ and does not use the target $S_6$ success predicate
\[
  \widehat K_j\Loewner\Theta
\]
or any hidden probe outside the declared response family.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[No post-hoc test-family promotion]
A family selected by optimizing or filtering on successful anti-localization, such as
\[
  L\in\operatorname*{argmin}_{L'}\lambda_{\max}\bigl(K(L')\bigr),
\]
without an upstream selection record is not an accepted anti-localization test family.  Its best possible status is \texttt{support-only}; if the dependence is hidden, the status is \texttt{smuggled} or \texttt{overread}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Accepted test-family status requires G9.  A selection rule depending on the target success predicate is not upstream-visible and therefore fails the gate.  The numerical truth of the resulting matrix inequality can remain support evidence, but it cannot serve as an accepted test-family certificate because it uses target-layer information to choose the test itself.
\end{proof}

\section{All-six-channel anti-localization record}

\begin{definition}[All-six-channel anti-localization status]
For an accepted family claim, the record must include statuses for:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Role & Anti-localization reading & Typical gate \\
\midrule
$P_1$ & carrier rewrite / gauge repair & rewrite declared or absent-with-record \\
$P_2$ & feasible channels / conditioning & null legality, frame, family closure \\
$P_3$ & route / holonomy & protocol block budget, route-union ledger \\
$P_4$ & staging / sectors / refinement & predictive transport and defect summability \\
$P_5$ & packaging / canonicalization & exact package, idempotence, upstream selector \\
$P_6$ & audit / pricing & currency matrix, budget, audit monotonicity \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[All-six-channel downgrade]
If an anti-localization claim asserts a formed layer without explicit $P_1,\dots,P_6$ statuses, then the strongest justified status is \texttt{support-only}.  If the missing channel is used implicitly, the status is \texttt{overread} or \texttt{smuggled} depending on visibility.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The accepted test-family gate includes all-six-channel status as G8.  A missing channel prevents the record from distinguishing exact package, route protocol, and audit budget failures.  Therefore the claim cannot be promoted to accepted family anti-localization.  If the proof relies on the missing channel without record, the no-smuggling/no-overreading rule applies.
\end{proof}

\section{Summary theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Admissible predictive family membrane theorem]
Let $\mathsf{Cl}$ be a formed closure/layer.  Suppose $\mathsf T$ is an accepted anti-localization test-family record on $\mathsf{Cl}$.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item all declared native recombinations have uniformly bounded predictive capacity;
\item mixed protocol recombinations are controlled only when the block protocol budget is accepted;
\item budgets transfer across readouts only through explicit difference-capacity defects;
\item diagonal-only, current-only, route-local, public-shadow, or post-hoc-selected certificates do not promote to accepted anti-localization;
\item the no-needle conclusion is scoped to the formed closure and declared native response family.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Part (i) is the accepted family anti-localization theorem.  Part (ii) is the protocol-block theorem.  Part (iii) is the readout-transfer theorem.  Part (iv) follows from the no-smuggling/no-overreading gate and the diagonal counterexample.  Part (v) is the closure-only scope of the record.
\end{proof}
