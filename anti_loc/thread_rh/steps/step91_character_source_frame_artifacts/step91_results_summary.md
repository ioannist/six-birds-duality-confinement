# Step 91: Dirichlet/Hecke character-source frame abstraction

This step formalizes the character-source route to anti-invariant budget collapse.

The key object is the response-side source frame

\[
F_n=\sum_{\chi\in\mathcal X_n}\lambda_{\chi,n}Q_\chi^*\Theta_\chi^{-1}Q_\chi.
\]

If

\[
F_n\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1},\qquad \Lambda_n\to\infty,
\]

then the anti-invariant currency collapses:

\[
K_n^-\preceq \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_0^-+E_{{\rm src},n}.
\]

The important warning is that finite conductor windows or trace-only sources do not provide a full lower frame on an infinite completed anti-invariant response space. They are support evidence unless they carry a tail/exhaustivity or Plancherel record.

Dirichlet characters are a strong finite-quotient source family because complete character sets are tight frames on finite abelian groups. Hecke/idele-class characters are the more plausible completed-carrier route, but require a global carrier and explicit Plancherel/explicit-formula records.
