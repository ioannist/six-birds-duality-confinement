# Nonclaim Boundary — Step 81

This step does not prove RH and does not prove the Weil--Douglas bridge.

It proves a gamma constant decomposition and a signed-to-positive balance identity under an explicit sign convention. It does not determine the exact sign orientation of the completed Weil convention. That orientation must be fixed by the actual carrier/exact formula record.

The gamma slot has a positive paired difference representation, but its separated diagonal diverges. The divergent diagonal cannot be used as free completion payment.

The pole/completion slot is not automatically positive; it must be represented by a positive finite-rank feature or recorded as a defect.

Finite tail/support terms remain support-only until an exhaustive or fixed-ledger tail record is supplied.
