# Step 151 nonclaim boundary

Step 151 does not prove RH.

Step 151 does not prove `H_R = 0`.

Step 151 does not prove that shifted Sonin/prolate packets lie in Burnol's co-Poisson subspace.

Step 151 explicitly rejects the shortcut:

```text
raw log shift => zeta-factorized Mellin transform
```

A log shift/scaling operation gives a nonvanishing Mellin multiplier. It cannot by itself create a `zeta(s)` factor.

The step proves only the exact direct-exclusion criteria and the residual object:

```math
Xi^{BC}_{ell,a} = B_{ell,a}^* Pi_{Y_a} B_{ell,a}.
```

If this residual is nonzero and no budget is supplied, any RH claim must be scoped away from the missed residual sector.
