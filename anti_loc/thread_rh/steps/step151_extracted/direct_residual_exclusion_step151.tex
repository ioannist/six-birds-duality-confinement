\begin{abstract}
Step 150 shows that the positive source-frame/positive fixed-ledger trace squeeze has become diagnostic rather than proof-producing: the missing source-budget condition is already collapse-strength. Step 151 therefore pivots to the direct residual-exclusion problem on the Burnol--Sonine residual carrier. The active target is
\[
        H_R=0,
        \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
        \Pi_{Y_a}\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}=0
\]
for every finite-place log-shift mode \(\ell\). We prove the exact adjoint evaluator criterion, the exact co-Poisson/zeta-factor certificate, and the obstruction statement: a raw log-shift does not create a zeta factor. Thus direct residual exclusion is not earned by the current construction. The remaining object is the explicit adequacy residual
\[
        \Xi^{\BC}_{\ell,a}=\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}^{*}\Pi_{Y_a}\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}.
\]
\end{abstract}

\section{Carrier and residual block}

Let \(0<a<1\). Burnol's framework supplies a Hilbert space \(L_a\), the zero-evaluator span
\[
        Y_a=\overline{\Span\{Y^a_{\rho,k}:\zeta(\rho)=0,
        0\le k<m_\rho\}},
\]
and the co-Poisson subspace
\[
        P_a=Y_a^\perp .
\]
Let \(P_\infty\) denote the imported archimedean Sonin/prolate projection and let \(\tau_\ell\) denote the finite-place log-shift mode associated to \(\ell\in\mathcal L\). Let
\[
        \mathfrak B_{\ell,a}
        =J_aP_\infty\tau_\ell(I-P_\infty),
\]
where \(J_a\) transports the shifted boundary block into Burnol's \(L_a\)-carrier.

\begin{definition}[Burnol--Sonine residual carrier]
The completed residual carrier is
\[
        H_R
        =\overline{\Span\{\Ran\Pi_{Y_a}\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}:
        \ell\in\mathcal L\setminus\{0\}\}}
        \subseteq Y_a.
\]
\end{definition}

Thus \(H_R=0\) precisely when every shifted boundary packet is invisible to Burnol zero evaluators.

\section{Direct residual-exclusion criteria}

\begin{theorem}[Equivalent direct exclusion criteria]
For a fixed \(\ell\), the following are equivalent.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\Pi_{Y_a}\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}=0\).
\item \(\Ran \mathfrak B_{\ell,a}\subseteq P_a\).
\item For every legal input \(u\) and every zero evaluator \((\rho,k)\),
\[
        M(\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}u)^{(k)}(\rho)=0.
\]
\item For every zero evaluator \((\rho,k)\),
\[
        \mathfrak B_{\ell,a}^{*}Y^a_{\rho,k}=0.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The equivalence of (1) and (2) follows from \(P_a=Y_a^\perp\). The equivalence of (1), (3), and (4) follows from the reproducing/evaluator identity
\[
        \langle f,Y^a_{\rho,k}\rangle=M(f)^{(k)}(\rho)
\]
for \(f\in L_a\), plus density of the zero-evaluator span in \(Y_a\).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Adjoint zero-evaluator test]
Assume \(P_\infty=P_\infty^*\), \(\tau_\ell^*=\tau_{-\ell}\), and \(J_a\) is a bounded transport into \(L_a\). Then
\[
        \boxed{
        \mathfrak B_{\ell,a}^{*}Y^a_{\rho,k}
        =(I-P_\infty)\tau_{-\ell}P_\infty J_a^*Y^a_{\rho,k}.
        }
\]
Therefore
\[
        \boxed{
        \Pi_{Y_a}\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}=0
        \iff
        (I-P_\infty)\tau_{-\ell}P_\infty J_a^*Y^a_{\rho,k}=0
        \quad\forall(\rho,k).
        }
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
This is immediate from the definition
\(\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}=J_aP_\infty\tau_\ell(I-P_\infty)\)
and the adjoint relations.
\end{proof}

This is the first exact formula for the direct route. It says that direct exclusion is equivalent to a transported zero-evaluator invariance condition under the shifted Sonin/prolate projection.

\section{Co-Poisson certificate}

\begin{theorem}[Zeta-factor certificate]
Suppose that for every legal input \(u\), there is a legal function \(\alpha_{\ell,u}\) such that
\[
        M(\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}u)(s)=\zeta(s)\alpha_{\ell,u}(s),
\]
with the correct Burnol support, pole, endpoint, and Sonine records. Then
\[
        \Pi_{Y_a}\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}=0.
\]
Equivalently, \(\Ran\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}\subseteq P_a\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If \(\rho\) is a zero of \(\zeta\) of multiplicity \(m_\rho\), then
\[
        \left(\zeta(s)\alpha_{\ell,u}(s)\right)^{(k)}\big|_{s=\rho}=0
        \quad (0\le k<m_\rho),
\]
provided the product is legal at \(\rho\). Hence all zero evaluators annihilate \(\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}u\), and the direct exclusion criteria apply.
\end{proof}

\begin{warning}[Raw log-shift obstruction]
A log-shift/scaling operation does not create a \(\zeta\)-factor. In Mellin variables it contributes a nonvanishing exponential/power multiplier. Therefore the implication
\[
        \text{shifted Sonin/prolate boundary packet}
        \Longrightarrow
        \zeta(s)\text{-divisible Mellin transform}
\]
is not automatic. Any zeta factor must come from a genuine co-Poisson synthesis, a deeper projection cancellation, or an additional declared source/quotient record.
\end{warning}

\section{Adequacy residual if direct exclusion fails}

When the direct exclusion criterion is not proved, the active residual is
\[
        \boxed{
        \Xi^{\BC}_{\ell,a}
        =\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}^{*}\Pi_{Y_a}\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}
        \succeq0.
        }
\]
For all legal \(u\),
\[
        \langle u,\Xi^{\BC}_{\ell,a}u\rangle
        =\|\Pi_{Y_a}\mathfrak B_{\ell,a}u\|_{L_a}^{2}.
\]
Thus \(\Xi^{\BC}_{\ell,a}\) is exactly the part of the shifted boundary-packet sector seen by Burnol zero evaluators.

\begin{corollary}[Direct route trichotomy]
For every shift mode \(\ell\), exactly one of the following records is needed.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Exact exclusion:} \(\Xi^{\BC}_{\ell,a}=0\).
\item \textbf{Budgeted residual:} \(\Xi^{\BC}_{\ell,a}\preceq E_{\ell,a}\) with a declared vanishing or controlled defect budget.
\item \textbf{Scoped nonclaim:} the theorem is restricted to the orthogonal complement of the visible residual sector.
\end{enumerate}
\end{corollary}

\section{Step 151 verdict}

The direct residual-exclusion route is the correct proof-producing pivot after the source-budget obstruction. However, it is not closed by the current construction. Burnol gives the exact criterion \(P_a=Y_a^\perp\), and co-Poisson gives the exact zeta-factor certificate, but the raw shifted Sonin/prolate block does not visibly satisfy that certificate.

The next analytic object is therefore
\[
        \boxed{
        (I-P_\infty)\tau_{-\ell}P_\infty J_a^*Y^a_{\rho,k}.
        }
\]
If this vanishes for all zero evaluators, then \(H_R=0\). If not, its norm distribution is the first direct measurement of \(\Xi^{\BC}\).
