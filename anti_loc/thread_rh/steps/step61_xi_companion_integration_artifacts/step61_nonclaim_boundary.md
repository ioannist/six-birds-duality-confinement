
# Step 61 nonclaim boundary

The main manuscript's reference to the \(\Xi\)-companion is not a claim that every formed layer has exact adequacy.  It states only that promotion from native membrane to layer-dissolving membrane requires an adequacy residual record.

The main manuscript does not prove the full transfer/repair calculus for \(\Xi\).  That belongs to the standalone companion paper.

The companion \(\Xi\)-paper does not prove that a native membrane is accepted.  It assumes native and dissolving probe families on a legal quotient and analyzes the residual blind spot between them.
