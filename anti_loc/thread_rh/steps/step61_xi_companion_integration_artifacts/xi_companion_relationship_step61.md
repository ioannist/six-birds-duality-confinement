# Xi companion relationship inserted into the main manuscript

## Main paper role
The formed-layer membrane paper uses \(\Xi_C(D\mid L)\) as an adequacy gate. It does not reprove the full \(\Xi\)-calculus.

The main paper needs only this interface:

\[
\text{native membrane} + \Xi\text{-adequacy budget}
\Rightarrow
\text{layer-dissolving membrane}.
\]

## Companion paper role
The companion paper develops \(\Xi\) as a standalone theory of blind-spot currency:

\[
\Xi_C(D\mid L)
=
K_{DD}-K_{DL}K_{LL}^{\dagger}K_{LD}.
\]

It proves:

1. projection identity: \(\Xi=T_D(I-P_L)T_D^*\),
2. optimal residual identity,
3. exact adequacy criterion,
4. strict-extension monotonicity,
5. transfer/data-processing,
6. public-shadow warnings,
7. obstruction/repair calculus.

## Nonclaim boundary
A native membrane does not automatically imply safety against layer-dissolving probes. That promotion requires \(\Xi=0\) or a declared \(\Xi\)-budget.

A public-shadow \(\Xi\)-bound does not promote to intrinsic adequacy without reconstruction and defect records.
