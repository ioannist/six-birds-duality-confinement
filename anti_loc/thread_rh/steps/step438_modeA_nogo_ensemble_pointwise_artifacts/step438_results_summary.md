# Step 438 Results Summary

## Result

`C_ensemble_distributional_not_pointwise` is promoted to theorem-grade as a structural no-go for ensemble-only Route 2 constructions.

## Theorem-grade reason

Erdős-Schlein-Yau and Tao-Vu universality identify limiting `k`-point correlation measures through vague/test-function convergence. These theorems do not determine individual eigenvalues. Montgomery and Odlyzko show that zeta zeros are statistically GUE-like, but statistical universality cannot select the exact deterministic sequence `{gamma_n}`.

## Counterexample mechanism

If `{gamma_n}` has the Montgomery/GUE pair-correlation limit, then `{gamma_n+1}` has the same normalized pair-correlation limit. Universality cannot distinguish the two. Therefore it cannot imply `Spec(H)={gamma_n}`.

Mark `C_ensemble_distributional_not_pointwise` as theorem-grade with proof reference to Step 438. Keep it active for any Route 2 grammar that relies on random matrix universality without an independent pointwise spectral mechanism.
