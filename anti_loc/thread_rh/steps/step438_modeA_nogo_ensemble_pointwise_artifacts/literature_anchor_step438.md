# Step 438 Literature Anchor

## Erdős-Schlein-Yau

Erdős, Schlein, and Yau proved bulk universality for Wigner matrices: under broad moment and normalization hypotheses, local bulk correlation functions converge to the universal sine-kernel limit. The relevant convergence is convergence against test functions, i.e. measure-level/vague convergence of local correlation measures.

## Tao-Vu

Tao and Vu's four-moment and related universality results show that broad classes of generalized Wigner ensembles share the same local eigenvalue statistics under matching moment hypotheses. Again, the output is distributional local statistics, not a deterministic eigenvalue list.

## Wigner-Dyson-Mehta

The Wigner-Dyson-Mehta universality paradigm predicts that local eigenvalue statistics in broad random matrix classes are governed by symmetry class: GUE for complex Hermitian systems with broken time-reversal symmetry, with sine-kernel local correlations in the bulk.

## Montgomery 1973

Montgomery's pair correlation conjecture predicts that normalized nontrivial zeta-zero spacings have pair correlation

```text
R_2(alpha) = 1 - (sin(pi alpha)/(pi alpha))^2,
```

matching GUE pair correlation.

## Odlyzko 1987/2001

Odlyzko's computations verify Montgomery-GUE statistics for zeta zeros to high numerical precision at very large heights. This is powerful evidence for statistical agreement but not a pointwise spectral construction.
