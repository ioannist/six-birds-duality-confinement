# Nonclaim boundary for Step 77

Step 77 does not claim RH.

It does not claim that the classical Weil explicit formula has been converted into a positive feature package.

It does not claim that the gamma, pole/completion, or tail slots have been matched.

It does not claim that the Douglas contraction exists.

It proves only the log-side autocorrelation algebra and the prime-slot diagonal repair rule:

\[
\sum_a w_a\|\tau_a f-f\|^2
=
2(\sum_a w_a)h_f(0)-\sum_a w_a(h_f(a)+h_f(-a)).
\]

Thus any RH bridge using a positive prime feature must account for the diagonal term \(2\sum_a w_a\,h_f(0)\).
