# Nonclaim boundary — Step 148

Step 148 does not prove RH.

It does not prove the independent source-work record

\[
\sup_N\operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)<\infty.
\]

It proves that this record cannot be replaced by a fixed positive weight schedule once the cumulative source frame has a diverging lower bound.

It also does not license an \(N\)-dependent shrinking ledger. Such a ledger would be moving-window evidence, not a fixed completed residual ledger.

The valid conclusion is only:

\[
F_N^\Omega\succeq\Lambda_NG_R,
\quad
\Lambda_N\to\infty,
\quad
\sup_N\operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)<\infty
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{tr}(G_RK_R^\omega)=0.
\]

Thus the source-work bound is the next load-bearing analytic target, not a routine normalization detail.
