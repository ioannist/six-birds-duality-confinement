# Step 226 Results Summary

## Target

Test whether the Bridge Impossibility Corollary, derived on the RH track at step 189, also holds on BSD.

## BSD Bridge Impossibility Analog

Let `C_BSD` be a carrier with a proved BSD-adjacent closure

`Xi_C_BSD = 0`

that is not equivalent to full BSD. If a typed bridge `B` transports that closure to

`Delta_BSD^BK = 0`

or to full strong BSD, then the composite `C_BSD + B` is BSD-strength. The bridge is not free; it contains the missing BSD content.

## BSD Records Read

The BSD track is diagnostic-complete, not proof-complete. The b-52a bridge defect is

`Delta_BSD^BK = E_an/period + E_ht/reg + E_finite + sum_p E_p + E_det`.

The frontier obligations are `BK_component_maps`, `component_vanishing`, `higher_rank_cycles`, `all_support_primes`, and `global_audit_promotion`.

The BSD records already distinguish imported partial rows from proof-complete closure:

- Gross-Zagier/Kolyvagin rows are low-rank adequacy records.
- Skinner-Urban/Iwasawa rows are p-adic theorem rows under hypotheses and control gates.
- Density rows do not promote to pointwise BSD.
- Finite scalar and `dim Sha[p]` shadows do not promote to determinant-line source columns.

## Candidate Carrier Assessment

Every audited non-BSD-equivalent carrier fails to transport to full BSD without a BSD-strength bridge:

- Mordell-Weil finite generation gives finite generation, not analytic rank or leading coefficient.
- Faltings finiteness is a genus >= 2 rational-points theorem, not BSD for elliptic curves.
- Modularity gives the analytic `L(E,s)` carrier and continuation, not the rank/leading coefficient formula.
- Fermat's Last Theorem is a consequence of modularity, not BSD.
- Iwasawa main conjectures give p-adic/Selmer bridges under hypotheses; full classical BSD needs control, all primes, rank, Sha, and determinant-line gates.
- Gross-Zagier/Kolyvagin and p-converse style results prove rank 0/1 or conditional partial BSD; the bridge to full BSD is precisely the remaining BSD-strength content.

## Literature Audit

Published bridge theorems are powerful but partial:

- Gross-Zagier relates `L'(E,1)` to Heegner heights.
- Kolyvagin Euler systems give rank <= 1 consequences and Sha finiteness in stated settings.
- Skinner-Urban prove Iwasawa main conjectures for `GL_2` under hypotheses, giving partial BSD consequences after control.
- Bhargava-Skinner-Zhang prove a majority rank-BSD result, not pointwise full BSD.
- Modularity supplies the analytic carrier but not the BSD equality.

No audited source gives a non-target-strength bridge from a non-BSD-equivalent theorem to full BSD.

## Verdict

`V_bsd_Bridge_Impossibility_verified`.

The Bridge Impossibility pattern extends from RH to BSD. Partial bridges remain valuable, but any bridge from a non-BSD-equivalent carrier to full BSD imports BSD-strength content.
