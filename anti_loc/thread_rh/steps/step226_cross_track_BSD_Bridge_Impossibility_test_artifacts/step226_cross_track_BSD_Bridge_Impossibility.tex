\section{Statement}

\textbf{BSD Bridge Impossibility Analog.}
Let \(C\) be a BSD-adjacent carrier with a proved closure \(\Xi_C=0\) that is not equivalent to BSD. If a typed bridge \(B\) transports \(\Xi_C=0\) to
\[
\Delta_{\rm BSD}^{\rm BK}=0
\]
or to full strong BSD, then the composite \(C+B\) is BSD-strength. The bridge itself contains the missing BSD content.

\section{BSD Track State}

\section{Literature Audit}

Gross--Zagier supplies the derivative-height bridge. Kolyvagin's Euler systems give rank \(\le 1\) consequences and Sha finiteness in stated settings. Skinner--Urban supply Iwasawa main conjecture rows under hypotheses. Bhargava--Skinner--Zhang give a majority/density theorem. Wiles and BCDT supply modularity, hence the analytic carrier. None of these gives a non-target-strength bridge from a non-BSD-equivalent theorem to full BSD.

\section{Verdict}

\[
\boxed{\texttt{V\_bsd\_Bridge\_Impossibility\_verified}}
\]

Partial bridges are valuable. They do not contradict Bridge Impossibility: they either close scoped BSD cases under hypotheses or leave precisely BSD-strength component-map and vanishing obligations.
