# Step 443 Audit Currency Construction

## A_W typed object

`A_W` is the typed hermitian form

```text
A_W = (W, Test_W, Pos_W)
```

where:

- `Test_W` is a finite admissible test-function space generated by autocorrelation forms `f=g*g*`.
- `W[f]` is the Weil explicit-formula hermitian form.
- `Pos_W` is the typed predicate `W[f] >= 0` for all finite-scope admissible `f`.

## Concrete branch

Concrete `W[f]` may be computed by either side of the explicit formula:

```text
W[f] = sum_rho fhat(gamma_rho)
```

or

```text
W[f] = archimedean[f] + trivial[f] - sum_n Lambda(n) f(log n)/sqrt(n).
```

Both are illegal as construction primitives for this route: the first uses zero heights; the second uses primes/von Mangoldt data.

## Formal-symbol branch

One may instead declare `W` as a formal hermitian symbol with predicate `Pos_W`. This avoids direct arithmetic input, but then no numerical value of `W[f_k]` exists for the dissolving probe family. The adequacy residual cannot be driven to zero or even computed as a target-sensitive quantity from native primitives.

## Outcome

`A_W` reproduces the same meta-pattern as Steps 441 and 442: concrete audit is smuggling; formal audit is insufficient.
