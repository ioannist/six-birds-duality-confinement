\section*{Step 268: Connes-Consani Recoverability Attempt}

\paragraph{Verdict.}
\[
\mathrm{V\_connes\_consani\_recoverability\_blocked}.
\]
The Connes-Consani 2014 and 2018 papers do not imply the cascade-needed
recoverability theorem for the Branch A Burnol/Sonine commutator.

\paragraph{Connes-Consani 2014.}
The arithmetic site is
\[
\mathcal A=(\widehat{\mathbb N^\times},\bar{\mathbb N}),
\qquad
\bar{\mathbb N}=(\mathbb N\cup\infty,\inf,+).
\]
Its points over \(\mathcal O\subset\mathbb R_{\max}\) form
\[
\mathbb Q^\times\backslash \mathbb A_\mathbb Q/\widehat{\mathbb Z}^{\,*}.
\]
The trace formula used is
\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{\rm distr}\left(\int_G h(u)U_u\,d^*u\right)
=\sum_{v\in\Sigma_\mathbb Q}\int_{\mathbb Q_v^*}
\frac{h(u^{-1})}{|1-u|}\,d^*u.
\]
The associated counting distribution satisfies
\[
\frac{\partial_s\zeta_N(s)}{\zeta_N(s)}
=-\int_1^\infty N(u)u^{-s}d^*u,
\qquad
\zeta_N(s)=\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s).
\]

\paragraph{Connes-Consani 2018.}
The zeta-side map is
\[
E(f)(v)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb N^\times}f(nv).
\]
After Fourier transform in the logarithmic variable this corresponds to
multiplication by \(\zeta(is)\).  The Scaling Site is
\[
\mathcal S=(\mathbb R_+\rtimes\mathbb N^\times,\mathcal O),
\]
with sections of \(\mathcal O\) described as convex piecewise affine functions
with integral slopes.  The Riemann-Roch strategy records the criterion
\[
\mathrm{RH}\Longleftrightarrow \langle f,f\rangle\leq0
\]
under the stated degree/codegree constraints, with
\[
\langle f,f\rangle=D\bullet D,
\qquad
D=\int f(\lambda)\Psi_\lambda\,d^*\lambda.
\]
The complex lift uses
\[
C(G)=\mathbb Q^*\backslash(\mathbb A_\mathbb Q\times G)/
(\widehat{\mathbb Z}\times{\rm id}).
\]

\paragraph{Derivation attempt.}
The cascade requires a faithful bridge
\[
J:H_{\rm Sonine}\to H_{\rm CC}
\]
such that \(J P_\lambda J^{-1}\) is a Connes-Consani projection and
\[
J[M_\zeta,P_\lambda]J^{-1}
\]
is a Connes-Consani operator or cohomology class.  It further requires the
essential-norm invariant
\[
\Phi_{\max}(\sigma,\ell)
\]
to be recoverable as a Hochschild or cyclic trace of that class.

\paragraph{Gap.}
Connes-Consani supply an adelic/scaling-site spectral realization and a
Riemann-Roch quadratic-form strategy.  They do not supply the Burnol/Sonine
carrier embedding, the commutator identity, or the essential-norm trace formula.
Thus Branch A recoverability is not score-3; it is a framework-internal bridge
claim unless a new compatibility theorem is proved.
