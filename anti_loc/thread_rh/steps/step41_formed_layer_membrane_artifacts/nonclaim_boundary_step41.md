# Step 41 nonclaim boundary

The formed-layer membrane theorem is deliberately closure-scoped.

It does not claim that all substrates are needle-free. It claims that after a layer is formed, and after its native predictive probe family, exact package, audit, response transport, and witness ledger are accepted, the layer has no unresolved native predictive recombination needle exactly when the transported family currency matrices are bounded by the declared budget.

Non-closed objects may contain needles. Undeclared probes may contain needles. Public shadows may contain needles. Those are not counterexamples; they are outside the accepted membrane claim.
