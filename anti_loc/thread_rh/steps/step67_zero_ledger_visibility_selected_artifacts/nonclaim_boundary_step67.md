# Nonclaim boundary — Step 67

This note does not prove RH or zero confinement for the zeta zeros.

It proves only the ledger-level implication:

- a fixed completed zero ledger squeezed by vanishing budgets gives exact zero ledger;
- an exhaustive finite-window ledger with vanishing tail also gives exact zero ledger;
- moving finite ledgers without tail/exhaustivity records are support-only.

The RH route still requires construction of the actual completed anti-invariant zero ledger, readout separation for the critical line, explicit-formula domination, anti-invariant budget collapse, and zero-side visibility/exhaustivity.
