\section*{Step 214: Branch A Weyl Sequence Diagnostic}

We test the sequence
\[
  u_n={\kappa_{\rho_n,0}^{1/2}\over \|\kappa_{\rho_n,0}^{1/2}\|},
  \qquad \rho_n=1/2+i\gamma_n,\quad n=1,\ldots,10.
\]
The sampled model is the Burnol-boundary kernel
\[
  \kappa_{\rho_n}(\tau)\approx K_{1/2}^{\Gamma}(1/2+i\tau,\rho_n).
\]

\subsection*{Operator}

The Step 173 finite-grid projection is
\[
  Pf=f-\operatorname{sinc}*f-\sum_{j<N}\Psi_j\langle\Psi_j,f\rangle.
\]
With \(\ell=\log 2\),
\[
  C_\ell f=(I-P)e^{i\ell\tau}Pf.
\]
The grid uses 120 Gauss--Legendre nodes in \([-80,80]\) and 24 PSWF terms.

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}

\]
Thus
\[
  \min_{1\le n\le 10}\|C_\ell u_n\|=0.7934538793.
\]
With conservative diagnostic error floor \(0.05\), the lower bound is
\[
  \delta_{10}\ge 0.7434538793.
\]

\subsection*{Verdict}

\[
\boxed{\texttt{V\_branch\_A\_weyl\_essential\_obstruction}}
\]
as a finite-grid diagnostic.  It is not yet a full noncompactness theorem:
one must still prove the sequence is weakly null in the full \(H_\eta\), prove
the lower bound persists as \(n\to\infty\), and settle the exact transport
normalization.
