\section{Purpose}
Steps 130--138 isolated the obstruction to importing the Bui--Pratt--Robles--Zaharescu shifted second-moment theorem as an unrestricted lower frame.  The obstruction is a squarefree Boolean near-null sector of the GCD--log shifted main kernel.  Steps 131--138 showed that the actual residual coefficient class may avoid this sector if the regularized Burnol/M\"untz seed has a controlled high-divisibility tail after passing through the zeta/M\"untz incidence map.

The present step asks whether the needed estimate can be imported from known analytic-number-theory machinery.  It treats the Heap--Soundararajan prime-block and \(\Omega\)-truncation architecture as imported infrastructure and translates it into the membrane gate.

\section{Objects}
Let \(P_y\) be a finite set of small primes, and identify squarefree divisors of
\[
P_y^\#=\prod_{p\le y}p
\]
with subsets \(S\subseteq P_y\).  The finite zeta/M\"untz incidence operator is
\[
  (Z_y b)(T)=\sum_{S\subseteq T}b(S).
\]
Let \(Y_{R,N}\) be the finite residual sector produced by the boundary-to-co-Poisson residual and the Burnol-to-Dirichlet regularized shadow.  Its actual seed synthesis map is
\[
  B_N:Y_{R,N}\longrightarrow \mathcal H_y,
\]
with Gram
\[
  G_{B,N}=B_N^*B_N.
\]
For a declared prime-block cutoff \(K=(K_j)_j\), let
\[
  P_{\Omega\le K}
\]
be the projection onto coefficients satisfying all block conditions
\[
  \Omega_j(S)\le K_j.
\]
The seed-tail gate is
\[
  \sigma_{K,N}
  =
  \left\|
  (I-P_{\Omega\le K})B_NG_{B,N}^{-1/2}
  \right\|.
\]
The incidence-conditioned tail gate from Step 135 is
\[
  \tau_{\Omega,N}^{Z}
  =
  \left\|
  Z_y(I-P_{\Omega\le K})B_NG_{B,N}^{-1/2}
  \right\|.
\]
For a blind Walsh family \(\mathcal B_R\), Step 138 introduced
\[
L_{R,K}(y)=\|\Pi_{\mathcal B_R}Z_yP_{\Omega\le K}\|,
\qquad
T_{R,K}(y)=\|\Pi_{\mathcal B_R}Z_y(I-P_{\Omega\le K})\|.
\]
Thus
\[
\delta_{R,K,N}=L_{R,K}+T_{R,K}\sigma_{K,N}
\]
controls the GCD--log blind residual.

\section{Imported analytic-number-theory architecture}
The relevant imported architecture is not a new proof of lower moments.  It is the structural use of prime blocks and \(\Omega\)-cutoffs.  In the Heap--Soundararajan method one decomposes primes into blocks and builds short Dirichlet polynomials
\[
  \mathcal N(s,\alpha)=\prod_j \mathcal N_j(s,\alpha),
\]
where each block polynomial is truncated by a rule of the form
\[
  \Omega(n_j)\le K_j.
\]
The block thresholds are declared before the moment estimate and are not chosen after seeing a residual direction.

\section{Main import theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Actual seed-tail import trichotomy]
Let \(B_N\) be the actual finite Burnol/M\"untz residual seed synthesis map.  Exactly one of the following records is needed.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \textbf{Exact \(\Omega\)-compatible seed.}  If
\[
  B_N=P_{\Omega\le K}B_N,
\]
then
\[
  \sigma_{K,N}=0
\]
and
\[
  \Xi_{\mathrm{GCD},N}\preceq L_{R,K}(y)^2G_{B,N}.
\]

\item \textbf{Tail-controlled seed.}  If
\[
  \left\|
  (I-P_{\Omega\le K})B_NG_{B,N}^{-1/2}
  \right\|
  \le \sigma_{K,N},
\]
then
\[
  \Xi_{\mathrm{GCD},N}
  \preceq
  \bigl(L_{R,K}(y)+T_{R,K}(y)\sigma_{K,N}\bigr)^2G_{B,N}.
\]
In particular, if
\[
  L_{R,K}+T_{R,K}\sigma_{K,N}\le \delta<1,
\]
then the restricted BPRZ source lower frame keeps a positive floor on the actual residual class.

\item \textbf{Persistent \(\Omega\)-blind residual.}  If no upstream-declared \(K
\) gives
\[
  L_{R,K}+T_{R,K}\sigma_{K,N}<1,
\]
then
\[
  \Xi_{\mathrm{GCD},N}=R_N^*\Pi_{\mathrm{sf}}R_N
\]
remains a genuine residual requiring a separate source-frame record, compact/tail payment, or scoped nonclaim.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first case is immediate from the definition of \(\sigma_{K,N}\).  The second case is the Step 138 blind-projected incidence decomposition
\[
\|\Pi_{\mathcal B_R}Z_yB_NG_{B,N}^{-1/2}\|
\le
L_{R,K}+T_{R,K}\|(I-P_{\Omega\le K})B_NG_{B,N}^{-1/2}\|.
\]
Squaring gives the operator inequality for \(\Xi_{\mathrm{GCD},N}\).  The third case is the contrapositive of the source-floor gate.
\end{proof}

\section{What is imported and what is not}
Heap--Soundararajan supplies an \(\Omega\)-block architecture for constructing short Euler-product mimics.  It does not prove that an arbitrary Burnol/M\"untz seed has small \(\Omega\)-tail.  Burnol supplies the co-Poisson/M\"untz and Sonine carrier in which the seed lives.  It does not impose blockwise \(\Omega\)-cutoffs on arbitrary residual atoms.

Therefore there is no free imported theorem of the form
\[
  \sigma_{K,N}\to0
\]
for a generic Burnol/M\"untz seed.  The lawful route is to build an \(\Omega\)-compatible residual dictionary from the beginning, or to prove a new Burnol-to-\(\Omega\) density theorem.

\section{New defect: \(\Omega\)-compatible Burnol exhaustion}
Let \(\mathcal D_{\Omega,N}\) be an upstream-declared Burnol/M\"untz atom family whose regularized Dirichlet shadows obey the block cutoffs.  Let \(P_{\mathcal D_{\Omega,N}}\) be the response-space projection onto their span.  Define
\[
  \epsilon_{B\to\Omega,N}
  =
  \left\|
  (I-P_{\mathcal D_{\Omega,N}})M_{R,N}G_{R,N}^{-1/2}
  \right\|.
\]
This is the geometric price of insisting that the residual dictionary be source-readable and \(\Omega\)-compatible.  The old seed-tail problem is replaced by the new density problem
\[
  \epsilon_{B\to\Omega,N}\to0
\]
or at least
\[
  \epsilon_{B\to\Omega,N}\le \epsilon<1.
\]

\section{Effective source gate after Step 139}
The restricted BPRZ route now has the form
\[
  R_N^*K_qR_N
  \succeq
  \gamma_q\beta_R(1-\delta_{R,K,N}^2)G_{B,N},
\]
where
\[
  \gamma_q\asymp \log q
\]
is the imported source strength on the nonblind class and
\[
  \delta_{R,K,N}=L_{R,K}+T_{R,K}\sigma_{K,N}.
\]
Thus a uniform floor
\[
  \delta_{R,K,N}\le\delta<1
\]
is already enough for source strength to diverge, provided the finite residual windows promote through a fixed/exhaustive ledger.

\section{No-smuggling record}
The following choices must be declared upstream:
\begin{itemize}

\item thresholds \(K_j\),
\item Burnol/co-Poisson atom family,
\item regularized M\"untz cutoff,

\end{itemize}
No threshold or atom may be selected because it makes the final GCD-log blind residual pass.

\section{Status}
Step 139 does not close RH and does not prove the actual \(\Omega\)-tail estimate.  It records that the only lawful import from Heap--Soundararajan is architectural.  The next constructive target is therefore an \(\Omega\)-compatible Burnol/M\"untz residual dictionary theorem.
