\section*{Step 252: Branch C \(L_{\rho,k}(G)\) versus zeta derivatives}

\paragraph{Completed zeta function.}
We use
\[
\xi(s)=\frac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s).
\]
At a non-trivial zero \(\rho\), both \(\zeta(\rho)\) and \(\xi(\rho)\) vanish,
so derivative comparisons begin at \(k=1\).

\paragraph{Derivative table at \(\rho_1\).}
For \(\rho_1=1/2+14.134725141734693\ldots i\),
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}

\hline
0 & 5.28444\cdot10^{-81} & 9.21239\cdot10^{-84}\\
1 & 0.793160433357 & 0.00138271908922\\

3 & 0.569370575252 & 0.00111290451070\\
4 & 0.520289648618 & 0.000474784799997
\end{array}
\]

\paragraph{Ratio test.}
For the Branch C triple \((\rho_1,G_\star)\),
\[
\frac{|L_{\rho_1,k}(G_\star)|}{|\zeta^{(k)}(\rho_1)|}
=0.365,\ 0.874,\ 1.961,\ 4.233
\quad (k=1,2,3,4),
\]
which is not constant.  The corresponding xi ratios are even less stable:
\[
209,\ 358,\ 1003,\ 4638.
\]
Factorial-normalized variants also fail to produce a stable scalar
relationship.

\paragraph{Growth-rate comparison.}
Step 250 found exponential fit parameters \(b\approx0.61,0.68,0.77\) for the
three Branch C triples.  Successive derivative log-ratios do not match these:
the mean \(\zeta\)-derivative log ratio is \(-0.1405\) at \(\rho_1\) and
\(0.2064\) at \(\rho_2\).

\paragraph{Verdict.}
No simple scalar analytical relation to \(\zeta^{(k)}(\rho)\) or
\(\xi^{(k)}(\rho)\) was identified.  The data remain compatible with a more
complicated Burnol-kernel/projection/test-function dependence.
