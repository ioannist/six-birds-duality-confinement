# Nonclaim boundary

Step 85 does not prove RH and does not prove the Weil-Douglas bridge.

It proves a structural obstruction: paired CND/shift features are positive but vanish at the origin. Therefore positivity alone is not coercivity. Any RH route using the paired prime/gamma carrier must supply a legal-zero or low-frequency record.

The following are support-only unless upgraded:

- finite cutoffs showing positivity of the paired symbol;
- positivity on the anti-invariant subspace without a gap record;
- treating the pole finite-rank slot as if it paid full low-frequency coercivity;
- assuming anti-invariant symmetry removes the zero at xi=0.
