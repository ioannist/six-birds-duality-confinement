\section*{Step 242: Hecke H4 Per-Character Finite Diagnostics}

\subsection*{H4 Specification}

For a Hecke character \(\chi\), H4 asks for finite matrix diagnostics on the
first few zeros of \(L(s,\chi)\). The intended matrix entries are
\[
c_{ij}^{\chi}(\ell)
 =
\left\langle
\kappa_{\chi,i},
(I-P_{\infty,\chi})M_{m_\ell}P_{\infty,\chi}
\kappa_{\chi,j}
\right\rangle,
\]
with Gram matrix
\[
G_{ij}^{\chi}=\langle \kappa_{\chi,i},\kappa_{\chi,j}\rangle.
\]
The finite residual is the compressed matrix-source quantity, for example
\[
\Xi_{\mathrm{matrix}}^{\chi}
 =
\operatorname{tr}\left(
G_\chi^{-1}(c^\chi)^*G_\chi^{-1}c^\chi
\right),
\]
or the equivalent normalized finite diagnostic.

\subsection*{No-Go Discipline}

H4 cannot assume auxiliary GRH for \(L(s,\chi)\), cannot promote finite
character packets to full character-spectrum evidence, and cannot identify
the trivial Hecke scalar \(L\)-function with the Burnol/Sonine carrier.

\subsection*{Literature Audit}

Iwaniec-Kowalski supply the analytic framework for Hecke \(L\)-functions.
Conrey-Iwaniec and related work supply Hecke zero-spacing and family
statistics. Burnol's Fourier/zeta papers supply the zeta/Sonine kernel
machinery and related \(L\)-function context. Recent de Branges/RKHS papers
give reproducing-kernel methods for low-lying \(L\)-function zeros.

These sources do not supply the H4 package: a Hecke projected kernel,
\(\kappa_{\chi,w}\), \(P_{\infty,\chi}\), and the lawful finite
matrix-source residual.
