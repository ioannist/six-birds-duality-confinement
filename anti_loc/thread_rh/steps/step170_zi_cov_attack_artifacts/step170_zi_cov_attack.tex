\section{T2a. Direct Operator Computation}

The full ZI-COV(i) target is
\[
  \Psym\Mz G=\Mz A_\infty G.
\]
If an inherited kernel \(K_\infty(s,s')\) were available, then
\[
  (\Psym\Mz G)(s)
  =
  \int K_\infty(s,s')\zetaF(s')G(s')\,d\mu(s'),
\]
and the condition would be
\[
  \zetaF(s)(A_\infty G)(s)
  =
  \int K_\infty(s,s')\zetaF(s')G(s')\,d\mu(s')
  \quad\text{for all legal }G.
\]
The inherited records do not supply such a \(K_\infty\). They supply instead
the abstract orthogonal projection \(\Psym=\Uinf P_\infty\Uinf^{-1}\). Hence
the exact direct formula is
\[
  A_\infty^{\rm form}G
  =
  M_{1/\zeta}\Psym\Mz G,
\]
where this is a bounded legal operator exactly on domains where division by
\(\zeta\) is controlled and the output satisfies the Burnol carrier records.

\section{T2b. Target-Equivalence Test}

Suppose full ZI-COV(i) holds. Then for every legal \(G\),
\[
  \Psym\Mz G=\Mz A_\infty G.
\]
Let \(\rho\) be a zeta zero in an evaluation region where the Mellin functions
are defined. Then for all \(0\le k<m_\rho\),
\[
  \partial_s^k(\Psym\Mz G)(\rho)=0.
\]
Thus full ZI-COV implies that the projected zeta ideal is annihilated by every
zero-evaluator jet. This is a condition on \(\Psym\):
\[
  Y_{\rho,k}\Psym M_\zeta=0
  \quad\text{on the legal profile class}.
\]
It is not, by itself, a statement that \(\zeta\) has no zeros.

The converse target-equivalence step would require an additional inherited fact:
for each zero \(\rho\) in the projection's alleged spectral region, the family
\(\Psym\Mz G\) can realize a nonzero \(k\)-jet at \(\rho\). No inherited
definition of \(P_\infty\) provides this jet-surjectivity. Moreover, the exact
definition of \(P_\infty\) is Sonin/prolate support-Fourier geometry, not a
projection onto a zeta-zero region. Therefore:
\[
  \boxed{\text{TE result: not target-equivalent from inherited records}.}
\]

\section{T2c. Subclass Proof}

Let \(\Omega\) be a measurable region in the Mellin boundary or typed strip
such that
\[
  \|1/\zeta\|_{L^\infty(\Omega)}<\infty.
\]
Define the zero-free-output subclass
\[
  \mathcal G_\Omega
  =
  \{G\ \text{legal}:
    \Psym\Mz G\ \text{is supported in }\Omega
    \text{ and belongs to the legal Mellin carrier}\}.
\]
For \(G\in\mathcal G_\Omega\), set
\[
  A_\infty^\Omega G
  :=
  M_{1/\zeta}\Psym\Mz G.
\]
Since \(1/\zeta\) is bounded on \(\Omega\), \(A_\infty^\Omega\) is bounded on
this subclass with the inherited Hilbert norm restricted to the output support.
Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \Mz A_\infty^\Omega G
  &=
  M_\zeta M_{1/\zeta}\Psym\Mz G
  \\
  &=
  \Psym\Mz G,
\end{aligned}
\]
so ZI-COV(i) holds on \(\mathcal G_\Omega\).

This subclass is deliberately narrower than the full carrier. It avoids the
unbounded division by \(1/\zeta\) near zeta zeros and therefore does not imply
any global zero-location statement.
