\section{Second Kernel Jet}

Burnol's kernel is recorded as
\[
K_a^\Gamma(z,w)=
\frac{E(z)\overline{E(w)}-E(1-z)\overline{E(1-w)}}{z+w-1}.
\]
Step 247 gave
\[
\partial_{\bar w}K_a^\Gamma(z,w)=
\frac{E(z)\overline{E'(w)}+E(1-z)\overline{E'(1-w)}}{z+w-1}.
\]
Differentiating again gives
\[
\partial_{\bar w}^2K_a^\Gamma(z,w)=
\frac{E(z)\overline{E''(w)}-E(1-z)\overline{E''(1-w)}}{z+w-1}.
\]
The sign is negative in the second term because
\[
\partial_{\bar w}\overline{E'(1-w)}=-\overline{E''(1-w)}.
\]
The denominator contributes no \(\partial_{\bar w}\) term under the inherited \(z+w-1\) convention.
