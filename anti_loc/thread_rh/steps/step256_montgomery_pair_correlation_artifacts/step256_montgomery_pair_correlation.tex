\section*{Step 256: Montgomery Pair Correlation}

\paragraph{Carrier.}
Let \(\rho_n=1/2+i\gamma_n\) be non-trivial zeta zeros, ordered by
\(\gamma_n\).  Normalize
\[
\widetilde\gamma_n=\gamma_n\frac{\log\gamma_n}{2\pi}.
\]
The pair count is
\[
R_2(\alpha,\beta;T)
=\frac{1}{N(T)}
\#\{(n,m):\alpha<\widetilde\gamma_n-\widetilde\gamma_m<\beta,\ n\ne m\}.
\]
The conjectural GUE limit is
\[
R_2(\alpha,\beta;T)\to
\int_\alpha^\beta
\left(1-\left(\frac{\sin\pi u}{\pi u}\right)^2\right)\,du.
\]

\paragraph{Known status.}
Montgomery proved a restricted pair-correlation theorem under RH, usually
phrased in the Fourier-support/range \(|u|<1\).  The full GUE pair-correlation
conjecture is open.  Odlyzko's computations gave strong high-zero numerical
evidence.  Hejhal studied triple correlations, and Rudnick--Sarnak proved
n-level correlation results for principal \(L\)-functions in restricted
support.

\paragraph{Classification.}
Montgomery pair correlation is not known to be RH-equivalent and is not a
single-\(L\) zero-location residual.  It therefore lies outside the Riemann
Dichotomy and outside the single-\(L\) Selberg-Class Dichotomy Generalization.
It does, however, belong to the broader Selberg-Class Cross-Correlation
Extension after subtype refinement.
