\begin{abstract}
This note reorganizes the anti-localization manuscript around its matured thesis: anti-localization is a consequence of a broader formed-layer membrane theory.  A formed layer has a membrane when every native predictive probe recombination has a finite audited shadow price, every layer-dissolving readout is adequate to the native probe family up to an audited residual, and all critical-pair witnesses are completed, priced, or lawfully excluded.  The earlier scalar capacity and Schur geometry remain essential, but they are no longer the title-level object.  The correct paper now has three centers: predictive probe-family currency, adequacy residuals, and exact-six membrane records.  The root-composite RH route and the Navier adequacy map are applications/obligations, not solved claims.
\end{abstract}

\section{What the Paper Is Now}

The previous title family, ``anti-localization and Schur squares,'' understates the object.  Anti-localization is the main consequence, but the proof structure is broader:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \textbf{Currency:} capacity is a shadow-value matrix for a native probe family.
\item \textbf{Membrane:} a formed layer has no native predictive recombination needle exactly when the transported currency matrices stay within a common budget.
\item \textbf{Adequacy:} a native membrane promotes to a layer-dissolving membrane only when $\Xi_C(D\mid L)$ is zero or budgeted.
\item \textbf{Completion:} a failed budget produces a critical-pair witness that must be completed, priced, or lawfully excluded.
\item \textbf{Exact-six acceptance:} the matrix inequality is not by itself an accepted Six Birds claim; all six records must be accepted.
\item \textbf{Root-composite obligation:} RH becomes a root-composite anti-invariant membrane obligation, not a solved theorem.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Core Theorem Stack}

\subsection{Scalar identity pack}
\begin{theorem}[Scalar capacity identities, schematic]
On the legal energy quotient, for a probe $g$,
\[
\ProbeCap_C(g;\Ran\Gamma)
=g_\Gamma^*C_\Gamma^\dagger g_\Gamma
=\int_0^\infty \langle g_\Gamma,e^{-tC_\Gamma}g_\Gamma\rangle\,dt
=\inf_{B_\Gamma^*q=g_\Gamma}\|q\|^2.
\]
If the probe sees a legal null mode, the variational capacity is infinite.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Family currency theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Probe-family currency]
Let $L_\Gamma=L\Gamma$ and $C_\Gamma=\Gamma^*C\Gamma$.  Define
\[
\K_{C,\Gamma}(L)=L_\Gamma C_\Gamma^\dagger L_\Gamma^*.
\]
Then every native recombination $y$ has capacity
\[
\ProbeCap_C(y^*L;\Ran\Gamma)=y^*\K_{C,\Gamma}(L)y.
\]
Thus family anti-localization is $\K_{C,\Gamma}(L)\preceq\Theta$, not diagonal scalar bounds.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Minimum-spend duality}
\begin{theorem}[Resistance matrix]
For desired response $z$, under range and null-mode legality,
\[
\operatorname{Cost}_C(z;L,\Gamma)=z^*\K_{C,\Gamma}(L)^\dagger z,
\]
with infinite cost for $z\notin\Ran L_0$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Membrane theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Formed-layer membrane]
For transported predictive currencies
\[
\widehat K_j=R_jL_j\Gamma_j(\Gamma_j^*C_j\Gamma_j)^\dagger\Gamma_j^*L_j^*R_j^*,
\]
under formed-closure, exact-package, native-family, null-legality, all-six, and completed witness-ledger gates,
\[
\widehat K_j\preceq\Theta\quad\forall j
\]
is equivalent to having no unresolved native predictive recombination needle.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Adequacy residual theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Adequacy residual]
For native probes $L$ and dissolving probes $D$ on the legal quotient, define
\[
\Xi_C(D\mid L)=K_{DD}-K_{DL}K_{LL}^\dagger K_{LD}.
\]
Then
\[
\Xi_C(D\mid L)=\inf_A (D-AL)C^\dagger(D-AL)^*
\]
in Loewner order, and
\[
\Xi_C(D\mid L)=0\iff \ker L_0\subseteq\ker D_0.
\]
Moreover strict native-family extension can only decrease $\Xi$.
\end{theorem}

\section{What Moves to Companion Papers or Appendices}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item \textbf{$\Xi$ companion paper.}  The adequacy residual has enough structure for a standalone article: Schur residual, conditional currency, strict-extension monotonicity, predictive residual ladders, blind-spot applications.
\item \textbf{RH root-composite program.}  The nine-record RH obligation is a research program, not a solved section.  It belongs as an obligation theorem and perhaps a companion program paper.
\item \textbf{Navier adequacy map.}  Keep this short: it is a sanity-check application, not a parallel NS project.
\item \textbf{Toy finite diagnostics.}  Demote to remarks/appendix or omit.  They are support-only diagnostics, not Six Birds simulations.
\item \textbf{Phase diagrams/PICA/quantum/Born-rule speculations.}  Leave out of the main paper.  They are future philosophical/programmatic essays.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Landing / Conclusion Draft}

The paper should conclude with a statement of the following form:

\begin{quote}
Anti-localization is not merely a bound on spatial concentration and not merely a positivity estimate.  It is the membrane condition of a formed layer.  A formed layer earns a no-needle claim only when its native predictive probe family has a finite audited shadow-price matrix, when all recombination critical pairs are completed, when layer-dissolving probes are adequate to the native family up to a recorded residual, and when the record transports through the exact-six channels of rewrite, feasibility, route, staging, packaging, and audit.  Raw substrates, public shadows, and undeclared probes may still contain needles; that is not a defect of the theorem but the theorem's scope discipline.
\end{quote}

For RH:
\[
\boxed{\mathsf A_Z\preceq\mathsf K^-\preceq\Theta^-}
\]
remains an obligation, not a conclusion.  For Navier, the energy/enstrophy ledger leaves a blow-up blind spot unless additional lawful structure controls $\Xi_C(D\mid L)$.

\section{Nonclaim Boundary}

The manuscript should explicitly refuse the following claims:
\begin{enumerate}
\item No claim that all Six Birds systems anti-localize.
\item No claim that raw substrates have no needles.
\item No claim that diagonal scalar probes control recombinations.
\item No claim that public shadows are lawful witnesses.
\item No claim that RH is proved.
\item No claim that Navier regularity is proved.
\item No claim that fixed-package iteration contracts capacity.
\item No claim that native no-needle implies layer-dissolving no-needle without adequacy.
\end{enumerate}
