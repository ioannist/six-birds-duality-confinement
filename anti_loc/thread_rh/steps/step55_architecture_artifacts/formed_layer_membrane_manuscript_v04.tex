\begin{abstract}
This manuscript develops a formed-layer membrane theory.  Anti-localization is one major consequence of the theory, but the central object is broader: a formed layer carries a native predictive probe-family currency matrix, and the layer has a membrane precisely when all native predictive recombinations are priced inside an accepted audit budget.  The scalar capacity identity is refined into a matrix currency theorem
\[
  \K_{C,\Gamma}(L)=L\Gamma(\Gamma^*C\Gamma)^\dagger\Gamma^*L^*,
\]
with reciprocal minimum-spend duality.  The adequacy residual
\[
  \Xi_C(D\mid L)=K_{DD}-K_{DL}K_{LL}^\dagger K_{LD}
\]
is treated as a first-class object: it is the unique optimal blind-spot currency measuring what a layer-dissolving probe family can see that the native probe family cannot.  Predictive membranes, critical-pair completion, all-six-channel records, transfer, membrane taxonomy, type promotion, adequacy under strict extension, and root-composite zero-confinement obligations are stated as one framework.  The Riemann and Navier--Stokes discussions are obligations and demonstrations of scope, not solved claims.  The closure-only scope is constitutive: the theory asserts no-native-needle results only for formed closures/layers with adequate native probe families and accepted all-six audit records.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why the title changed}

The earlier title centered anti-localization.  The correct landing is now broader:
\[
\boxed{\text{formed-layer membrane theory.}}
\]
A formed layer has a membrane when every native way of addressing, recombining, transporting, or dissolving it has an audited price.  Anti-localization is the no-cheap-native-needle theorem inside this membrane theory.

\begin{nonclaim}[No raw substrate theorem]
The theory does not claim that a raw substrate, provisional package, public shadow, or undeclared response family contains no needles.  It claims that a formed closure/layer with an accepted native membrane has no unresolved native layer-dissolving needles within the declared scope.
\end{nonclaim}

\begin{nonclaim}[Applications are obligations unless gates are earned]
The RH and Navier--Stokes sections state obligation schemata.  They do not assert RH or Navier--Stokes regularity.  A claim becomes accepted only when the exact carrier, native family, adequacy record, predictive transport, and all-six audit gates are supplied.
\end{nonclaim}

  \mathsf{Mem}=(E_j,H_j,\Gamma_j,C_j,L_j,D_j,R_j,\Theta,\Xi_j,P_1,\ldots,P_6,\mathsf N).

Here \(\Gamma_j\) is the exact package, \(C_j\) is the audit, \(L_j\) is the native probe family, \(D_j\) is an optional layer-dissolving probe family, \(R_j\) transports responses to a common predictive response space, \(\Theta\) is the native budget, \(\Xi_j\) is the adequacy residual budget, and \(P_1,\ldots,P_6\) are the all-six records.

\subsection{The formed-layer membrane theorem}
At stage \(j\), define
\[
  C_{\Gamma,j}=\Gamma_j^*C_j\Gamma_j,
  \qquad
  L_{\Gamma,j}=L_j\Gamma_j,
\]
and transported family currency
\[
  \widehat K_j
  =R_jL_{\Gamma,j}C_{\Gamma,j}^\dagger L_{\Gamma,j}^*R_j^*.

\begin{theorem}[Formed-layer membrane theorem]
Assume formed closure, exact package, null-mode legality, declared native family, lawful response transport, protocol honesty, predictive stability, all-six-channel records, and completed critical-pair ledger.  Then the following are equivalent at the mathematical ledger level:

  \widehat K_j\Loewner\Theta\quad\text{for every accepted predictive stage }j,

\[
  \text{there is no unresolved native predictive recombination witness }(j,y)

Consequently, for every native recombination \(y\),
\[
  \sup_j \Capacity_{C_j}(y^*R_jL_j;\range\Gamma_j)\le y^*\Theta y.
\]
A Six Birds acceptance claim additionally requires the all-six records and nonclaim boundary.
\end{theorem}

\section{Scalar capacity and probe-family currency}

\begin{definition}[Scalar capacity]
For \(\ell(u)=\langle g,u\rangle\), with \(g_\Gamma=\Gamma^*g\),
\[
  \Capacity_C(\ell;\range\Gamma)=
  \sup_{a\ne0}\frac{|\langle g_\Gamma,a\rangle|^2}{\langle a,C_\Gamma a\rangle},
\]
with infinite capacity if the probe sees a legal null mode.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Scalar identity pack]
Under null-mode legality,
\[
  \Capacity_C(\ell;\range\Gamma)=\langle g_\Gamma,C_\Gamma^\dagger g_\Gamma\rangle
  =\int_0^\infty\langle g_\Gamma,e^{-tC_\Gamma}g_\Gamma\rangle\,dt.
\]
If \(C=B^*B\), then
\[
  \Capacity_C(\ell;\range\Gamma)=\inf\{\|q\|^2:(B\Gamma)^*q=g_\Gamma\}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{definition}[Probe-family currency]
For a native family \(L:H\to Y\), define
\[
  L_\Gamma=L\Gamma,
  \qquad
  \K_{C,\Gamma}(L)=L_\Gamma C_\Gamma^\dagger L_\Gamma^*.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Family capacity theorem]
For every recombination \(y\in Y\),
\[
  \Capacity_C(y^*L;\range\Gamma)=y^*\K_{C,\Gamma}(L)y.
\]
Thus family anti-localization is the Loewner budget
\[
  \K_{C,\Gamma}(L)\Loewner\Theta.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Minimum-spend duality]
For a desired native response \(z\in Y\),
\[
  \Cost_C(z;L,\Gamma)=\inf\{a^*C_\Gamma a:L_\Gamma a=z\}
  =z^*\K_{C,\Gamma}(L)^\dagger z,
\]
provided \(z\in\range L_\Gamma\); otherwise the cost is \(+\infty\).  Hence \(\K\) is compliance/shadow value and \(\K^\dagger\) is resistance/minimum spend.
\end{theorem}

\begin{countermodel}[Scalar probes do not control recombinations]
Let \(H=\mathbb C\), \(C=1\), and \(Lu=u(1,\ldots,1)\in\mathbb C^m\).  Then \(\K=\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^*\).  Every coordinate probe has capacity \(1\), but the normalized all-ones recombination has capacity \(m\).  Diagonal checks are not family checks.
\end{countermodel}

\section{Schur squares and lawful witnesses}

\begin{theorem}[Schur-capacity equivalence]
For
\[
  M=\begin{pmatrix}a&g^*\\ g&C\end{pmatrix},\qquad C\succeq0,
\]
one has \(M\succeq0\) iff \(g\perp\ker C\) and
\[
  g^*C^\dagger g\le a.

\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Family Schur square]
Under null-mode legality,
\[
  \begin{pmatrix}\Theta&L_\Gamma\\L_\Gamma^*&C_\Gamma\end{pmatrix}\succeq0
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \K_{C,\Gamma}(L)\Loewner\Theta.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Compressed internal Schur identity]
Let \(M=M^*>0\), let \(\Phi:\mathbb C^k\to H\) be an isometric low package, and set \(P=\Phi\Phi^*\), \(Q=I-P\),
\[
  A=\Phi^*M\Phi,\qquad B=QM\Phi,\qquad C_Q=QMQ|_{QH}.

Then the compressed Schur margin is
\[
  S=A-B^*C_Q^\dagger B=(\Phi^*M^\dagger\Phi)^{-1}
\]
when the indicated inverse is legal.  This is the lawful same-branch witness; ambient diagonal formulas are public shadows unless a reducing bridge is audited.
\end{theorem}

\section{Adequacy residual \texorpdfstring{\(\Xi_C(D\mid L)\)}{Xi}}

\subsection{Why \(\Xi\) is central}
The native family \(L\) is what a layer officially knows how to sense.  A layer-dissolving family \(D\) represents probes that could break the layer.  The adequacy residual measures what \(D\) can see that \(L\) cannot.

Work on the legal energy quotient \(E_C=(\ker C)^\perp\), with \(C_0=C|_{E_C}>0\).  Write \(L_0,D_0\) for the restrictions.

\begin{definition}[Adequacy residual]
Define
\[
  K_{LL}=L_0C_0^{-1}L_0^*,\qquad
  K_{DL}=D_0C_0^{-1}L_0^*,\qquad
  K_{DD}=D_0C_0^{-1}D_0^*.
\]
The adequacy residual is
\[
  \boxed{\Xi_C(D\mid L)=K_{DD}-K_{DL}K_{LL}^\dagger K_{LD}.}
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[\(\Xi\) as optimal blind-spot currency]
Let
\[
  T_L=L_0C_0^{-1/2},\qquad T_D=D_0C_0^{-1/2},
\]
and let \(P_L\) be the orthogonal projection onto \(\range(T_L^*)\).  Then
\[
  \Xi_C(D\mid L)=T_D(I-P_L)T_D^*\succeq0.
\]
Moreover, with \(A_*=K_{DL}K_{LL}^\dagger\),
\[
  (D_0-AL_0)C_0^{-1}(D_0-AL_0)^*
  =\Xi_C(D\mid L)+(A-A_*)K_{LL}(A-A_*)^*.
\]
Therefore \(\Xi_C(D\mid L)\) is the unique Loewner-minimal residual over all factorizations \(D\approx AL\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Exact adequacy]
The following are equivalent:
\[
  \Xi_C(D\mid L)=0,
\]
\[
  \exists A\text{ such that }D_0=AL_0,
\]
\[
  \ker L_0\subseteq\ker D_0.
\]
Thus exact adequacy is exactly the absence of a legal hidden direction.
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[Adequacy promotion]
If \(K_{LL}\Loewner\Theta_Y\) and \(\Xi_C(D\mid L)\Loewner\Xi_Z\), then
\[
  K_{DD}\Loewner A_*\Theta_YA_*^*+\Xi_Z.
\]
Thus a native membrane plus an adequacy residual budget promotes to a layer-dissolving membrane.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Strict extension monotonicity of \(\Xi\)]
Let \(L^+=\begin{bmatrix}L\\M\end{bmatrix}\).  Then
\[
  \Xi_C(D\mid L^+)\Loewner\Xi_C(D\mid L).
\]
More sharply,
\[
  \Xi_C(D\mid L,M)=\Xi_C(D\mid L)-K_{DM\mid L}K_{MM\mid L}^\dagger K_{MD\mid L}.
\]
Same-family repetition gives equality.
\end{theorem}

\section{Predictive membranes and critical-pair completion}

\begin{definition}[Predictive membrane]
A predictive membrane is a family of transported currencies \(\widehat K_j\) with a common budget \(\Theta\),
\[
  \widehat K_j\Loewner\Theta\qquad\forall j.
\]
A predictive witness is a pair \((j,y)\) with \(y^*(\widehat K_j-\Theta)y>0\).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Summable predictive propagation]
If
\[
  \widehat K_{j+1}\Loewner(1+\varepsilon_j)\widehat K_j+D_j,
\]
with \(\prod_j(1+\varepsilon_j)<\infty\) and \(\sum_jD_j\Loewner D_\infty\), then
\[
  \widehat K_n\Loewner P_\infty(\widehat K_0+D_\infty).
\]
Thus summable defects preserve predictive membranes under a sufficiently large budget.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Critical-pair witness theorem]
For \(\mathsf D=\K-\Theta\),
\[
  \K\npreceq\Theta\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\exists y:y^*\mathsf Dy>0.
\]
Support-minimal witnesses are native critical pairs.  Accepted membrane claims require every native critical pair to be priced, resolved by lawful rewrite, or excluded with a nonclaim record.
\end{theorem}

\section{Navier adequacy demonstration}

This section is deliberately short.  It demonstrates \(\Xi\)-language outside RH without opening a Navier project.

For a Fourier cutoff carrier on \(\mathbb T^d\) with Sobolev audit
\[
  C_{s,N}e_k=(1+|k|^2)^s e_k,
\]
and pointwise derivative probe of order \(q\),
\[
  D_{q,x,N}e_k\sim |k|^qe^{ik\cdot x},
\]
the capacity scales like
\[
  \Capacity_{s,N}(D_q)\asymp\sum_{|k|\le N}\frac{|k|^{2q}}{(1+|k|^2)^s}.
\]
Uniform boundedness requires
\[
  s>q+\frac d2.
\]
Thus in three dimensions, energy \((s=0)\) and enstrophy/dissipation \((s=1)\) do not adequately price pointwise velocity or vorticity/strain blow-up probes.  A Navier membrane proof would require stronger coercive audit, channelized feasible carrier, parabolic smoothing with source-admission, or a scoped nonclaim.
