# Possible standalone Xi companion paper

Working title:

**Adequacy Residuals and Blind-Spot Currency in Formed-Layer Membrane Theory**

Core object:

\[
\Xi_C(D\mid L)=K_{DD}-K_{DL}K_{LL}^{\dagger}K_{LD}.
\]

Core claims:

- Xi is the Schur complement of dissolving currency after conditioning on native currency.
- Xi is the unique Loewner-minimal residual of attempted factorization D ≈ AL.
- Xi = 0 iff the native family sees every legal dissolving direction.
- Xi is monotone under strict native-family extension.
- Same-family repetition does not reduce Xi.
- Predictive Xi ladders require summable residual propagation.

Applications:

- NS: energy/enstrophy vs pointwise blow-up probes.
- Immune systems: known antigens vs novel pathogens.
- Cryptography: declared adversaries vs unknown attacks.
- Logic: declared axioms vs formula space.

This could be a companion paper if the main membrane paper becomes too broad.
