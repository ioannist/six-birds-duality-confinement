# Nonclaim boundary for the Ξ companion paper

The Ξ companion paper does **not** claim:

1. Raw substrates have no dissolving probes.
2. Ξ is automatically zero for formed closures.
3. Public-shadow Ξ bounds promote to intrinsic Ξ bounds without a bridge.
4. Finite-stage adequacy is predictive adequacy.
5. Same-family repetition improves adequacy.
6. Budget repair is proof-strengthening; it weakens the claim unless paired with strict extension.
7. Navier, RH, cryptographic, immune, or logical applications are solved.

It claims only the exact blind-spot residual laws after the carrier, legal quotient, native family, dissolving family, and audit have been declared.
