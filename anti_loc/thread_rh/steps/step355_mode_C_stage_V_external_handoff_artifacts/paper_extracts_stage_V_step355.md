# Step 355 Paper Extracts

Short verbatim extracts are kept narrow.  Page numbers are PDF-text page
indices from the fetched public PDFs.

## Arthur, Functoriality and the Trace Formula

Source: `https://www.math.utoronto.ca/arthur/pdf/arthur-func-and-trace-formula.pdf`

- p. 1, abstract: "apply the trace formula to the principle of functoriality"
- p. 3, Sec. 1: "functoriality can be regarded as an identity between automorphic L-functions"

Relevance: supplies the standard functoriality/trace-formula vocabulary, but
not the specific finite Hecke-to-Burnol/Sonine pairing transfer.

## Sakellaridis, Relative Functoriality and Functional Equations via Trace Formulas

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.03881`

- p. 1, abstract: "scalar transfer factors are replaced by 'transfer operators' or 'Hankel transforms'"
- p. 1, abstract: "prove global comparisons (in the form of Poisson summation formulas)"
- p. 4, Sec. 1.1: "a matching is missing in the case of 'beyond endoscopy'"

Relevance: closest match to `lambda_operator`; it names transfer operators,
but the paper is not a Burnol/Sonine zeta-fiber descent theorem.

## Watson, Rankin Triple Products and Quantum Chaos

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.0425`

- p. 3, abstract: "triple-correlation integrals of eigenforms in terms of central values"
- p. 3, abstract: "explicit formulas for all relevant GL2 Whittaker functions"

Relevance: demonstrates test-function/period-to-L-value transfer technology,
but for triple products and QUE, not H6.

## Langlands, Functoriality and Reciprocity

Source: `https://publications.ias.edu/sites/default/files/functoriality.pdf`

- p. 1: "central issues are still elusive"
- p. 2: "Functoriality in the L-group"

Relevance: confirms the external vocabulary and openness of central
functoriality issues at broad program level.
