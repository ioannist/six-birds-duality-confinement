# Step 264 Nonclaim Boundary

This step does not prove Navier-Stokes regularity, construct a singularity,
claim NS is unprovable, or solve RH, BSD, or Hodge.

The NS Attack Foreclosure Conjecture is a cross-track replication of the
attack-strategy meta-theorem.  It forecloses only carrier pivoting, bridge
import, and cascade-internal computation in isolation from named external
classical content.

It explicitly does not foreclose:

- EXT1 sector-matched radius-window product theorem;
- EXT2 BKM/BG continuation time gate;
- EXT3 sector match;
- EXT4 tail/lift budget;
- Gevrey dominance and derivative-stack fixed-ledger gates;
- a fresh PDE carrier or new typed mechanism;
- an NS proof or disproof in general.

Weak-solution and modified-equation results retain their scoped statuses and do
not promote to Clay smoothness without an NS-strength bridge.
