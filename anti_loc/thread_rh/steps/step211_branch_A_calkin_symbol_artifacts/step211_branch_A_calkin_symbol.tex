\section*{Step 211: Branch A Calkin Symbol Attempt}

Step 162 defines
\[
  A_\eta=C^*(P_\infty,M_{m_\ell},P_\eta,I)\subset B(H),
  \qquad K_\eta=A_\eta\cap K(H),
\]
and the active quotient object \(q_\eta(C_\ell P_\eta)\).  Step 154 gives
\[
  C_\ell=(I-P_\infty)M_{m_\ell}P_\infty,
\]
with \(P_\infty\) represented by the Step 173 operational kernel
\[
K_\infty^{op}(s,s')=\delta(\tau-\tau')-
{\sin(\lambda(\tau-\tau'))\over \pi(\tau-\tau')}
-\sum_n\Psi_n^\lambda(s)\overline{\Psi_n^\lambda(s')}.
\]

\subsection*{G1}

If \(P_\eta\) is the finite projection onto
\(\operatorname{span}\{\kappa_1,\kappa_2,\kappa_3\}\), then
\[
P_\eta\in K(H),\qquad C_\ell P_\eta\in K(H),
\qquad q_\eta(C_\ell P_\eta)=0.
\]
This is a finite diagnostic collapse, not a full Branch A theorem.

For the full Branch A projection onto the closed span of all zeta evaluator
atoms, compactness is not automatic and the quotient problem remains meaningful.

\subsection*{G2}

A faithful boundary symbol
\[
\sigma_B:A_\eta/K_\eta\to C(X)
\]
was attempted in two natural forms.  A Toeplitz/principal symbol is not
available because \(P_\infty\) is not given as a Hardy/Toeplitz projection but
as a sinc--PSWF operational projection.  The ambient Calkin map
\(B(H)\to B(H)/K(H)\) exists, but it does not provide a computable faithful
boundary symbol for \(A_\eta/K_\eta\).

\subsection*{G3--G5}

The normal form
\[
C_\ell=U_\ell W_\ell+K_\ell
\]
was not derived.  The inherited kernel data do not imply a principal-angle
decomposition with compact remainder.  Consequently lower-faithfulness of
\(U_\ell\) and compactness of \(K_\ell\) remain unavailable.

\subsection*{Verdict}

\[
\boxed{\texttt{V\_branch\_A\_calkin\_symbol\_not\_faithful}.}
\]

The specific missing content is a faithful Calkin boundary-symbol theorem, or
equivalently a Toeplitz-extension/normal-form theorem, for the
\((P_\infty,M_{m_\ell},P_\eta)\) algebra on the full zero-evaluator carrier.
