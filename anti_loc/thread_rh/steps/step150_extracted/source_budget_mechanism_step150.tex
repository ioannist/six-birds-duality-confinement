\section{Purpose}
Step 149 showed that the source audit budget
\[
  \frac{\operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)}{\Lambda_N^\Omega}\to 0
\]
is not a free normalization.  Under a completed lower-frame inequality
\[
  F_N^\Omega \succeq \Lambda_N^\Omega G_R,
  \qquad \Lambda_N^\Omega\to\infty,
\]
this budget is already a residual-collapse certificate.  Step 150 audits whether any lawful independent mechanism can supply it.

\section{No-free source-budget obstruction}
\begin{theorem}[budget-collapse equivalence]
Assume
\[
  F_N^\Omega\succeq \Lambda_N^\Omega G_R,
  \qquad \Lambda_N^\Omega\to\infty,
\]
and \(K_R^\omega\ge0\) is fixed and trace-class. Then
\[
  \frac{\operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)}{\Lambda_N^\Omega}
  \ge
  \operatorname{tr}(G_RK_R^\omega).
\]
Consequently, any mechanism proving
\[
  \operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)=o(\Lambda_N^\Omega)
\]
also proves
\[
  \operatorname{tr}(G_RK_R^\omega)=0.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since \(F_N^\Omega-\Lambda_N^\Omega G_R\succeq0\) and \(K_R^\omega\succeq0\) is trace-class,
\[
  \operatorname{tr}((F_N^\Omega-\Lambda_N^\Omega G_R)K_R^\omega)\ge0.
\]
The displayed inequality follows.  Dividing by \(\Lambda_N^\Omega\) and letting \(N\to\infty\) gives the collapse statement.
\end{proof}

\section{Mechanism audit}
We audit the candidate mechanisms proposed at Step 149.

\subsection{Explicit-formula conservation}
An explicit-formula identity can balance prime, archimedean, pole, and zero terms.  However, if the source frame used for the squeeze is positive and lower-frame coercive, then its trace against a positive fixed ledger cannot be cancelled by signed terms inside the same positive trace.  A signed explicit-formula conservation law can be useful only if it is promoted as a separate signed ledger with all channel records.  It cannot serve as an auxiliary proof of
\[
  \operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)=o(\Lambda_N^\Omega)
\]
while retaining the positive lower-frame interpretation.

\subsection{Plancherel identity}
A Plancherel theorem can promote finite source windows to a completed lower frame, or justify a response-space isometry.  It does not reduce the trace exposure of a positive lower frame against a positive fixed ledger.  It pays exhaustivity; it does not pay the source-budget obstruction.

\subsection{Trace cancellation}
Trace cancellation cannot occur inside
\[
  \operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)
\]
when both factors are positive.  Any cancellation mechanism must leave the positive-source squeeze and become a signed audit theorem.  Such a theorem would need its own non-smuggling, tail, and adequacy records.

\subsection{Moving weights}
A choice
\[
  \omega_z^{(N)}\sim (\Lambda_N^\Omega)^{-1}\omega_z
\]
can force bounded exposure, but only by changing the ledger with \(N\).  This is not a fixed completed residual ledger.  Its status is moving-window support only.

\subsection{Direct residual invisibility}
The only clean positive-source mechanism is direct residual invisibility:
\[
  G_R^{1/2}K_R^\omega G_R^{1/2}=0.
\]
Because the weights are strictly positive on visible zero-evaluator directions, this is equivalent to no visible weighted residual off-critical mass in \(H_R\).  This is not an auxiliary estimate; it is the target theorem.

\section{Pivot theorem}
\begin{theorem}[positive trace-squeeze pivot]
Within the positive lower-frame route, every lawful source-budget mechanism is one of the following:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item collapse-equivalent: it proves \(\operatorname{tr}(G_RK_R^\omega)=0\);
  \item moving-ledger: it changes \(K_R^\omega\) with \(N\) and is not a completed proof;
  \item signed-ledger: it leaves the positive lower-frame trace and requires a separate signed audit theorem;
  \item support-only: it proves only finite-window or Plancherel support without controlling the fixed ledger.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the trace-squeeze route is diagnostic unless a direct residual-exclusion theorem is supplied.
\end{theorem}
