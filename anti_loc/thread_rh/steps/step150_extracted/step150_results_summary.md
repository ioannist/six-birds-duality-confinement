
# Step 150: Source-Budget Mechanism Audit

## Main verdict

\[
\boxed{\text{No independent positive-source budget mechanism was found.}}
\]

Step 149 showed that if

\[
F_N^\Omega\succeq \Lambda_N^\Omega G_R,
\qquad \Lambda_N^\Omega\to\infty,
\]

then any fixed positive trace-class residual ledger satisfies

\[
\frac{\operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)}{\Lambda_N^\Omega}
\ge
\operatorname{tr}(G_RK_R^\omega).
\]

So a source audit budget

\[
\operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)=o(\Lambda_N^\Omega)
\]

is already a residual-collapse certificate.

Step 150 audits the possible escape mechanisms and finds that none supplies a free auxiliary estimate inside the positive lower-frame route.

---

## Mechanism audit

### 1. Explicit-formula conservation

A signed explicit-formula identity may balance prime, archimedean, pole, and zero terms, but it does not cancel a positive trace

\[
\operatorname{tr}(F_N^\Omega K_R^\omega)
\]

when both factors are positive. It can only become a separate signed-ledger theorem with its own gates.

Status:

\[
\boxed{\texttt{not\_a\_positive\_source\_budget}}
\]

### 2. Plancherel identity

Plancherel can promote finite windows to completed lower frames. It pays exhaustivity. It does not make the positive source exposure sublinear.

Status:

\[
\boxed{\texttt{promotion\_support\_only}}
\]

### 3. Trace cancellation

There is no cancellation inside a trace of two positive operators. Any cancellation route must leave the positive lower-frame trace and become a signed audit theorem.

Status:

\[
\boxed{\texttt{requires\_signed\_ledger}}
\]

### 4. Moving weights

A moving schedule

\[
\omega_z^{(N)}\sim (\Lambda_N^\Omega)^{-1}\omega_z
\]

can bound exposure only by changing the ledger with \(N\). That is not a fixed completed residual ledger.

Status:

\[
\boxed{\texttt{moving\_window\_support\_only}}
\]

### 5. Direct residual invisibility

The only clean positive-source mechanism is direct collapse:

\[
G_R^{1/2}K_R^\omega G_R^{1/2}=0.
\]

But this is the theorem itself, not an auxiliary estimate.

Status:

\[
\boxed{\texttt{target\_equivalent}}
\]

---

## Main theorem

Within the positive lower-frame route, every lawful source-budget mechanism is one of:

\[
\boxed{\text{collapse-equivalent}},
\]

\[
\boxed{\text{moving-ledger}},
\]

\[
\boxed{\text{signed-ledger outside the positive source trace}},
\]

or

\[
\boxed{\text{finite-window support only}}.
\]

Therefore the trace-squeeze route is now diagnostic rather than proof-producing unless a direct residual-exclusion theorem is supplied.
