# Step 82: Pole/Tail Balance and the No-Free-Diagonal Gate

## Purpose
Step 82 studies whether the pole/completion and tail/support slots can pay the diagonal repair introduced when the signed prime term is rewritten as a positive shift-difference feature.

## Main conclusion
A finite-rank pole/completion feature cannot pay a full diagonal bill on an infinite-dimensional anti-invariant core:

\[
P\ge0,\ \operatorname{rank}P<\infty,\ \dim H=\infty
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
P\not\succeq \delta I
\quad (\delta>0).
\]

So the pole slot can pay only finite completion/null/endpoint directions. The infinite-dimensional complement must be paid by a tail/full-rank coercive source, a lawful quotient/gating record, or a paired renormalized positive representation.

## Main theorems

### No-free-diagonal theorem
Finite-rank positive operators cannot dominate any positive scalar multiple of the identity on an infinite-dimensional core.

### Pole plus tail diagonal payment
If

\[
q_{\rm pole}[f]\ge \delta_P\|Pf\|^2,
\qquad
q_{\rm tail}[f]\ge \delta_Q\|Qf\|^2,
\]

with \(P+Q=I\), then

\[
q_{\rm pole}[f]+q_{\rm tail}[f]
\ge
\min(\delta_P,\delta_Q)\|f\|^2.
\]

Thus the tail or another full-sector source is load-bearing for full-core diagonal payment.

### Diagonal-growth obstruction
If a candidate completed currency contains

\[
\alpha_L I\preceq K_{\rm cmp,L}
\]

on a fixed nonzero response space, then

\[
\|K_{\rm cmp,L}\|\ge \alpha_L.
\]

Therefore anti-invariant budget collapse is impossible unless \(\alpha_L\to0\), the diagonal is neutralized in a paired representation, or the response space is lawfully narrowed.

### Paired representation rule
For singular kernels such as the gamma/archimedean feature, the paired difference form may be finite while the separated diagonal is infinite. Such a diagonal cannot be treated as spendable currency without a renormalization record.

## RH implication
The prime slot is naturally positive only after a diagonal repair:

\[
q_{{\rm pr},L}=P_{{\rm pr},L}+d_{{\rm pr},L}\|f\|^2.
\]

But leaving \(d_{{\rm pr},L}I\) inside the final currency blocks budget collapse. The completed zeta carrier must therefore supply one of:

1. a direct paired positive representation of the whole completed form;
2. a tail/full-rank lower-frame source covering the infinite complement;
3. a lawful quotient/gating record;
4. an explicit defect budget.

## Bottom line
The pole/completion slot is important but finite-rank. It cannot carry the full infinite-dimensional diagonal repair. The next load-bearing analytic task is to construct a paired completed Weil representation or a full-sector tail/coercive source.
