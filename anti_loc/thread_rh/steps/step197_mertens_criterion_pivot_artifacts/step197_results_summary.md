# Step 197 Results Summary

## Verdict

`V_mertens_CRCFT_TE`.

The Mertens criterion carrier is a classically-RH-equivalent carrier.  The weak Mertens criterion

```text
M(x) = O(x^{1/2 + epsilon}) for every epsilon > 0
```

is equivalent to RH, so closure of the Mertens residual is CRCFT-TE: target-equivalent to RH.

## Carrier Declaration

Let

```text
M(x) = sum_{n <= x} mu(n)
```

where `mu` is the Mobius function.  The carrier is the discrete/asymptotic sequence ledger generated by the Mobius sequence and its partial-sum operator.

Native probes:

- individual Mobius values `mu(n)`;
- interval and dyadic partial sums;
- arithmetic-progression partial sums;
- Dirichlet-series probe `1/zeta(s) = sum mu(n)n^{-s}` in its convergence region and by continuation.

Dissolving probes are the barrier functions `x^{1/2+epsilon}` for `epsilon > 0`.

The residual is

```text
Xi_M(epsilon) = limsup_{x -> infinity} |M(x)| / x^{1/2+epsilon}.
```

Closure means `Xi_M(epsilon)=0` for every `epsilon>0`.

## Strong vs Weak Mertens

The strong Mertens conjecture

```text
|M(x)| < sqrt(x)
```

is strictly stronger than RH and was refuted by Odlyzko--te Riele (1985), who proved that the normalized ratio exceeds the square-root barrier in magnitude.  This refutation does not refute RH.

The weak form with arbitrary `epsilon>0` remains open and is equivalent to RH by Littlewood's criterion.

## CRCFT Classification

The carrier is CRE because closing `Xi_M` for all `epsilon>0` is logically equivalent to RH.  Its primary CRCFT mode is target-equivalence:

```text
CRCFT-TE: closure of Xi_M <=> RH.
```

There is a finite-data/asymptotic interface through computable values of `M(x)`, but that interface is not a CTMT matrix-element terminus: finite Mertens data cannot promote to the required asymptotic envelope without exactly the RH-equivalent estimate.
