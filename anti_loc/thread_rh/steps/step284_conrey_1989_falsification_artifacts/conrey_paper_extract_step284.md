# Conrey 1989 Extract -- Step 284

Primary source: J. B. Conrey, *More than two fifths of the zeros of the Riemann zeta-function are on the critical line*, J. reine angew. Math. 399 (1989), 1-26.

Main theorem:

> at least 2/5 of the zeros of the Riemann zeta-function are simple and on the critical line.

Method provenance:

> Our method is a refinement of the method Levinson

Mollifier input:

> the main new element here is the use of a mollifier

Later refinement from Bui-Conrey-Young 2011:

> at least 41.05% of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are on the critical line.

BCY method note:

> With the use of a new two-piece mollifier, we make a modest improvement

Limitation context from Radziwill:

> We establish limitations to how well one can mollify zeta(s) on the critical line

Audit reading: Conrey supplies a major positive-proportion theorem, not RH.  The audited mollifier lineage improves 40% to about 41%, and no source supplies a route from positive proportion to 100%.
