\section*{Step 220: Joint Scan for \(\Phi_{\max}\)}

\subsection*{Definition}

For Gaussian wavepackets \(u_T\), define
\[
\Phi(\sigma,\ell)=\lim_{T\to\infty}\|C_\ell u_T\|,
\qquad C_\ell=(I-P_\infty)M_{m_\ell}P_\infty.
\]
The scan is performed numerically at \(T=10000\).

\subsection*{Coarse Grid}

The grid uses ten \(\sigma\)-values and ten \(\ell\)-values, giving 100 cells.  The coarse maximum is
\[
(\sigma,\ell)=(0.4,2.0),\qquad \Phi=0.4898488468.
\]

\subsection*{Refined Grid}

A \(5\times5\) grid of step \(0.05\) around the coarse maximum gives
\[
\sigma_{\max}=0.35,\qquad \ell_{\max}=2.0,
\]
with
\[
\Phi_{\max}=0.4904766190.
\]
With conservative numerical error \(7.5\cdot10^{-2}\),
\[
\Phi_{\max}\ge 0.4154766190.
\]
The value is far below the a priori operator bound \(1\).

\subsection*{Robustness}

At \((\sigma,\ell)=(0.35,2.0)\), the values are stable:
\[
\Phi(T=1000)=0.4904766300,\quad
\Phi(T=5000)=0.4904766200,\quad
\Phi(T=10000)=0.4904766190.
\]
Using 48 PSWF terms instead of 24, and dps 100 instead of dps 80, produces the same displayed value.

\subsection*{Verdict}

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{V\_phi\_max\_bounded\_below\_one}}.
\]
The wavepacket-family essential-norm diagnostic peaks near \(0.49\), not near \(1\).
