# BKM 1984 Extract -- Step 282

Primary source: J. T. Beale, T. Kato, and A. Majda, *Remarks on the breakdown of smooth solutions for the 3-D Euler equations*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 94 (1984), 61-66.

Springer/Duke article record:

> maximum norm of the vorticity controls the breakdown of smooth solutions

Continuation direction:

> if the vorticity remains bounded, a smooth solution persists

Standard continuation condition:

> integral_0^T ||omega(t)||_{L^infty} dt < infinity

Standard breakdown condition:

> integral_0^T* ||omega(t)||_{L^infty} dt = infinity

Audit reading: BKM is a criterion.  It identifies the vorticity integral whose finiteness allows continuation, and whose divergence accompanies breakdown.  It does not prove that the integral remains finite for 3D Navier-Stokes for all time.
