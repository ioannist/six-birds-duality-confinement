\section*{Step 282: NS Score-2 Audit of Beale--Kato--Majda}

\paragraph{Target.}
The step tests whether the Beale--Kato--Majda blowup criterion supplies the NS cascade's EXT2--EXT4 time-gate closure.

\paragraph{Published theorem type.}
BKM is a continuation/breakdown criterion.  The public Springer/Duke record summarizes the theorem as saying that the maximum norm of the vorticity controls breakdown of smooth three-dimensional Euler solutions.  In the standard formulation, a smooth solution can be continued provided
\[
  \int_0^T \|\omega(t)\|_{L^\infty}\,dt < \infty,
\]
and breakdown at a critical time forces divergence of the corresponding integral.

\paragraph{Cascade need.}
The NS ten-layer CTMT chain requires a time-gate closure: a proof that the relevant vorticity-control integral remains finite for all target-class smooth 3D Navier--Stokes solutions, or a target-class singularity mechanism showing failure.

\paragraph{Gap.}
BKM identifies the quantity whose control is sufficient for continuation.  It does not supply a global bound for that quantity.  Thus it moves the cascade to the vorticity-integral obligation but does not close the obligation.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
\[
  \boxed{V_{\mathrm{BKM\_NS\_bridge\_blocked}}}
\]
BKM is criterion, not closure.  The score-2 candidate downgrades to score-1, extending the cross-track downgrade pattern to 6-of-6.
