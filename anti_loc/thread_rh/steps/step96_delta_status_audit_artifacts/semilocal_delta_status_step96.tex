\section{Purpose}
After the hybrid reset, the archimedean/gamma slot is imported from the Connes--Consani Sonin/prolate positivity mechanism.  The active semilocal obstruction is
\[
  \Delta_S := q_{\mathrm{Weil},S}-q_{\mathrm{pos},S},
\]
where $S$ is a finite set of places containing $\infty$, $q_{\mathrm{Weil},S}$ is the actual semilocal Weil form on the semilocal response core, and
\[
  q_{\mathrm{pos},S}=q_{\infty}^{\mathrm{CC}}+q_{\mathrm{pr},S}^{\mathrm{pair}}+q_{\mathrm{pole},S}^{+}+q_{\mathrm{tail},S}^{+}

is the candidate positive feature package.

This note audits whether $\Delta_S$ is naturally compact/finite-rank, source-absorbable, tail-defect-only, or a genuine infinite-dimensional obstruction.

\section{Imported facts from the Tier-0 literature}
\begin{enumerate}[label=F\arabic*.]
\item Connes--Consani's archimedean result gives a positive trace functional $\operatorname{Tr}(\vartheta(f)S)$ associated to Sonin compression and identifies the archimedean obstruction as a trace-remainder. After applying the boundary-condition operator $Q$, the remainder is represented by an operator of the form $-2I+K_I$ with $K_I$ compact, indeed Hilbert--Schmidt on bounded support intervals.
\item The semilocal prolate paper gives the semilocal Hardy--Titchmarsh transform.  For finite $S$, the response measure is deformed by the finite Euler-factor product
\[
  dm_S(s) = \left|\prod_{v\in S} L_v(1/2-is)\right|^2 ds,

and the semilocal cyclic pair remains even.  It also gives a Hilbertian isomorphism connecting archimedean and semilocal Sonin spaces.  However, the actual semilocal prolate operator coefficients are deferred.
\item The finite spectral-triple construction gives finite-dimensional restrictions $QW_\lambda^N$ and self-adjoint rank-one perturbations whose spectra lie on the critical line and numerically approximate zeta zeros.  Its missing step is convergence of regularized determinants / fixed-or-exhaustive promotion to $\Xi$.
\item The chiral adelic Dirac construction supplies an enlarged carrier with finite-prime truncations, compact arithmetic perturbations under summability, and spectral-shift jumps.  It is a plausible Tier-2 carrier, not automatically a descent proof for the zeta-only carrier.
\item Conrey--Li show that natural de Branges/RKHS shift-positivity conditions fail for zeta and Dirichlet $L$-functions. Any proposed positive feature package that reduces to those conditions fails the survival audit.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Status types}
\begin{definition}[Status types]
A semilocal cross-term record has one of the following statuses.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \texttt{compact-after-quotient}: after support and boundary/vanishing conditions, the obstruction is compact or Hilbert--Schmidt relative to the relevant form topology.
\item \texttt{finite-rank-window}: the obstruction is finite-rank on a moving finite window only.
\item \texttt{source-absorbable}: the positive part of the obstruction is controlled by an upstream-visible source frame.
\item \texttt{tail-defect}: the obstruction is not killed but is carried by an explicit vanishing tail budget.
\item \texttt{infinite-obstruction}: the obstruction leaves an uncovered infinite-dimensional positive sector.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\section{Audit decomposition}
Write schematically
\[
  \Delta_S = \Delta_{\infty}^{\mathrm{CC}} + \Delta_{\mathrm{fin},S} + \Delta_{\mathrm{pole},S} + \Delta_{\mathrm{tail},S}.
\]
Here $\Delta_{\infty}^{\mathrm{CC}}$ is the residual between the archimedean Weil distribution and the imported Sonin trace feature, $\Delta_{\mathrm{fin},S}$ is the finite-place semilocal measure/injection mismatch, $\Delta_{\mathrm{pole},S}$ records boundary/null/pole corrections, and $\Delta_{\mathrm{tail},S}$ records finite-to-completed promotion.

\begin{proposition}[Archimedean component]
The archimedean residual has status \texttt{compact-after-quotient}: after the support and vanishing gates, the Connes--Consani trace-remainder becomes a compact/Hilbert--Schmidt correction plus finite-codimension conditioning.  Therefore it is not the load-bearing unresolved infinite-dimensional obstruction in the hybrid route.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Semilocal finite-place component]
For finite $S$, the semilocal Hardy--Titchmarsh transform gives a common response space and a bounded finite Euler-factor deformation of the archimedean measure.  This makes $\Delta_{\mathrm{fin},S}$ a well-defined semilocal operator/form, but the uploaded semilocal literature does not prove it is compact or finite-rank on the completed anti-invariant core.  Its present status is \texttt{source-absorbable-candidate} or \texttt{tail-defect}, not \texttt{compact-after-quotient}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Finite spectral-triple windows]
The finite spectral-triple operators provide \texttt{finite-rank-window} evidence.  They are strong numerical and structural evidence for the finite-source mechanism, but they are not exact RH confinement unless their determinant/zero ledgers promote by a fixed-or-exhaustive squeeze.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Step 96 status theorem]
The semilocal $\Delta_S$ record has the following audited status.
\[
  \boxed{\Delta_S\;\text{is not presently certified as finite-rank or compact on the completed core}.}
\]
The archimedean subcomponent is compact-after-quotient by Connes--Consani's result.  The finite-place semilocal cross-term remains an unresolved full-sector object.  It can be accepted only by one of the following additional records:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item a semilocal prolate theorem proving compact-after-quotient status for $\Delta_{\mathrm{fin},S}$;
\item an upstream-visible Hecke/Dirichlet source frame absorbing $\Delta_S^+$ with $\Lambda_n\to\infty$;
\item a fixed-or-exhaustive tail record proving the finite-window defects vanish on the completed ledger;
\item a scoped nonclaim that explicitly excludes the unresolved sector.
\end{enumerate}
Without one of these records, $\Delta_S$ remains a genuine infinite-dimensional obstruction candidate.
\end{theorem}

\section{Implications for the RH program}
The hybrid route should not try to prove that $\Delta_S$ is automatically small merely because the archimedean piece is positive.  The correct next construction target is narrower:
\[
  \boxed{\text{prove source absorption or compact-after-quotient for the finite-place semilocal cross-term}.}
\]

  \succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1},\qquad \Lambda_n\to\infty,

with fixed/exhaustive ledger control.
