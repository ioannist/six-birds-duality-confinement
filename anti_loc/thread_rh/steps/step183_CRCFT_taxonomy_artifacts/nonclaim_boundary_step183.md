# Step 183 Nonclaim Boundary

CRCFT does not prove RH.

CRCFT does not close `Xi_BC`, `Xi_BC_Hecke`, `Xi_dB`, or `Xi_HP`.

CRCFT does not remove any retained no-go.

CRCFT does not claim that all possible future carriers are foreclosed.

The CRCFT-coverage statement is a conjecture, not a theorem.

The corpus integration is flagged but not performed in this step.

CRCFT extends CTMT but does not replace it: CTMT remains the matrix-element
terminus mode inside the broader carrier-foreclosure taxonomy.
