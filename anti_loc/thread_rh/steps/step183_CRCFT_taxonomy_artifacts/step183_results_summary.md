# Step 183: CRCFT Taxonomy Formalization

Verdict:

`final_verdict = V_CRCFT_formalized`.

Foundational typed-condition candidate:

`Classical-RH Carrier Foreclosure Taxonomy (CRCFT)`.

Formal statement:

A typed primary carrier `C` with residual `Xi_C` aimed at RH is
classically-RH-equivalent (CRE) when either it is one of the named classical
RH approaches or closure of `Xi_C` is logically RH-equivalent. Under the
framework discipline, tested CRE carriers terminate in one or more typed
foreclosure modes:

1. `CRCFT-CTMT`: post-reduction carrier-native matrix-element terminality.
2. `CRCFT-TE`: pre-reduction target-equivalence.
3. `CRCFT-BF`: carrier bridge-failure or wrong spectral/comparison type.

Instances recorded:

- Burnol/Sonine Branch A: `CRCFT-CTMT-stuck`, kappa theorem, step 179.
- Burnol/Sonine Branch B: `CRCFT-CTMT-stuck`, same kappa theorem, steps 177-178.
- Burnol/Sonine Branch C: `CRCFT-CTMT-foreclosed-numerical`, nonzero `L_{rho,k}` across 4 data points, steps 175-176.
- Hecke H6: `CRCFT-BF`, ledger-relative non-comparability, step 168.
- de Branges `H(E_RH)`: `CRCFT-TE`, de Branges RH condition / Conrey-Li survival, step 181.
- HP/BK standard `H_xp`: `CRCFT-BF`, continuous/arithmetic spectrum mismatch, step 182.
- HP/BK modified: `CRCFT-TE`, exact cutoff/boundary matching target-equivalent, step 182.

Coverage conjecture:

Every CRE carrier exhibits CRCFT in at least one of the three modes. This is a
conjecture supported by 4 carriers / 7 instances, not a theorem.

Relationship to CTMT:

CTMT is one CRCFT mode. CRCFT strictly extends CTMT by also covering
target-equivalence and bridge-failure.
