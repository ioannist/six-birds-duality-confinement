# Step 123 nonclaim boundary

Step 123 does not prove RH and does not prove the Hecke/Dirichlet lower frame.

It proves a conditional exponent-balancing theorem.  The theorem says when the regularized Müntz/co-Poisson shadow can be accurate while the resulting Dirichlet polynomial remains short enough for available source estimates.

No claim is made that the exponent model is sharp.  The parameters alpha, eta, beta, theta_src are analytic obligations to be earned from the specific carrier and source family.

No finite-window evidence is promoted to a completed RH claim without fixed/exhaustive tail control.

No scalar Heap--Soundararajan lower moment is treated as an operator-valued lower frame.

No de Branges/RKHS shift-positivity shortcut is invoked.
