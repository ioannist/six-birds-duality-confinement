\section*{Step 210: Chebyshev \(\psi\) Prime-Counting Carrier}

Define
\[
  \psi(x)=\sum_{p^k\le x}\log p=\sum_{n\le x}\Lambda(n).
\]
The carrier is a scalar prime-counting function on \(\mathbb R_+\), modeled in
\(L^2_{\rm loc}(\mathbb R_+,dx/x)\) or a weighted asymptotic-envelope space.
Native probes are \(\psi(x)\), \(\psi(x)-x\), and weighted averages.  The
dissolving envelopes are \(x^{1/2+\epsilon}\) and
\(\sqrt{x}\log^2x\).

\subsection*{Residuals}

Two standard residuals are
\[
  \Xi_\psi(\epsilon)=\limsup_{x\to\infty}
  {|\psi(x)-x|\over x^{1/2+\epsilon}}
  \qquad(\epsilon>0)
\]
and
\[
  \Xi_\psi=\limsup_{x\to\infty}
  {|\psi(x)-x|\over \sqrt{x}\log^2x}.
\]
By von Koch (1901),
\[
  \psi(x)-x=O(\sqrt{x}\log^2x)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \mathrm{RH}.
\]

\subsection*{Explicit formula}

The Riemann--von Mangoldt explicit formula gives
\[
  \psi(x)-x=-\sum_\rho {x^\rho\over\rho}
  +\log(2\pi)-{1\over2}\log(1-x^{-2})
\]
away from endpoint conventions.  This decomposition does not produce CTMT:
each term \(x^\rho/\rho\) is explicit after choosing \(\rho\).  The problem is
not an undecided matrix element; it is the global summability and cancellation
needed for the von Koch bound.  That bound is exactly target-equivalent.

\subsection*{Mertens relation}

The Step 197 Mertens carrier and this \(\psi\)-carrier are related by Perron
and Mellin transforms involving \(1/\zeta\) and \(-\zeta'/\zeta\).  Both are
scalar asymptotic CRE carriers and both classify as CRCFT-TE.

\subsection*{Verdict}

\[
  \boxed{\texttt{V\_psi\_CRCFT\_TE}.}
\]

Classical references: Riemann (1859), von Mangoldt (1895), de la Vallee
Poussin (1899), von Koch (1901), and Edwards (1974).
