\section{Purpose}
Steps 32--35 isolated the RH-facing zero-confinement mechanism in two records:
\[
\mathsf A_Z\preceq \K^-,\qquad \K^-\preceq \T^-.
\]
Here \(\mathsf A_Z\) is the zero-side anti-invariant displacement matrix and \(\K^-\) is the carrier-side anti-invariant probe-family currency.  Step 35 showed that \(\K^-=0\) is a very strong condition: it is equivalent, after null-mode legality, to the absence of anti-invariant native readout on the legal quotient.  Otherwise one needs a coercive audit mechanism.

This note classifies honest strict-extension sources that can produce anti-invariant coercivity.  The guiding question is:
\[
C_\Gamma\succeq \lambda (L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma,
\qquad \lambda\to\infty,
\]
without using the target zero-confinement conclusion as input.

\section{Setup}
Let \(E\) be the packaged coordinate space of a formed closure/layer, let \(C_\Gamma\succeq0\) be the packaged audit form, and let
\[
L^-_\Gamma:E\to Y^-
\]
be the declared anti-invariant native probe family.  Assume null-mode legality:
\[
\Ker C_\Gamma\subseteq \Ker L^-_\Gamma.
\]
The anti-invariant currency matrix is
\[
\K^-=(L^-_\Gamma)C_\Gamma^\dagger(L^-_\Gamma)^*.
\]
For a positive definite response budget \(\T^-_0\), we say that the audit is \(\lambda\)-coercive against the anti-invariant family if
\[
C_\Gamma\succeq \lambda (L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma.
\]

\begin{lemma}[coercive currency collapse]
If \(C_\Gamma\succeq \lambda (L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma\) and null-mode legality holds, then
\[
\K^-\preceq \lambda^{-1}\T^-_0.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For every \(y\in Y^-\), write \(g=(L^-_\Gamma)^*y\).  The form inequality gives
\[
|\langle y,L^-_\Gamma x\rangle|^2
\le (y^*\T^-_0 y)\, x^*(L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma x
\le \lambda^{-1}(y^*\T^-_0 y)x^*C_\Gamma x.
\]
Taking the variational supremum over \(x\) gives
\[
y^*\K^- y\le \lambda^{-1}y^*\T^-_0y.
\]
\end{proof}

\section{Source I: completed feature factorization}
The first honest source is a completed positive feature map
\[
W:E\to Z,
\qquad C_\Gamma\succeq \lambda W^*W.
\]
Suppose the anti-invariant probe family factors through the completed feature:
\[
L^-_\Gamma=A W.
\]

\begin{theorem}[feature-factor coercivity]
Assume
\[
A^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}A\preceq \kappa I_Z,
\qquad
C_\Gamma\succeq \lambda W^*W.
\]
Then
\[
\K^-\preceq \frac{\kappa}{\lambda}\T^-_0.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Since \(L^-_\Gamma=AW\),
\[
(L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma
=W^*A^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}AW
\preceq \kappa W^*W.
\]
Thus
\[
C_\Gamma\succeq \lambda W^*W\succeq \frac{\lambda}{\kappa}(L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma,
\]
and the preceding lemma applies.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{RH reading.}
The feature map \(W\) is not a signed raw explicit-formula term.  It must be a completed positive feature map, e.g.
\[
W_{\rm cmp}=\begin{bmatrix} W_{\rm pr}\\ W_\infty\\ W_{\rm pole}\\ W_{\rm tail}\end{bmatrix},
\]
whose quadratic form is visible and positive in the completed carrier.  The theorem is only lawful if the factorization \(L^-_\Gamma=A W_{\rm cmp}\) is supplied upstream of the anti-localization claim.

\section{Source II: defective completed factorization}
Exact factorization may be too strong.  Suppose
\[
L^-_\Gamma=A W+E,
\]
with visible residual \(E\).  If
\[
A^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}A\preceq \kappa I,
\]
then by \((u+v)^*(u+v)\le 2u^*u+2v^*v\),
\[
(L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma
\preceq 2\kappa W^*W+2E^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}E.
\]

\begin{theorem}[defective factorization budget]
If
\[
C_\Gamma\succeq \lambda W^*W+\mu E^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}E,
\]
then
\[
\K^-\preceq \max\left\{\frac{2\kappa}{\lambda},\frac{2}{\mu}\right\}\T^-_0.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The displayed inequalities imply
\[
(L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma
\preceq
\max\left\{\frac{2\kappa}{\lambda},\frac{2}{\mu}\right\} C_\Gamma.
\]
Apply the coercive currency lemma.
\end{proof}

\section{Source III: root-composite character forcing}
Let a finite group \(G\) act equivariantly on \(E\), \(Y^-\), and the audit.  Suppose the response space decomposes into character sectors
\[
Y^- = \bigoplus_{\chi\in \widehat G^-}Y_\chi,
\qquad L^-_\Gamma=\bigoplus_\chi L_\chi.
\]
Let \(C_\chi\) be the restricted carrier-side form on the corresponding sector.  If for every nontrivial anti-invariant character sector
\[
C_\chi\succeq \lambda_\chi L_\chi^*\Theta_\chi^{-1}L_\chi,
\]
then
\[
\K^-_\chi\preceq \lambda_\chi^{-1}\Theta_\chi.
\]
Consequently, if \(\lambda_\chi\to\infty\) for all nontrivial anti-invariant sectors, then \(\K^-\to0\) on the full anti-invariant character family.

\begin{warning}[trace shadow]
Coercing only the invariant or trace character does not coerce the anti-invariant family.  Character-wise budget control is required.  Determinants, norms, and trace shadows are not Loewner-order certificates unless accompanied by a character-sector domination record.
\end{warning}

\section{Source IV: threshold/refinement accumulation}
Suppose a refinement ladder adds anti-invariant audit increments
\[
C_n=C_0+\sum_{j=1}^n D_j,
\qquad D_j\succeq \delta_j (L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma.
\]
Then
\[
C_n\succeq \left(\sum_{j=1}^n\delta_j\right)(L^-_\Gamma)^*(\T^-_0)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma,
\]
and therefore
\[
\K^-_n\preceq \left(\sum_{j=1}^n\delta_j\right)^{-1}\T^-_0.
\]
Thus the anti-invariant budget collapses if
\[
\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\delta_j=\infty.
\]

\begin{warning}[finite-stage overread]
Every finite stage may improve the budget while still failing exact collapse.  If \(\sum\delta_j<\infty\), the budget has a positive lower limit.  Divergent cumulative anti-invariant hardening is the mathematical distinction between asymptotic collapse and finite support-only improvement.
\end{warning}

\section{Source V: parity-fixed readout and lawful nonclaim gating}
A different source is exact fixed-layer restriction:
\[
L^-_\Gamma=0
\]
on the legal quotient.  Then \(\K^-=0\) immediately.  This is not coercive hardening; it is absence of anti-invariant native readout.  It is lawful only if the closure's native probe family is genuinely parity-fixed or fixed-sector by upstream declaration.

If instead anti-invariant probes are removed merely to make the theorem true, the result is an explicit nonclaim/gating move, not an anti-invariant no-needle theorem.

\section{No-go theorem: partial hardening is not collapse}
Let
\[
C_\lambda=\begin{pmatrix}1+\lambda&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad L^-=I_{\mathbb R^2}.
\]
Then
\[
\K^-_\lambda=\begin{pmatrix}(1+\lambda)^{-1}&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The first anti-invariant coordinate collapses, but the full family budget satisfies
\[
\|\K^-_\lambda\|=1
\]
for every \(\lambda\).  Thus hardening selected anti-invariant directions does not collapse the native anti-invariant family.

\section{Strict-extension source record}
An anti-invariant coercivity claim requires a source record
\[
\mathsf{CoercSource}=(W,A,E,\lambda,\mu,G,\{\lambda_\chi\},\{\delta_j\},\mathsf{Visibility},\mathsf{Nonclaims}).
\]
The accepted status requires:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the feature map or character decomposition is declared upstream;
\item all completion pieces used in \(W\) are visible and positive as a carrier-side currency;
\item residuals are recorded as defects, not suppressed;
\item coercive constants are independent of the zero-confinement conclusion;
\item finite-stage bounds are not overread as exact collapse;
\item excluded probes carry explicit nonclaim records.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
Anti-invariant budget collapse cannot be obtained from anti-linear symmetry alone.  It requires one of the following strict-extension sources:
\[
\text{completed feature factorization},\quad
\text{root-composite character forcing},\quad
\text{divergent threshold/refinement hardening},\quad
\text{exact fixed-layer readout},
\]
or a claim-weakening nonclaim gate.  The RH-facing obligation is therefore sharpened: identify which upstream square supplies a positive anti-invariant feature map or character-sector coercivity, and prove that its cumulative strength collapses the anti-invariant currency without using RH as input.
