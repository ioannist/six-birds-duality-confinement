# Step 122 Nonclaim Boundary

Step 122 does not prove RH.

Step 122 does not prove that the regularized Burnol-to-Dirichlet shadow defect tends to zero.

Step 122 does not prove the Heap-Soundararajan scalar lower-bound method lifts to an operator-valued lower frame.

Step 122 does not prove finite-window source frames promote to the completed zeta ledger.

Step 122 does not license naive Dirichlet partial sums on the critical line.

Step 122 only proves that, for the smooth Müntz/co-Poisson regularization, the Burnol-to-Dirichlet readability problem is controlled by three explicit defects:

1. Mellin modeling error,
2. Müntz contour residual,
3. coefficient/support/tail defect.

If their sum tends to zero, the shadow is complete. If their sum stays below one, a positive visibility floor may remain. If it reaches one, the missed shadow sector is a genuine Xi-residual.
