# Step 87 summary — Zeta low-frequency alternatives audit

This step audits the four lawful ways to resolve the low-frequency zero of the paired completed Weil carrier.

The paired prime/gamma feature package is positive, but its CND/shift symbol satisfies
\[
\Psi(0)=0.
\]
Therefore positivity is not coercivity. Anti-invariance alone does not create a spectral gap.

The four alternatives are:

1. **Quotient/gap.** Lawful only as a scoped statement unless the removed low-frequency sector has a vanishing tail/exhaustivity record.
2. **Zero-side cancellation.** If the zero-side readout vanishes fast enough at the legal zero, local capacity is finite. For \(\Psi(\xi)\sim \xi^2\) in one dimension, the threshold is \(s>1/2\).
3. **Source coercivity.** A strict-extension source ladder with full anti-invariant lower-frame coverage and \(\Lambda_n\to\infty\) collapses the low-frequency budget.
4. **Defect/nonclaim.** Honest but not a proof of exact confinement unless the defect vanishes on a fixed/exhaustive zero ledger.

Conclusion: the next viable RH construction path is either to prove zero-side low-frequency cancellation for the zeta readout, or to build a full-sector character/source coercivity ladder. Quotient/gap is scoped unless paired with exhaustive tail control; defect/nonclaim is support-only.
