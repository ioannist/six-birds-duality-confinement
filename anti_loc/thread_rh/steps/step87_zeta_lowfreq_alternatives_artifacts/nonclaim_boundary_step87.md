# Nonclaim boundary — Step 87

This audit does not prove RH and does not prove that the completed zeta carrier resolves its low-frequency soft mode.

It classifies the lawful low-frequency exits for a paired CND/shift-feature carrier:

1. quotient/gap,
2. zero-side cancellation,
3. source coercivity,
4. defect/nonclaim.

Only cancellation or full-sector source coercivity can plausibly support an unscoped RH route. Quotient/gap requires a vanishing tail/exhaustivity record. Defect/nonclaim is honest but does not prove exact confinement unless the defect vanishes on a fixed or exhaustive ledger.
