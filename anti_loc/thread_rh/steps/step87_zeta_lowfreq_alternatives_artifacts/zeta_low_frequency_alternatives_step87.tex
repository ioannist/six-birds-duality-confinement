\section{Purpose}

The paired completed Weil feature package has the correct positivity shape only if it is treated as a paired feature, not as separated diagonal bookkeeping.  However every paired shift/CND feature has a legal low-frequency zero.  This note audits the four lawful ways to resolve that zero for the completed zeta carrier.

\[
\text{quotient/gap},\qquad
\text{zero-side cancellation},\qquad
\text{source coercivity},\qquad
\text{defect/nonclaim}.
\]

\section{The low-frequency obstruction}

Let
\[
q_\Psi[f]=\int_{\mathbb R}\Psi(\xi)|\widehat f(\xi)|^2\,d\xi
\]
be a translation-invariant paired feature form with CND symbol
\[
\Psi(\xi)=c\xi^2+2\int_{\mathbb R}(1-\cos(a\xi))\,\mu(da),
\qquad c\ge0,\quad \mu\ge0.
\]

\begin{lemma}[paired features vanish at zero]
Every such symbol satisfies
\[
\Psi(0)=0.
\]
Consequently positivity of the paired feature is not a spectral gap.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Since \(1-\cos(0)=0\), every term in the representation vanishes at \(\xi=0\).
\end{proof}

\begin{warning}[anti-invariance is not a gap]
For the log-side involution \(Jf(t)=\overline{f(-t)}\), real anti-invariant functions are odd.  Odd functions can have Fourier mass arbitrarily close to \(\xi=0\).  Therefore the \(J=-1\) sector does not by itself eliminate the low-frequency zero.
\end{warning}

\section{Alternative I: quotient or gap}

A quotient/gap record excludes a low-frequency sector
\[
|\xi|<\varepsilon
\]
or quotients a legal null mode.  If the scoped carrier satisfies
\[
\Psi(\xi)\ge \lambda_\varepsilon>0
\qquad (|\xi|\ge \varepsilon),
\]
then capacities are bounded on the scoped carrier.

\begin{proposition}[status of gap control]
Gap control on \( |\xi|\ge \varepsilon\) is a scoped membrane. It promotes to full confinement only if the excluded low-frequency sector has a vanishing tail/exhaustivity record.
\end{proposition}

\section{Alternative II: zero-side cancellation}

Suppose near a legal zero in dimension \(d\),
\[
\Psi(\xi)\gtrsim \rho^{2r}.
\]
If the zero-side readout/probe has
\[
|\widehat g(\xi)|\lesssim \rho^s,
\]
then the local capacity is bounded by
\[
\int_0^\epsilon \rho^{2s-2r+d-1}\,d\rho.
\]

\begin{theorem}[cancellation threshold]
The local capacity is finite iff
\[
s>r-\frac d2.
\]
For a one-dimensional shift zero with \(r=1\), the threshold is
\[
s>\frac12.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The integral converges near zero iff the exponent \(2s-2r+d-1\) is greater than \(-1\), equivalently \(s>r-d/2\).
\end{proof}

For RH this becomes the question whether the zero-side anti-invariant displacement map \(V_Z\) has enough cancellation against the log-side legal zero of the paired carrier.  Separation of the fixed locus by \( \operatorname{Re}s-1/2\) is not the same as low-frequency cancellation on the log-side core.

\section{Alternative III: source coercivity}

A source/coercivity record supplies positive source terms \(D_n\) such that, on the anti-invariant sector,
\[
C_{\Gamma,n}+D_n
\succeq
\Lambda_n (L^-_\Gamma)^*(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}L^-_\Gamma.
\]
Then
\[
K_n^- \preceq \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_0^-.
\]

\begin{theorem}[source coercivity collapses low-frequency budget]
If \(\Lambda_n\to\infty\) and the source records are upstream-visible and cover the full anti-invariant sector, then the carrier-side anti-invariant currency satisfies \(K_n^-\to0\).
\end{theorem}

This is the strongest non-gating route, but it requires a full-sector character/source lower frame.  Partial character hardening leaves hidden anti-invariant directions.

\section{Alternative IV: defect or nonclaim}

A defect/nonclaim record carries the unresolved low-frequency sector as a positive defect \(E_{\mathrm{low},n}\) or explicitly narrows the claim.  If
\[
E_{\mathrm{low},n}\not\to0
\]
on a fixed or exhaustive ledger, exact confinement does not follow.

\begin{proposition}[defect floor no-go]
If
\[
\mathsf A_Z\preceq \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_0^-+E_{\mathrm{low},n}
\]
with \(\Lambda_n\to\infty\) but \(E_{\mathrm{low},n}\to E_\infty\ne0\), then the squeeze proves at most
\[
\mathsf A_Z\preceq E_\infty.
\]
It does not prove \(\mathsf A_Z=0\).
\end{proposition}

\section{Conclusion}

The paired prime/gamma carrier is a plausible positive carrier, but it is not yet an RH membrane because it has a low-frequency legal zero. The zeta route must now provide either:
\[
\text{low-frequency cancellation of }V_Z,
\]
or
\[
\text{full-sector source coercivity},
\]
or a quotient/tail record strong enough to recover exact fixed/exhaustive confinement.
