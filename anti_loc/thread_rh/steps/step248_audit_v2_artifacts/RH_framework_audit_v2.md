## 1. Executive Summary of the Post-200 Arc

The post-200 arc changed the status of the RH framework in three ways. First, the Burnol kappa dependency that had blocked multiple pre-201 verdicts was resolved by manager-led paper audit of Burnol 2002/2004. Second, the Branch A/B/C routes were re-tested with kappa and operational wavepacket tools; none produced RH closure, but each now has a sharper typed status. Third, framework-level conditions were validated across all five Clay-class tracks under `anti_loc/`.

The strongest RH-track numerical result is now Branch A wavepacket non-compactness evidence for the broader operator `C_l P_infty`: the weak-null Gaussian family gives an essential-norm lower witness with refined maximum `Phi_max approx 0.4905`. This does not decide the specific `C_l P_eta` compactness question, but it is a genuine operator-theoretic finding outside the kappa-aligned near-parallel artifacts.

Branch B did not close: the finite matrix source chain reached an eight-layer CTMT recursion and ended at a precision wall driven by transport-sampling ambiguity and `G^{-1}` ill-conditioning. Branch C remains foreclosed as a full-carrier route: step 196 supplied k=0 nonzero data; step 247 replaced noisy k=1 finite differences with proper analytic derivative values still sharply nonzero.

The Dichotomy survey now covers 19 carriers. No Riemann-RH Dichotomy refuter was found. Several outside-scope carriers were clarified: Bagchi universality is unconditional value-distribution, Ramanujan tau and Dirichlet/automorphic L-functions belong to a Selberg-class family generalization, and Lindelof is a weaker sub-conjecture, not an RH-equivalent carrier.

Framework findings now include six corpus-pending typed conditions or meta-patterns. Three of them, CTMT recursion, Bridge Impossibility, and CRCFT modes, have 5-track validation. Independent Codification adds a different kind of evidence: Bridge Impossibility was natively codified in RH, Hodge, and P-vs-NP before cross-track validation.
