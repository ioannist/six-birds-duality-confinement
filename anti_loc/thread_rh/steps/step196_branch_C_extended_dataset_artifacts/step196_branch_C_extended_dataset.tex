\section*{Target}

This step extends the Branch C foreclosure dataset from Steps 175--176.  The tested matrix element is
\[
  L_{\rho,k}(G)=\langle M_\zeta G,\mathsf P_\infty y_{\rho,k}\rangle.
\]
For \(k=0\), using the Step 173 operational identity and the Step 174 symbolic formula, the value is evaluated as
\[
  L_{\rho,0}(G)
   =
   \zeta(\rho)G(\rho)-I_{\mathrm{sinc}}(\rho;G)
   -\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\Psi_n^\lambda(\rho)J_n(G).
\]
At zeta zeros the first term is numerically negligible, giving the Step 175/176 convention
\[
  L_{\rho,0}(G)
   =
   -I_{\mathrm{sinc}}(\rho;G)
   -\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\Psi_n^\lambda(\rho)J_n(G).
\]
The \(k=1\) values in this step are diagnostic central finite differences of the already projected value:
\[
  L_{\rho,1}(G)
   \approx
   -i\frac{P_{\infty}M_\zeta G(1/2+i(\gamma+h/2))
           -P_{\infty}M_\zeta G(1/2+i(\gamma-h/2))}{h},
   \qquad h=0.02.
\]

\section*{Numerical Setup}

The computation preserves the Step 175/176 normalization:
\[
  \lambda=1,\qquad U=200,\qquad \Delta u=0.05.
\]
The zeta values are computed with \texttt{mpmath} at 50 decimal digits.  Mellin transforms of the compact bump generators are evaluated by Gauss--Legendre quadrature.  The prolate terms are approximated by diagonalizing the sinc kernel on \([-1,1]\), with primary size \(320\) and an alternate size \(240\) used in the error estimate.

\section*{Legal Burnol Generators}

Each generator has the form
\[
  g(t)=b_1(t)-\alpha b_2(t)+\beta_3 b_3(t),\qquad
  b_j(t)=\exp\!\left(-\frac{1}{1-((t-c_j)/\epsilon)^2}\right)
\]
on its bump support and \(0\) outside.  The coefficients are chosen by the two moment equations \(G(0)=0\) and \(G(1)=0\), so each generator is legal in the Step 174 sense.

\section*{Error and Pattern Summary}

All cases satisfy \(|L|-\mathrm{error}>0\).  The certified minimum lower bound is
\[
  \min(|L|-\mathrm{error})=3.4126458948014055\times 10^{-2}.
\]
For \(k=0\), the observed range is
\[
  3.467955\times 10^{-2}\le |L|\le 2.170389\times 10^{-1}.
\]
For \(k=1\), the observed range is
\[
  2.885740\times 10^{-1}\le |L|\le 4.017945\times 10^{-1}.
\]
The \(k=1\) results are numerically noisier because they are finite-difference diagnostics, but their lower bounds remain positive.

No clean monotone law in \(\gamma\) is visible at this sample size.  The \(k=0\) correlation between \(\gamma\) and \(|L|\) is approximately \(-1.98\times 10^{-1}\), which is too weak to support a scaling law.

\section*{Verdict}

\[
\boxed{\texttt{V\_branch\_C\_dataset\_generic\_foreclosure}}
\]

The extended dataset reinforces the Step 176 Branch C full-carrier foreclosure.  Under the inherited Step 175/176 numerical conventions, the tested matrix elements are robustly nonzero across multiple zeta zeros, multiple legal Burnol generators, and the first derivative-evaluator diagnostics.  This does not close \(\Xi_{BC}\), prove RH, or settle all possible legal generators; it strengthens the empirical obstruction to the full-carrier Branch C ZI-COV(i) route.
