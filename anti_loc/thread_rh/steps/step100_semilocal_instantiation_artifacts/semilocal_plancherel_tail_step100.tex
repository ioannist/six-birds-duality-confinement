\section{Purpose}
Step 99 stated the abstract promotion gate:
\[
\text{finite character/source frame}
+\text{Plancherel/exhaustivity}
+\text{vanishing tail}
\Longrightarrow
\text{completed anti-invariant lower frame}.
\]
Step 100 instantiates this gate on a concrete carrier-native choice: the semilocal Connes--Consani--Moscovici response space
\[
Y_S^- = L^2(\R,dm_S)^-,
\]
where \(S\) is a finite set of rational places containing \(\infty\), the superscript \(-\) denotes the anti-invariant sector for \(\xi\mapsto-\xi\), and
\[
 dm_S(\xi)=\left|L_\infty\!\left(\frac12-i\xi\right)
 \prod_{p\in S_f} L_p\!\left(\frac12-i\xi\right)\right|^2d\xi.
\]
This is the smallest honest semilocal arena for the hybrid route: the archimedean/Sonin positivity record, finite Euler factors, anti-invariant symmetry, and spectral windows live in one Hilbert geometry.

\section{Semilocal response carrier}
Let
\[
S=\{\infty\}\cup S_f,
\qquad
L_p(s)=(1-p^{-s})^{-1},
\qquad
L_\infty(s)=\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2).
\]
Define
\[
E_S(\xi)=L_\infty\!\left(\frac12-i\xi\right)
\prod_{p\in S_f}L_p\!\left(\frac12-i\xi\right).
\]
Then
\[
Y_S=L^2(\R,|E_S(\xi)|^2d\xi),
\qquad
Y_S^-:=\{y\in Y_S:y(-\xi)=-y(\xi)\}.
\]
The semilocal Hardy--Titchmarsh transform puts finite Euler factors into the measure of this response space. Thus primes are no longer an external signed sum; they deform the response geometry.

\section{Windows and tail forms}
There are two independent truncations.

\subsection{Spectral windows}
For \(T>0\), let
\[
P_T:Y_S^-\to Y_S^- ,\qquad (P_Ty)(\xi)=1_{|\xi|\le T}y(\xi).
\]
The tail projection is
\[
P_T^\perp=I-P_T.
\]
A tail form against a budget operator \(B\succ0\) is
\[
\mathcal T_T[y]=\ip{P_T^\perp y}{B P_T^\perp y}_{Y_S}.
\]
For exact confinement, the relevant condition is not norm convergence alone but zero-ledger exhaustivity:
\[
\operatorname{tr}(P_T^\perp\mathsf A_ZP_T^\perp)\to0.
\]

\subsection{Finite-place windows}
Let \(S_n=\{\infty\}\cup\{p\le X_n\}\). The finite-place response space is
\[
Y_{S_n}^-=L^2(\R,dm_{S_n})^-.
\]
The inclusion from one semilocal geometry to another is not automatically isometric. A lawful bridge must record either a unitary identification, an equivalent-norm estimate, or a defect form.

\subsection{Character/conductor windows}
Let \(\mathcal X_n\) be a finite conductor/Hecke-character window. It defines readouts
\[
Q_\omega:Y_S^-\to Z_\omega,
\qquad \omega\in\mathcal X_n,
\]
and source frame
\[
F_n=\sum_{\omega\in\mathcal X_n}\lambda_{\omega,n}Q_\omega^*\Theta_\omega^{-1}Q_\omega.
\]
Finite character orthogonality proves an exact frame on the finite quotient it sees; it does not prove the completed lower frame unless the quotient windows exhaust \(Y_S^-\).

\section{Promotion theorem on the semilocal carrier}
\begin{theorem}[Semilocal Plancherel/tail promotion]
Let \(Y_S^-\) be as above. Let \(\Pi_n:Y_S^-\to Y_n^-\) be a window map, and suppose the finite/window source frame satisfies
\[
F_n^{\rm win}\succeq \Lambda_n B_n
\quad\text{on }Y_n^-.
\]
Assume a completed Plancherel/exhaustivity record
\[
(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}\preceq \Pi_n^*B_n\Pi_n+T_n
\quad\text{on }Y_S^-.
\]
Then the lifted source frame
\[
\widetilde F_n:=\Pi_n^*F_n^{\rm win}\Pi_n
\]
satisfies
\[
\widetilde F_n+\Lambda_nT_n\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\widetilde F_n\succeq\Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}-\Lambda_nT_n.
\]
Thus finite source frames promote to completed lower frames only up to the declared tail form.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The finite lower-frame hypothesis gives
\[
\Pi_n^*F_n^{\rm win}\Pi_n\succeq \Lambda_n\Pi_n^*B_n\Pi_n.
\]
The exhaustivity hypothesis gives
\[
\Pi_n^*B_n\Pi_n\succeq (\Theta_0^-)^{-1}-T_n.
\]
Combining,
\[
\widetilde F_n\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}-\Lambda_nT_n,
\]
which is equivalent to the claimed tail-paid lower-frame inequality.
\end{proof}

\section{Carrier-native RH source gate}
In the hybrid semilocal route, the completed source gate is
\[
F_n\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1},
\qquad \Lambda_n\to\infty,
\]
plus absorption of the semilocal cross-term
\[
\Delta_S^+\preceq F_n+E_{{\rm abs},n}.
\]
If these hold with vanishing defects in a fixed/exhaustive zero-ledger sense, then
\[
K_n^-=L^-C_n^\dagger(L^-)^*\preceq \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_0^-+E_{{\rm src},n},
\]
and the anti-invariant budget collapses.

\section{What is now concrete}
The semilocal instantiation supplies the following carrier-native records:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \(Y_S^-=L^2(\R,dm_S)^-\) as response space.
\item \(P_T\) as the spectral window map.
\item finite-place windows \(S_n\) through the local-factor measure \(dm_{S_n}\).
\item character/conductor readouts \(Q_\omega\) as source probes.
\item tail forms \(T_n\) as explicit promotion defects.
\item the lower-frame target \(F_n\succeq\Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}\).
\end{enumerate}

\section{What remains unearned}
This step does not prove RH. It leaves two hard analytic obligations:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item Prove \(\Delta_S^+\preceq F_n+E_{{\rm abs},n}\) on the semilocal response core.
\item Prove the Plancherel/exhaustivity tail condition for the completed zero ledger.
\end{enumerate}
The high-leverage next attempt is the semilocal prolate compactness question: if \(\Delta_S\) is compact after quotient/tail records, then source absorption becomes a compact-perturbation problem rather than a full infinite-dimensional lower-frame problem.

\section{Nonclaim boundary}
Finite character orthogonality is accepted only on its finite quotient. Finite semilocal windows are accepted only as windows. A moving finite-window ladder is not a proof of exact confinement unless it is tied to a fixed/exhaustive zero ledger with vanishing tail. The Conrey--Li survival test remains active: this route must not collapse into a de Branges/RKHS shift-positivity condition.
