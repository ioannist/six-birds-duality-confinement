# Step 39 Nonclaim Boundary

Step 39 does not prove RH and does not assert that the actual completed zeta/Weil carrier satisfies the root-composite gates.

It proves only a contrapositive obstruction theorem for the proposed route:

- If explicit-formula domination and source-budget collapse hold, visible off-fixed mass is bounded.
- If the bound fails under a claimed record, at least one named gate is defective.

The theorem forbids replacing anti-invariant Loewner domination with trace balance, determinant/norm balance, invariant-sector control, or an ambient/public-shadow calculation.
