# Nonclaim boundary for standalone Xi theory

The standalone Xi theory does not claim that every formed layer has zero blind spot. It identifies the sharp residual that must be controlled to promote a native membrane to a layer-dissolving membrane.

It does not claim Navier regularity, RH, cryptographic security, immune coverage, or logical completeness. Each application requires a concrete formed carrier, declared native probes, declared dissolving probes, legal null quotient, predictive transports, and audited residual budgets.

The theory also does not license substrate-level literalization: Xi is a carrier-level promoted witness. A public shadow of Xi is support evidence only unless a transfer bridge is audited.
