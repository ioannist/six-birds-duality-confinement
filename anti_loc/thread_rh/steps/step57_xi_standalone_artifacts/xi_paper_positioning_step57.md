# Positioning: Why a standalone Xi paper exists

The main membrane paper now contains \(\Xi_C(D\mid L)\), but the object is strong enough to stand alone.

Suggested title:

**Adequacy Residuals and Blind-Spot Currency in Formed-Layer Membrane Theory**

Core thesis:

\[
\Xi_C(D\mid L) = \text{the shadow-price residual of layer-dissolving probes after conditioning on native probes.}
\]

It is the Schur complement of the dissolving currency after native currency is accounted for.  It is also the optimal residual in reconstructing dissolving probes from native probes.

Why it matters:

- It formalizes adequacy/exhaustivity.
- It separates native anti-localization from layer-dissolving anti-localization.
- It gives a monotone strict-extension law.
- It converts blind spots into a priced currency matrix.
- It has applications independent of RH.

Nonclaim:

A native membrane does not imply a full layer-dissolving membrane unless \(\Xi\) is zero or budgeted.
