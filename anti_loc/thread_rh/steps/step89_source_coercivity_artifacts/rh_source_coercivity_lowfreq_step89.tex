\section{Purpose}
Step 88 showed that the zero-side cancellation route requires a uniform primitive certificate. Mere oddness or fixed-locus separation does not remove the low-frequency legal zero of a paired shift/CND Weil carrier. This note audits the second serious route: source coercivity.

The question is:
\[
\text{Can upstream-visible source records charge the low-frequency anti-invariant sector strongly enough?}
\]

This is not yet an RH proof. It is the low-frequency resolution module needed before the paired Weil feature package can function as an accepted carrier-side currency.

\section{Setup}
Let $Y^-$ be the anti-invariant response space, and let it be decomposed as
\[
Y^- = Y^-_{\rm low}\oplus Y^-_{\rm hi},
\qquad
P_{\rm low}+P_{\rm hi}=I.
\]
The paired Weil carrier $C_{\rm pair}$ is positive but may vanish at low frequency. The high sector may have a local gap after quotienting a low-frequency neighborhood:
\[
C_{\rm pair}\succeq \gamma_{\rm hi} P_{\rm hi}^*\Theta_{\rm hi}^{-1}P_{\rm hi},
\qquad \gamma_{\rm hi}>0.
\]
The low sector requires additional source records.

\begin{definition}[Source record]
A source record is a tuple
\[
s=(Q_s,\Theta_s,\lambda_s,D_s)
\]
where $Q_s:Y^-\to Z_s$ is an upstream-visible character/probe readout, $\Theta_s\succ0$ is its response budget, $\lambda_s\ge0$ is its strength, and
\[
D_s\succeq \lambda_s (Q_s L^-_\Gamma)^*\Theta_s^{-1}(Q_s L^-_\Gamma).
\]
The cumulative response-side source frame is
\[
F_{\mathcal S}=\sum_{s\in\mathcal S}\lambda_s Q_s^*\Theta_s^{-1}Q_s.
\]
\end{definition}

\section{Low-sector source frame theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Low-frequency source coercivity]
Suppose the source records satisfy the low-sector lower-frame condition
\[
P_{\rm low}^*F_{\mathcal S,n}P_{\rm low}
\succeq
\Lambda_n P_{\rm low}^*\Theta_{\rm low}^{-1}P_{\rm low}.
\]
Let
\[
C_n=C_{\rm pair}+\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_n}D_s.
\]
If the high-sector gap also holds,
\[
C_{\rm pair}\succeq \gamma_{\rm hi} P_{\rm hi}^*\Theta_{\rm hi}^{-1}P_{\rm hi},
\]
and the low/high split is lawful on the legal quotient, then the carrier-side anti-invariant currency satisfies the block bound
\[
\K_n^-
\preceq
\Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_{\rm low}
\oplus
\gamma_{\rm hi}^{-1}\Theta_{\rm hi},
\]
up to declared cross-sector bridge defects.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On the low sector, the source-frame hypothesis gives
\[
\sum_s D_s\succeq \Lambda_n (L^-_{\rm low})^*\Theta_{\rm low}^{-1}L^-_{\rm low}.
\]
On the high sector, the paired carrier gap gives
\[
C_{\rm pair}\succeq \gamma_{\rm hi}(L^-_{\rm hi})^*\Theta_{\rm hi}^{-1}L^-_{\rm hi}
\]
after response normalization. Combining the two inequalities and applying the standard currency bound
\[
C\succeq \lambda L^*\Theta^{-1}L
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
LC^\dagger L^*\preceq \lambda^{-1}\Theta
\]
on each legal block proves the claim. Cross-sector terms are either absent by an accepted reducing split or become explicit bridge defects.\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Low-sector collapse]
If $\Lambda_n\to\infty$, then the low-sector budget collapses:
\[
P_{\rm low}\K_n^-P_{\rm low}^*\preceq \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_{\rm low}\to0.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{warning}[Low-sector collapse is not full RH collapse]
The preceding corollary resolves only the low-frequency obstruction. Exact zero confinement still requires either full-sector budget collapse,
\[
\K_n^-\preceq B_n,
\qquad \tr B_n\to0,
\]
or a fixed/exhaustive zero-ledger squeeze showing that the high-sector budget is irrelevant to the anti-invariant zero ledger. A bounded high-sector budget is not the same as exact confinement.
\end{warning}

\section{Incomplete source warning}

\begin{proposition}[Hidden low-frequency direction]
If there exists $0\ne y\in Y^-_{\rm low}$ such that
\[
Q_s y=0
\qquad\text{for all }s\in\mathcal S_n,
\]
then the lower-frame constant on the low sector is zero:
\[
\Lambda_n=0.
\]
No source-strength growth on the visible directions charges this hidden low-frequency direction.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For such $y$,
\[
y^*F_{\mathcal S,n}y=0.
\]
Thus no positive lower bound of the form $F_{\mathcal S,n}\succeq\Lambda_n\Theta_{\rm low}^{-1}$ can hold on all of $Y^-_{\rm low}$ with $\Lambda_n>0$.\end{proof}

\section{Upstream visibility and no target-selected sources}
A source family is lawful only if the records $Q_s$, $\Theta_s$, $\lambda_s$, and $D_s$ are declared by upstream zeta data before the zero-confinement test. Sources selected because they make
\[
\A_Z\preceq \K_n^-
\]
pass are post-hoc and have status \texttt{smuggled} or \texttt{support-only}.

Candidate upstream-visible source families for zeta include:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item prime-threshold source records generated by Euler/explicit-formula staging;
\item root-composite/character source records generated by a declared symmetry or automorphic family;
\item trace-formula spectral source records, if a trace carrier is chosen;
\item de Branges or adelic source records, if their carrier supplies the readout before the confinement test.
\end{enumerate}
None of these is accepted merely by being named. Each must satisfy the lower-frame inequality on the anti-invariant low sector.

\section{Exact-confinement relation}
Together with the fixed/exhaustive ledger theorem, a source route can yield exact confinement only if the final obstruction budgets satisfy
\[
\A_Z\preceq B_n,
\qquad
\tr B_n\to0.
\]
If the source route resolves only a low-frequency sector, then the remaining sectors must either be squeezed separately, shown absent from the zero ledger, or carried as a vanishing defect/tail. Otherwise the result is not RH but a scoped membrane statement.

\qquad \Lambda_n\to\infty
\]
on the relevant anti-invariant sector, with upstream visibility and no post-hoc selection.
