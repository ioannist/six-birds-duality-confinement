\section*{Step 325: Stirling derivation attempt for the height component}

We test whether the height component in the Step 324 diagnostic fit
\[
  \gamma(T,d)\approx A T^{-1}+B d^\beta,\qquad A_{\rm emp}=4.118,
\]
can be derived directly from the gamma factor in
\[
  \xi(s)=\frac12 s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s).
\]
The Branch C carrier uses
\[
  h(s)=\zeta(s)M(G_\star)(s),
\]
so rewriting through \(\xi\) gives
\[
  h(s)=\frac{2\pi^{s/2}}{s(s-1)\Gamma(s/2)}\xi(s)M(G_\star)(s).
\]

For \(s=1/2+iT\), Stirling gives
\[
\log\Gamma(s/2)
  =
  (s/2-1/2)\log(s/2)-s/2+\frac12\log(2\pi)+\frac{1}{6s}
  +O(s^{-3}).
\]
Also
\[
  \frac{d^j}{ds^j}\log\Gamma(s/2)
  =
  2^{-j}\psi^{(j-1)}(s/2),\qquad j\ge 1.
\]
For fixed \(j\) and \(T\to\infty\),
\[
  2^{-j}\psi^{(j-1)}(s/2)
  =
  O(T^{1-j})
\]
with the first phase correction from
\[
  \arg(s/2)=\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{1}{2T}+O(T^{-3}).
\]

\subsection*{Conclusion of the derivation attempt}

The fixed-order Stirling expansion of the gamma factor produces only ordinary
\(T^{-1}\), \(T^{-2}\), \(\ldots\) phase/amplitude corrections in the smooth
prefactor. It does not produce a \(k\log k\) coefficient in
\(\log|h^{(k)}(\rho)/k!|\). In this calculation the gamma-factor contribution
to the fitted \(\gamma\)-coefficient is therefore
\[
  A_{\Gamma,k\log k}=0.
\]
Two natural lower-order scales are visible:
\[
  A_{\arg(s/2)}=\frac12,\qquad A_{\rm Bernoulli}=\frac16,
\]
but both are an order of magnitude smaller than
\[
  A_{\rm emp}=4.118.
\]

Thus the Step 324 height component is not explained by the local Stirling
expansion of \(\Gamma(s/2)\) alone. The load-bearing source must involve the
non-local Taylor coefficient geometry of \(\xi\), the Mellin factor
\(M(G_\star)\), or the zero-spacing/interference mechanism rather than only the
gamma factor.
