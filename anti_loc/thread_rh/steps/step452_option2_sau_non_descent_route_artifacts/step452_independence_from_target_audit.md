# Step 452 Independence From Target Audit

## The load-bearing question

Can the non-descent witness be established independently of Theorem T, and can it still supply `Xi_SDTC_domination_records`?

## Witness C result

`W_C` is independent at the weak level:

```text
E^!_M^zero is suppressed from I_tr current trace visibility.
```

This follows from Step 448's visibility partition and Foundations-style typed non-collapse discipline. It does not assume RH.

But `W_C` does not supply the target. To become target-solving it must be paired with one of:

1. `A_Z(zeta)=0` directly;
2. accepted `Gamma_SDTC` source;
3. domination records `B_n` with trace tending to zero;
4. a theorem that non-descent of the zero ledger through `I_tr` forces anti-invariant ledger collapse.

Items 1 and 2 are target/recognition content. Item 3 is exactly `Xi_SDTC_domination_records`. Item 4 is not present in NDO or Foundations II, and if asserted as an axiom it is target-equivalent by Theorem T.

## Other witness forms

- Form A becomes a statement about analytic continuation or exact computation; it does not imply confinement.
- Form B has no finite cardinal-minimality analog for the zeta ledger.
- Form D is conjecture-conditional.
- Form E is compatible with off-critical symmetric packets.
- Form F audits candidate records but does not create them.

## Independence verdict

No witness both (a) remains independent of Theorem T and (b) supplies the SDTC domination-record target. The strongest independent witness is structurally real but non-solving; every solving strengthening is circular.
