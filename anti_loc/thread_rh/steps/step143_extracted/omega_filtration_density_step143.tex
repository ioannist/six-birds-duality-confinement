\section{Purpose}

Steps 130--142 reduced the source route to a diagonal density, blind-sector, and shortness problem.
The unrestricted BPRZ shifted second-moment main kernel is not a full lower frame on the full
coefficient space.  The source route survives only on a residual coefficient class that avoids the
GCD-log blind sector.  The proposed repair is to build an $\Omega$-compatible Burnol/Müntz
dictionary whose regularized Dirichlet shadows obey upstream Heap--Soundararajan block cutoffs.

This note audits whether the $\Omega$-compatible dictionary can be dense in the residual
Burnol/Sonine windows.

\section{Objects}

Let
\[
M_{R,N}:Y_{R,N}\to H_N
\]
synthesize a finite residual Burnol/Sonine window, with
\[
G_{R,N}=M_{R,N}^{*}M_{R,N}.
\]
Let $\mathcal D_{\Omega,K,N}$ be the declared $\Omega$-compatible Burnol/Müntz dictionary at
cutoff $K$ and residual window $N$, and let $P_{\Omega,K,N}$ be the projection onto its response
span.

The active density defect is
\[
\epsilon_{B\to\Omega,N}(K)
=
\left\|(I-P_{\Omega,K,N})M_{R,N}G_{R,N}^{-1/2}\right\|.
\]
The corresponding adequacy residual is
\[
\Xi_{B\to\Omega,N}(K)
=
M_{R,N}^{*}(I-P_{\Omega,K,N})M_{R,N}.
\]

\section{Relative density theorem}

Let $P_{D,N}$ be the projection onto the declared regularized Dirichlet-readable shadow closure.
Assume
\[
P_{\Omega,K,N}\uparrow P_{D,N}
\]
strongly as $K\to\infty$. Then for fixed $N$,
\[
\lim_{K\to\infty}
\left\|(P_{D,N}-P_{\Omega,K,N})M_{R,N}G_{R,N}^{-1/2}\right\|=0.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\limsup_{K\to\infty}\epsilon_{B\to\Omega,N}(K)
\le
\epsilon_{B\to D,N},
\]
where
\[
\epsilon_{B\to D,N}
=
\left\|(I-P_{D,N})M_{R,N}G_{R,N}^{-1/2}\right\|.
\]

Thus the $\Omega$-filtration does not by itself solve Burnol-to-Dirichlet readability.
It only says: once a Burnol residual window is Dirichlet-readable, finite $\Omega$ cutoffs can
exhaust that readable part.

\section{Diagonal ladder theorem}

For growing windows, fixed-$N$ convergence is insufficient.  A lawful ladder requires an upstream
sequence
\[
K_N\to\infty
\]
such that
\[
\eta_N^{\Omega}
:=
\left\|(P_{D,N}-P_{\Omega,K_N,N})M_{R,N}G_{R,N}^{-1/2}\right\|\to0.
\]
Then
\[
\epsilon_{B\to\Omega,N}(K_N)
\le
\epsilon_{B\to D,N}+\eta_N^\Omega.
\]
If $\epsilon_{B\to D,N}\to0$, the $\Omega$-compatible dictionary is dense.
If $\epsilon_{B\to D,N}\le\epsilon_0<1$ and $\eta_N^\Omega\to0$, the dictionary has a positive
visibility floor.

\section{Shortness and blind-sector constraints}

The ladder is not accepted unless it also satisfies the source shortness bound
\[
L_{\mathrm{eff}}(N,K_N)\le q_N^{\theta_{\mathrm{src}}-o(1)}
\]
and the GCD-log blind-sector floor
\[
\delta_{R_N,K_N,N}<1.
\]
Combining the density and blind-sector gates gives the effective source lower-frame floor
\[
\Lambda_N^\Omega
\gtrsim
\gamma_q
\left(1-\epsilon_{B\to\Omega,N}(K_N)^2\right)
\left(1-\delta_{R_N,K_N,N}^{2}\right).
\]
If the two factors have a positive lower bound and $\gamma_q\to\infty$, then the finite residual
source strength diverges, subject to fixed/exhaustive tail promotion.

\section{Obstruction theorem}

Let
\[
P_{\Omega,\infty}
=
\operatorname*{s-lim}_{K\to\infty}P_{\Omega,K}.
\]
If
\[
(I-P_{\Omega,\infty})M_R\ne0,
\]
then
\[
\Xi_{B\to\Omega}
=
M_R^{*}(I-P_{\Omega,\infty})M_R
\]
is a genuine adequacy residual.  It cannot be removed by increasing the $\Omega$ cutoff.
It must be source-absorbed by a different source family, compact/tail-paid, or declared as a scoped
nonclaim.

\section{Interpretation}

The $\Omega$-filtration density theorem gives a useful but limited positive result:
finite $\Omega$ cutoffs exhaust the Dirichlet-readable part of the residual class.
The remaining questions are exactly the framework-native gates:
\[
\epsilon_{B\to D,N}\quad
\text{(regularized shadow adequacy)},
\]
\[
K_N\quad
\text{(diagonal cutoff inside shortness)},
\]
\[
\delta_{R,K,N}\quad
\text{(GCD-log blind-sector floor)},
\]
and completed residual-tail promotion.

\section{Status}

This is not an RH proof. It is the Step-143 reduction:
\[
\Omega\text{-density}
=
\text{Dirichlet-readable shadow density}
+
\text{diagonal cutoff/shortness}
+
\text{blind-sector floor}.
\]
