# Nonclaim boundary — Step 135

Step 135 does not prove RH.

Step 135 does not prove that the actual Burnol/Müntz residual seed has a vanishing Ω-tail.

Step 135 does not prove that BPRZ gives an unrestricted matrix lower frame. Step 130 blocked that unrestricted import.

Step 135 proves a conditional response-space theorem: if the incidence-conditioned Ω-tail is small, then the GCD-log blind-sector residual is small.

The accepted claim is only:

\[
\Xi_{{\rm GCD},N}\preceq (\tau_{\Omega,N}^{Z})^2G_{B,N}.
\]

The open analytic record is:

\[
\tau_{\Omega,N}^{Z}
=\|Z_y(I-P_{\Omega\le K})B_NG_{B,N}^{-1/2}\|
\to0
\]

or at least \(\tau_{\Omega,N}^{Z}\le \tau<1\).

If this fails, \(\Xi_{\rm GCD}\) remains a genuine residual requiring a separate source-frame record, a compact/tail record, or a scoped nonclaim.
