# Step 260 Nonclaim Boundary

This step does not prove the density hypothesis, Lindelof, GRH, or RH.

It records zero-density estimates as a typed framework family: quantitative zero
counts in right-half rectangles.  The record distinguishes this family from
zero-location closure, zero/coefficient correlations, and critical-line growth
or moment asymptotics.

The Halasz/Lindelof item is used only as a one-way strength relation: strong
critical-line growth control supplies density consequences.  No converse and no
equivalence with RH is asserted.

The status is candidate, verified-on-3-density-instances, corpus-pending.
