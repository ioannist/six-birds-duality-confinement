# Step 113 nonclaim boundary

Step 113 does not prove RH.

It does not prove that the boundary-packet sector \(\mathcal B\) lies in Burnol's co-Poisson subspace \(P_a\).

It does not prove \(c_N\to1\) for the actual zeta boundary sector.

It proves:

\[
\mathcal B\subseteq P_a
\Rightarrow
\overline{\bigcup_N\mathcal A_N}\supseteq\mathcal B,
\]

and

\[
\delta_{\mathcal B,a}>0
\Rightarrow
\liminf_N\epsilon_N\ge\delta_{\mathcal B,a}.
\]

The next actual analytic question is the Burnol inclusion test.
