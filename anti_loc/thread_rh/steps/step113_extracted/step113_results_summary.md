# Step 113 summary — Burnol density/visibility theorem attempt

Step 113 treats \(c_N\) as a **spanning residual** of a constructible Burnol/co-Poisson atom family:

\[
\epsilon_N=\|(I-P_{\mathcal A_N})M_NG_{\mathcal B,N}^{-1/2}\|,
\qquad
c_N=1-\epsilon_N^2.
\]

The attempted theorem is now conditional and sharp:

\[
\mathcal B\subseteq P_a
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\overline{\bigcup_N\mathcal A_N}\supseteq\mathcal B
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c_N\to1.
\]

Burnol supplies the key complement theorem for \(0<a<1\):

\[
Y_a^\perp=P_a.
\]

Thus the density question becomes the inclusion gate:

\[
\boxed{\Pi_{Y_a}\mathcal B=0.}
\]

The exact obstruction is

\[
\delta_{\mathcal B,a}=\|(I-\Pi_{P_a})|_{\mathcal B}\|=\|\Pi_{Y_a}|_{\mathcal B}\|.
\]

If \(\delta_{\mathcal B,a}>0\), then no co-Poisson atom family contained in \(P_a\) can make the visibility residual vanish:

\[
\liminf_N\epsilon_N\ge\delta_{\mathcal B,a}.
\]

So Burnol's theory gives either a density route or a sharp obstruction.

The next analytic test is:

\[
\boxed{\Pi_{Y_a}P_\infty\tau_\ell(I-P_\infty)=0?}
\]
