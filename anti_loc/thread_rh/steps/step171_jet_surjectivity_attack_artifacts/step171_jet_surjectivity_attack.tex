\section{Verdict}

\[
\boxed{\texttt{jet\_surjectivity\_verdict =
V\_surj\_genuinely\_kernel\_required}.}
\]

The upgraded structural audit does not prove projected zero-jet surjectivity,
does not refute it at any specific zeta zero, and does not produce a partial
zero/order partition.  It proves the record-relative meta-conclusion that all
available structural routes reduce to the same missing datum: an exact
Mellin-side Sonin/prolate projection kernel
\[
  K_\infty(s,s')
\]
or an equivalent range-evaluator matrix theorem deciding
\[
  \langle \Mz G,\Psym y_{\rho,k}\rangle
\]
for legal Burnol inputs \(G\in\Blegal\).

\section{T1. Inherited definitions}

\subsection*{Projection}

Step 104 records the raw archimedean Sonin model:
\[
  H=L^2(\mathbb R)
\]
or the even subspace. \(P_\lambda\) is the spatial cutoff projection onto
\([-\lambda,\lambda]\), and
\[
  \widehat P_\lambda=\mathcal F^{-1}P_\lambda\mathcal F.
\]
The archimedean Sonin space is
\[
  \mathcal S_\lambda=\ker P_\lambda\cap\ker\widehat P_\lambda,
\]
and \(S_\lambda\) is the orthogonal projection onto \(\mathcal S_\lambda\).

Step 102 identifies \(P_\infty\) as the archimedean Sonin/prolate projection.
Step 162 places it in the Burnol/Sonine pulled-evaluator Hilbert context.
Step 170 records the Mellin-side representative:
\[
  \Psym=\Uinf P_\infty\Uinf^{-1}.
\]
Therefore the exact inherited formula for arbitrary Mellin-side \(F\) is
\[
  \boxed{
  (\Psym F)(s)
  :=
  (\Uinf P_\infty\Uinf^{-1}F)(s)
  }
\]
with \(P_\infty\) identified with the Sonin/prolate projection \(S_\lambda\)
under the inherited archimedean model.

The inherited records do not provide
\[
  (\Psym F)(s)=\int K_\infty(s,s')F(s')\,d\mu(s').
\]

\subsection*{Legal Burnol input}

Step 119 declares legal Burnol generators

  g_j\in C_c^\infty([a,A])
\]
with right-Mellin transform
\[
  \widehat g_j(s)=\int_0^\infty g_j(t)t^{-s}\,dt

  M(\mathcal Cg_j)(s)=\zeta(s)\widehat g_j(s).

Thus the legal Mellin-side input in this step is \(G=\widehat g\), not an
arbitrary atom, constant, polynomial, or evaluator-side vector.

\subsection*{Zero-evaluator jets}

Step 153 defines Burnol's zero-evaluator vector by
\[
  [f,Y^a_{w,k}]_{L_a}=M(f)^{(k)}(w),
  \qquad f\in L_a.
\]
In the completed Mellin model,
\[
  \MG(Y^a_{w,k})(s)=\partial_{\overline w}^{\,k}K_a^\Gamma(s,w)
\]
under the Hilbert convention, and
\[
  \MG(Y^a_{w,k})(s)=\partial_w^{\,k}K_a^\Gamma(s,w)
\]
under Burnol's analytic bilinear convention. Step 153 also sets
\[
  T_a:=\MG J_a\Uinf^{-1}
\]
and records the pulled zero-evaluator formula
\[
  \Uinf(J_a^*Y^a_{w,k})
  =
  T_a^*\partial_{\overline w}^{\,k}K_a^\Gamma(\cdot,w).
\]

Thus for legal \(F\), the jet \(F^{(k)}(\rho)\) is exactly the Burnol
zero-evaluator functional at \((\rho,k)\).

\section{T2. Surjectivity attack}

For a zeta zero \(\rho\) and \(0\le k<m_\rho\), define
\[
  L_{\rho,k}(G):=\partial_s^k(\Psym\Mz G)(\rho).
\]
Using the zero-evaluator vector \(y_{\rho,k}\) in the completed carrier,
\[
\begin{aligned}
  L_{\rho,k}(G)
    &= \langle \Psym\Mz G,y_{\rho,k}\rangle \\
    &= \langle \Mz G,\Psym^*y_{\rho,k}\rangle.
\end{aligned}
\]
Since \(\Psym\) is a transported orthogonal projection,
\[
  \Psym^*=\Psym,\qquad \Psym^2=\Psym,\qquad \Psym=\Psym^*\Psym,
\]
and hence
\[
  \boxed{
  L_{\rho,k}(G)=\langle \Mz G,\Psym y_{\rho,k}\rangle.
  }
\]
Surjectivity at \((\rho,k)\) is equivalent to this functional not vanishing
identically on the legal Burnol class \(\Blegal\). Refutation at
\((\rho,k)\) is equivalent to its identical vanishing on \(\Blegal\).

\subsection*{T2a. Constructive direction}

\paragraph{Mellin bump or atom near \(\rho\).}
If \(K_\infty(s,s')\) were known, one could try to choose \(G\) so that
\[
  \partial_s^k
  \int K_\infty(s,s')\zeta(s')G(s')\,d\mu(s')
  \bigg|_{s=\rho}
  \ne0.
\]
But the inherited carrier does not declare Mellin atoms or arbitrary bumps as
legal \(G\). It declares \(G=\widehat g\) for compactly supported Burnol input
with endpoint vanishings. No inherited Paley-Wiener density theorem is supplied
that lets such \(G\) approximate an atom in the required projected-evaluator
functional.

\paragraph{Sonin zero-evaluator atom.}
The vector \(Y^a_{\rho,k}\), or its pullback \(J_a^*Y^a_{\rho,k}\), is an
evaluator-side object:
\[
  \Uinf(J_a^*Y^a_{\rho,k})
  =
  T_a^*\partial_{\overline \rho}^{\,k}K_a^\Gamma(\cdot,\rho).
\]
Using it as an input would require a legal \(G\) solving a divisibility or
pullback problem such as \(M_\zeta G=J_a^*Y^a_{\rho,k}\), or a weaker projected
nonzero pairing theorem. No such record is inherited.

\paragraph{Constant or polynomial profile.}
These profiles are not declared legal Burnol inputs. Moreover, before applying
\(\Psym\),
\[
  \partial_s^k(\zeta G)(\rho)=0
  \qquad(0\le k<m_\rho)
\]
because of the zeta factor. The projection may mix the profile and revive a
jet, but deciding that requires \(K_\infty\) or the projected evaluator
\(\Psym y_{\rho,k}\).

\subsection*{T2b. Refutation direction: structural audit}

\paragraph{U1a. Pairing and duality.}
The strongest possible pairing refutation would prove, for some specific
\((\rho_0,k_0)\),
\[
  \Psym y_{\rho_0,k_0}\in(\Mz\Blegal)^\perp.
\]
Then
\[
  L_{\rho_0,k_0}(G)
  =
  \langle \Mz G,\Psym y_{\rho_0,k_0}\rangle
  =
  0
  \qquad(G\in\Blegal).
\]
The inherited range information is only
\[
  \Psym y_{\rho_0,k_0}\in\Ran\Psym
  =
  \Uinf(\mathcal S_\lambda),
  \qquad
  \mathcal S_\lambda=\ker P_\lambda\cap\ker\widehat P_\lambda.
\]
This is a support-Fourier Sonin condition.  No inherited Step
102/104/105/153/162 record identifies \(\Ran\Psym\), or any vector in it, with
\((\Mz\Blegal)^\perp\).  Thus the duality route reduces exactly to the missing
range-evaluator matrix element
\[
  \langle \Mz G,\Psym y_{\rho_0,k_0}\rangle.
\]

\paragraph{U1b. Zero-evaluator structure.}
Step 153 gives
\[
  y_{\rho,k}
  =
  T_a^*\partial_{\overline\rho}^{\,k}K_a^\Gamma(\cdot,\rho)
\]
in the transported completed model.  A structural zero-evaluator refutation
would need a theorem of the form
\[
  \Psym y_{\rho,k}
  =
  \sum_{j\le J}c_j\,y_{\rho'_j,k'_j}
\]
with labels for which multiplication by \(\zeta\) forces the pairings to
vanish, or at least
\[
  \langle \Mz G,\Psym y_{\rho,k}\rangle
  =
  \sum_{j\le J}c_j\,\partial_s^{k'_j}(\zeta G)(\rho'_j).
\]
No inherited record states that \(\Psym\) maps zero-evaluator atoms to
zero-evaluator atoms or to a zero-annihilating span.  Step 153 explicitly
leaves the projected Sonine kernel as a load-bearing projection, so this route
also requires the projected evaluator \(\Psym y_{\rho,k}\).

\paragraph{U1c. Algebraic idempotency.}
Projection algebra gives only
\[
\begin{aligned}
  L_{\rho,k}(G)
  &=\langle \Psym\Mz G,y_{\rho,k}\rangle \\
  &=\langle \Psym\Mz G,\Psym y_{\rho,k}\rangle \\
  &=\langle \Mz G,\Psym y_{\rho,k}\rangle .
\end{aligned}
\]
This inserts \(\Psym\) on the evaluator side; it does not commute
\(\Psym\) past \(M_\zeta\), does not prove \(\Mz\Blegal\perp\Ran\Psym\), and
does not preserve the zeta-divisibility ideal.  Therefore idempotency supplies
no cancellation beyond the same unknown pairing.

\paragraph{U1d. Specific zero test.}
Taking the lowest critical zero
\[
  \rho_1=\frac12+14.134725\ldots i,\qquad k_0=0,
\]
does not improve the situation.  Step 102/104/105 records are about
archimedean Sonin/prolate projection geometry and shifted compactness or
Calkin repair.  Step 142/143/144 records concern \(\Omega\)-compatible density,
blind-sector floors, and completed-tail promotion.  None of these records
contains a matrix element, eigenlabel, or cancellation identity tied to
\(\rho_1\) or to any zeta-zero label.  Hence no specific \((\rho_1,0)\)
vanishing or nonvanishing decision is inherited.

\subsection*{T2c. Partial outcome}

A partial verdict would require at least one decided subset:
\[
  L_{\rho,k}\not\equiv0\quad\text{on one family}
  \qquad\text{or}\qquad
  L_{\rho,k}\equiv0\quad\text{on another family}.
\]
The records give no such subset.  They provide neither a zero/order partition
nor a range theorem distinguishing special \(\rho\)'s.  Thus the partial route
also collapses to the missing \(K_\infty\) or equivalent range-evaluator
matrix.

\subsection*{U2. Non-kernel constructive direction}

\paragraph{Candidate \(G=Y_{\rho_0,k_0}\).}
This candidate is not legal: \(Y_{\rho_0,k_0}\) is an evaluator vector, while
legal \(G\)'s are right-Mellin transforms \(\widehat g\) of Step 119 Burnol
generators.  Even ignoring legality, the proposed test is
\[
  \langle \Mz Y_{\rho_0,k_0},\Psym y_{\rho_0,k_0}\rangle,
\]
which is again a projected-evaluator matrix element not supplied by Step 153.

\paragraph{Candidate \(G=1\).}
The constant profile is not a Step 119 legal Burnol transform with endpoint
vanishings.  Formally, the unprojected jet vanishes at \(\rho_0\):
\[
  \partial_s^{k_0}\zeta(s)\big|_{s=\rho_0}=0
  \qquad(k_0<m_{\rho_0}).
\]
The projected jet would be
\[
  \langle \zeta,\Psym y_{\rho_0,k_0}\rangle,
\]
and deciding whether projection revives the jet again requires \(K_\infty\) or
the equivalent projected-evaluator theorem.

\section{T3. Consequence}

  \boxed{\texttt{target\_equivalence\_consequence =
  kernel\_required\_not\_decided}.}

specific sharper-than-RH obstruction. The Step 170 zero-free-output subclass
remains the retained conditional result.
