\section{Purpose}

Step 110 split the boundary-packet source route into two gates:
\[
\gamma_N=\text{character/source lower-frame strength},
\qquad
c_N=\text{coefficient visibility of the boundary dictionary}.
\]
The present step asks whether the observed collapse of \(c_N\) is a defect of a
naive short-Dirichlet dictionary, or a genuine obstruction.  The repair proposed here
is to replace the naive dictionary by a Burnol/co-Poisson-native dictionary.

\section{Burnol-native coefficient dictionaries}

Burnol's co-Poisson/Sonine framework supplies the natural carrier.  At the critical
threshold \(a=1\), the zeta-zero evaluator vectors are complete/minimal in the
extended Sonine space \(L_1\), while for \(a<1\) the perpendicular complement is
the co-Poisson subspace \(P_a\).  This suggests that the coefficient dictionary should
not be an arbitrary short Dirichlet dictionary.  It should be generated from declared
co-Poisson/Sonine data:
\[
g_\nu\in L^1(a,A),\qquad A=1/a,
\]
with parity, support, Mellin, and vanishing records declared before the target
boundary-packet test.

In Mellin language the co-Poisson mechanism gives a native coefficient map of the
form
\[
g_\nu
\longmapsto
\zeta(s)\widehat g_\nu(s)
\]
on the critical strip, subject to the usual pole/completion records.  Thus a
Burnol-native dictionary is a declared finite family
\[
\mathcal D_N^{\mathrm{B}}
=
\operatorname{span}\{C(g_\nu):\nu\in I_N\},
\]
where \(C(g_\nu)\) denotes the co-Poisson synthesis of \(g_\nu\), or equivalently
its completed Mellin-side representative.

\section{Finite visibility identity}

Let
\[
M_N:Y_{\mathcal B,N}\to H_N
\]
synthesize a finite boundary-packet dictionary in the response Hilbert space, and let
\[
D_N:\mathbb C^{I_N}\to H_N
\]
synthesize the coefficient dictionary.  Put
\[
G_{\mathcal B,N}=M_N^*M_N,\qquad H_N=D_N^*D_N,
\]
and define
\[
R_N=D_N^\dagger M_N.
\]
Let \(P_N\) be the orthogonal projection onto \(\operatorname{Ran}D_N\), and
\[
E_{\mathrm{coef},N}=(I-P_N)M_N.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Coefficient visibility identity]
On the legal quotient of \(G_{\mathcal B,N}\),
\[
R_N^*H_NR_N
=
M_N^*P_NM_N
=
G_{\mathcal B,N}-E_{\mathrm{coef},N}^*E_{\mathrm{coef},N}.
\]
Consequently the visibility constant is
\[
c_N
=
\lambda_{\min}\!\left(
G_{\mathcal B,N}^{-1/2}R_N^*H_NR_NG_{\mathcal B,N}^{-1/2}
\right)
=
1-\|E_{\mathrm{coef},N}G_{\mathcal B,N}^{-1/2}\|^2.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since \(R_N=D_N^\dagger M_N\), one has \(D_NR_N=P_NM_N\).  Hence
\[
R_N^*H_NR_N
=
M_N^*(D_N^\dagger)^*D_N^*D_ND_N^\dagger M_N
=
M_N^*P_NM_N.
\]
The second equality follows from \(I=P_N+(I-P_N)\):
\[
M_N^*M_N-M_N^*P_NM_N
=
M_N^*(I-P_N)M_N
=
E_{\mathrm{coef},N}^*E_{\mathrm{coef},N}.
\]
The formula for \(c_N\) is the minimum generalized eigenvalue after quotienting
null directions.
\end{proof}

\section{Visibility repair by native strict extension}

\begin{proposition}[Projection monotonicity]
If \(D_N\) is replaced by a strict extension
\[
D_N^+=[D_N\;D_N^{\mathrm{new}}],
\]
then
\[
c_N(D_N^+)\ge c_N(D_N).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The range projection satisfies \(P_N^+\succeq P_N\), so
\[
M_N^*P_N^+M_N\succeq M_N^*P_NM_N.
\]
Taking minimum generalized eigenvalues relative to \(G_{\mathcal B,N}\) gives the
claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Native repair sufficiency]
Suppose a declared Burnol-native dictionary satisfies
\[
\|(I-P_N^{\mathrm{B}})M_NG_{\mathcal B,N}^{-1/2}\|\le \varepsilon_N.
\]
Then
\[
c_N^{\mathrm{B}}\ge 1-\varepsilon_N^2.
\]
If \(\varepsilon_N\to0\), the coefficient blind spot vanishes at finite-window level.
\end{theorem}

\section{Source-frame composition}

If the character/source Gram matrix satisfies
\[
G_{\mathcal X,N}\succeq \gamma_N H_N
\]
and the coefficient visibility gate satisfies
\[
R_N^*H_NR_N\succeq c_NG_{\mathcal B,N},
\]
then
\[
R_N^*G_{\mathcal X,N}R_N
\succeq
\gamma_Nc_NG_{\mathcal B,N}.
\]
Thus the effective source strength is
\[
\Lambda_N=\gamma_Nc_N.
\]
The completed route requires \(\Lambda_N\to\infty\), together with fixed/exhaustive
promotion and vanishing tail records.

\section{No-smuggling boundary}

The Burnol-native dictionary may not be chosen by looking at the final boundary
singular vectors and then adding them as atoms.  It must be declared through
co-Poisson/Sonine data: support interval, parity, Mellin transform, pole/completion
record, semilocal transport, and all-six channel status.  Otherwise the visibility
repair is target-selected and does not count as a lawful source record.

\section{Interpretation}

The identity
\[
G_{\mathcal B,N}-R_N^*H_NR_N=E_{\mathrm{coef},N}^*E_{\mathrm{coef},N}
\]
is exactly an adequacy residual.  It measures what the boundary-packet sector sees
that the coefficient dictionary cannot see.  In the notation of the membrane
framework, it is the finite \( \Xi \)-residual of the coefficient interface.
