# Step 111 nonclaim boundary

This step does not prove RH.

It does not prove that Burnol/co-Poisson atoms actually span the completed boundary-packet sector.

It does not prove an asymptotic lower bound for c_N.

It does not prove the Heap--Soundararajan scalar lower-moment method has an operator-valued lift.

It proves the finite algebraic visibility identities and shows, on a toy finite model, that visibility collapse can be dictionary-dependent: a native/co-Poisson-like dictionary can repair c_N while an ordinary integer Dirichlet dictionary leaves a large worst-direction blind spot.

The RH-level obligation remains: construct a non-smuggled Burnol-native coefficient dictionary and prove gamma_N c_N -> infinity with fixed/exhaustive promotion.
