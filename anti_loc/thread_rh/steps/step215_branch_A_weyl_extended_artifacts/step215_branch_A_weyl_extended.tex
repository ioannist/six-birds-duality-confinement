\section*{Step 215: Branch A Weyl Extension}

\subsection*{Setup}

We extend the Step 214 finite-grid singular/Weyl diagnostic for
\[
  C_\ell=(I-P_\infty)M_{m_\ell}P_\infty,\qquad \ell=\log 2,
\]
using
\[
  u_n=\frac{\kappa_{\rho_n,0}^{1/2}}{\|\kappa_{\rho_n,0}^{1/2}\|}.
\]
The CAND1 model is the Burnol-boundary kernel model
\[
  \kappa_{\rho_n}^{(1)}(\tau)=K_{1/2}^{\Gamma}(1/2+i\tau,\rho_n),
\]
with \(K_{1/2}^{\Gamma}\) sourced from Burnol 2002, Theorem 8 and equation 1.  The CAND2 comparison model is the Step 207 zeta-form single-term expression
\[
  \kappa_{\rho_n}^{(2)}(\tau)=
  \frac{\zeta(1/2+i\tau)}
  {(1/2+i\tau-\rho_n)\zeta'(\rho_n)\pi^{-\rho_n/2}\Gamma(\rho_n/2)}.
\]
CAND2 is not asserted to be the correct \(a=1/2\) transport formula; it is a robustness probe.

\subsection*{Numerical Result}

The CAND1 test was extended to \(n=1,\dots,20\).  The minimum over all twenty values is
\[
  \min_{1\le n\le 20}\|C_\ell u_n^{(1)}\|=0.7937590184564269,
\]
and the conservative lower bound after the displayed \(5\cdot 10^{-2}\) error budget is
\[
  \delta_{20}^{(1)}\ge 0.7437590184564269.
\]
On the new tail \(n=11,\dots,20\),
\[
  \min_{11\le n\le 20}\|C_\ell u_n^{(1)}\|=1.4403036850155422,
\]
so no decay appears in the extended range.

For CAND2, computed on \(n=1,\dots,10\),
\[
  \min_{1\le n\le 10}\|C_\ell u_n^{(2)}\|=0.2388748880100559,
\]
with conservative lower bound \(0.1888748880100559\).  The lower-bound pattern is therefore nonzero in the comparison model, but much weaker than CAND1.

\subsection*{Weak-Null Caveat}

The finite-span weak-null diagnostic does not pass:
\[
  \max_{i<j}|\langle u_i,u_j\rangle|=0.9999887398876486,
\]
and on the tail \(11\le i<j\le20\),
\[
  \max_{11\le i<j\le20}|\langle u_i,u_j\rangle|=0.9999845657507579.
\]
Thus the test sequence is not certified weakly null in the sampled span, and a theorem-grade Calkin obstruction is not obtained here.

\subsection*{Verdict}

The Step 214 finite-grid lower-bound diagnostic is strengthened, especially on \(n=11,\dots,20\), and the CAND2 comparison remains nonzero.  However, the weak-null caveat remains unresolved and in fact fails the finite-span test.  The verdict is
\[
  \boxed{\mathrm{V\_weyl\_extended\_obstruction\_diagnostic}}.
\]
