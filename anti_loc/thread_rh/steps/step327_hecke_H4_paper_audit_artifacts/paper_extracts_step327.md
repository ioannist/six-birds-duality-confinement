# Step 327 Paper Extracts

Scope: H4 needs `Hecke kappa_chi`, `P_infty,chi`, a projected reproducing kernel, and a finite matrix residual.

## Burnol 2004, `On Fourier and Zeta(s)`

Source: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0112254`

Short verbatim extract:

> "The method applies to Dirichlet L-series as well"

Short verbatim extract:

> "Pχ corresponds to multiplication by L(1 − s, χ)"

Short verbatim extract:

> "We let Wλχ ⊂ Kλ be the closure"

Short verbatim extract:

> "w is a non-trivial zero ρ"

Interpretation: Burnol gives the strongest H4-relevant framework. It constructs a Dirichlet-character co-Poisson operator, a Sonine-space closure `W_lambda^chi`, and zero-attached vectors `Z_{rho,k}`. This is a real Dirichlet-side analog of the zeta Sonine framework. It is not, however, the cascade's finite `kappa_chi` sampling ledger or finite Gram/c-matrix residual.

## Burnol 2001, `Sur certains espaces...`

Source: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0105120`

Short verbatim extract:

> "a subspace related to the Riemann zeta function"

Short verbatim extract:

> "The method applies to Dirichlet L-series as well"

Interpretation: confirms the Sonine quotient/vector idea and signals a Dirichlet extension, but it is not a finite projected-kernel residual construction.

## Conrey-Snaith 2007

Source: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0509480`

Short verbatim extract:

> "family of Dirichlet L-functions"

Short verbatim extract:

> "one-level density function for zeros"

Interpretation: useful for Dirichlet-family zero statistics and ratios, but it does not provide a Hecke Sonine projection, reproducing kernel, or finite residual matrix.
