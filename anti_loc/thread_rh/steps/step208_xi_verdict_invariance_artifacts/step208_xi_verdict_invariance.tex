\section*{Step 208: Candidate-Invariance Test for \(\Xi_{\rm matrix\_source}\)}

This step computes the finite matrix-source residual under both Step 207
candidate samples for
\[
  \kappa_i(\tau)=\bigl[T_{1/2}^*K_{1/2}^{\Gamma}(\cdot,\rho_i)\bigr](1/2+i\tau).
\]
The computation is diagnostic: it tests verdict invariance without choosing
which candidate is the true transport sample.

\subsection*{Finite-grid operator}

Using the Step 173 operational identity,
\[
K_\infty^{op}(s,s')=\delta(\tau-\tau')
-\frac{\sin(\lambda(\tau-\tau'))}{\pi(\tau-\tau')}
-\sum_n\Psi_n^\lambda(s)\overline{\Psi_n^\lambda(s')},
\]
the discretized projection is
\[
  Pf=f-\operatorname{sinc}*f-\sum_{n<N}\Psi_n\langle \Psi_n,f\rangle .
\]
For \(\ell=\log 2\),
\[
  C f=(I-P)M_{m_\ell}Pf,\qquad
  (M_{m_\ell}f)(\tau)=e^{i\ell\tau}f(\tau).
\]
The matrix entries are
\[
  c_{ij}^{(k)}
  ={1\over 2\pi}\int
  \overline{\kappa_i^{(k)}(\tau)}
  \bigl[(I-P)M_{m_\ell}P\kappa_j^{(k)}\bigr](\tau)\,d\tau.
\]

\subsection*{Computed commutator scale}

With 200 \(\tau\)-nodes and 24 PSWF terms:
\[
\max |c_{ij}^{\rm CAND1}|=9.03975455\cdot10^{-11},\qquad
\|c^{\rm CAND1}\|_F=9.03980135\cdot10^{-11}.
\]
\[
\max |c_{ij}^{\rm CAND2}|=2.20220836\cdot10^{16},\qquad
\|c^{\rm CAND2}\|_F=2.20332330\cdot10^{16}.
\]

\subsection*{Compressed-HS residual}

The inherited unnormalized formula is
\[
  \Xi_{\rm matrix\_source}
  =\operatorname{tr}(G^{-1}c^\dagger G^{-1}c).
\]
Using the Step 204/207 Gram matrix:
\[
\Xi_{\rm CAND1}
=0.0287707490271961-4.5574170385\cdot10^{-6}i,
\]
with \(|\Xi_{\rm CAND1}|=2.8770749388\cdot10^{-2}\) and error bound
\(1.4685454221\cdot10^{14}\).
\[
\Xi_{\rm CAND2}
=8.1516178439\cdot10^{65}+9.8051151700\cdot10^{61}i,
\]
with \(|\Xi_{\rm CAND2}|=8.1516179028\cdot10^{65}\) and error bound
\(2.6239388729\cdot10^{66}\).

\subsection*{Verdict}

Both candidate computations are precision-limited after compression by the
ill-conditioned inherited Gram inverse.  The closure/nonzero verdict is not
certified invariant.
\[
  \boxed{\texttt{V\_xi\_invariant\_partial}.}
\]
