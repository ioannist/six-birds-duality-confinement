\section{Purpose}
Steps 103--105 isolated the noncompact obstruction created by finite-place log-shifts in the semilocal Sonin/prolate setting.  For a nonzero shift $a=\log p$ the raw archimedean Sonin block
\[
  P_\infty\tau_a(I-P_\infty)
\]
contains a boundary-packet sector which is not compact.  Step 105 showed that a compact or finite-rank perturbation of $P_\infty$ cannot remove this sector at the Calkin level.

Therefore the active route is now source coercivity.  The goal of this note is to formulate the exact theorem by which Dirichlet/Hecke character sources can absorb the boundary-packet sector.

\section{Carrier identification}
We use the following carrier dictionary.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item $Y^-$ is the completed anti-invariant Sonine/co-Poisson response carrier.  Burnol's Sonine and co-Poisson spaces supply the natural candidate: the zeta-zero evaluators form complete/minimal systems in the critical threshold spaces, while co-Poisson subspaces describe perpendicular complements below threshold.
\item $P_\infty$ is the archimedean Sonin/prolate projection imported from the Connes--Consani archimedean positivity mechanism.

\]
\item The boundary-packet obstruction is generated by

  B_a:=P_\infty\tau_a(I-P_\infty).
\]
\item Dirichlet/Hecke character or mollifier source maps are denoted
\[
  Q_\omega:Y^-\to H_\omega.
\]
\end{itemize}

\section{Boundary packet sector}

\begin{definition}[Boundary-packet sector]
For a finite semilocal place set $S$ define
\[
  \mathcal B_S
  :=
  \overline{\operatorname{span}}
  \left\{
    \operatorname{Ran}\,|B_a|:
    a\in\mathcal L_S\setminus\{0\}
  \right\}\subseteq Y^-.
\]
Let $\Pi_{\mathcal B}$ denote the orthogonal projection onto $\mathcal B_S$.
\end{definition}

The point of $\mathcal B_S$ is that it isolates the noncompact boundary-packet modes detected in Step 104.  The compactness shortcut fails precisely when the positive part of the semilocal residual has nonzero mass on this sector.

\begin{definition}[Boundary dominance of the semilocal residual]
Let
\[
  \Delta_S=q_{\mathrm{Weil},S}-q_{\mathrm{pos},S}
\]
be the semilocal cross-term residual.  We say that $\Delta_S^+$ is boundary-dominated with compact tail if there are constants $c_B>0$ and compact positive $K_S\succeq0$ such that
\[
  \Delta_S^+
  \preceq
  c_B\Pi_{\mathcal B}+K_S.
\]
If $K_S=0$ the residual is purely boundary-dominated.
\end{definition}

\section{Source records}
A source record is upstream-visible data
\[
  s=(\omega,Q_\omega,\Theta_\omega,\lambda_{\omega,n},D_\omega)
\]
with declared origin in the arithmetic carrier.  Its response-side source contribution is
\[
  \lambda_{\omega,n}Q_\omega^*\Theta_\omega^{-1}Q_\omega.
\]
For a finite source family $\mathcal X_n$ define
\[
  F_n
  :=
  \sum_{\omega\in\mathcal X_n}
  \lambda_{\omega,n}Q_\omega^*\Theta_\omega^{-1}Q_\omega.
\]

\begin{definition}[Boundary lower frame]
The source family satisfies a boundary lower frame if
\[
  \Pi_{\mathcal B}F_n\Pi_{\mathcal B}
  \succeq
  \Lambda_n\Pi_{\mathcal B}(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}\Pi_{\mathcal B}-R_n^{\mathcal B},
\]
where
\[
  \Lambda_n\to\infty,
  \qquad
  R_n^{\mathcal B}\to0
\]
against the fixed/exhaustive zero ledger.
\end{definition}

A full anti-invariant source frame is stronger:
\[
  F_n\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}-R_n,
  \qquad \Lambda_n\to\infty.
\]
The boundary lower frame is the targeted version needed after the compactness test: it charges precisely the noncompact shifted Sonin boundary packets.

\section{Main theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Boundary-packet source absorption]
Assume:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(A\arabic*)]
\item $\Delta_S^+$ is boundary-dominated with compact tail:
\[
  \Delta_S^+\preceq c_B\Pi_{\mathcal B}+K_S.
\]
\item The source family satisfies the boundary lower frame
\[
  \Pi_{\mathcal B}F_n\Pi_{\mathcal B}
  \succeq
  \Lambda_n\Pi_{\mathcal B}(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}\Pi_{\mathcal B}-R_n^{\mathcal B}.
\]
\item The compact tail is captured by finite windows:
for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists a window $\Pi_{n}$ such that
\[
  (I-\Pi_n)K_S(I-\Pi_n)\preceq \varepsilon I.
\]
\item Cross terms between $\mathcal B_S$ and $\mathcal B_S^\perp$ are defect-paid by $E_{\mathrm{cross},n}$.
\end{enumerate}
Then for each $n$,
\[
  \Delta_S^+
  \preceq
  c_B\Lambda_n^{-1}F_n
  +E_{\mathrm{abs},n},
\]
where
\[
  E_{\mathrm{abs},n}
  =
  c_B\Lambda_n^{-1}R_n^{\mathcal B}
  +K_S^{\mathrm{tail}}(n)
  +E_{\mathrm{cross},n}.
\]
If
\[
  \Lambda_n\to\infty,
  \qquad
  E_{\mathrm{abs},n}\to0
\]
in the fixed/exhaustive ledger sense, then $\Delta_S^+$ is source-absorbed and the semilocal Weil--Douglas bridge can proceed with declared defects.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By boundary domination,
\[
  \Delta_S^+\preceq c_B\Pi_{\mathcal B}+K_S.
\]
The boundary lower frame gives, on $\mathcal B_S$,
\[
  \Pi_{\mathcal B}
  \preceq
  \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_0^-{}^{1/2}F_n\Theta_0^-{}^{1/2}
  +\Lambda_n^{-1}R_n^{\mathcal B}
\]
up to the fixed metric convention.  Equivalently, after transporting to the declared budget coordinates,
\[
  c_B\Pi_{\mathcal B}
  \preceq
  c_B\Lambda_n^{-1}F_n+c_B\Lambda_n^{-1}R_n^{\mathcal B}.
\]
The compact tail is captured by the finite window up to $K_S^{\mathrm{tail}}(n)$, and cross terms are assigned to $E_{\mathrm{cross},n}$.  Combining these inequalities gives the stated absorption bound.  The convergence of $\Lambda_n^{-1}$ and the declared tail/defect terms yields the fixed/exhaustive absorption conclusion.
\end{proof}

\section{How the four literature lines enter}

\subsection{Line A: Burnol Sonine/co-Poisson carrier}
Burnol supplies the natural $Y^-$ carrier.  The key input is that zeta-zero evaluator systems are complete/minimal at the critical threshold in extended Sonine spaces, and that co-Poisson subspaces occur as perpendicular complements below threshold.  In membrane language, this is the zero-ledger exhaustivity and carrier visibility record.

\subsection{Line B: Heap--Soundararajan dual mollifier source template}
Heap--Soundararajan provide a scalar positive bilinear mechanism using short Dirichlet polynomials.  Their method computes mixed mean values involving $L(1/2)$ and suitable Dirichlet polynomials and turns them into lower bounds of the right size.  The framework task is to lift this scalar positive bilinear form to operator-valued lower coercivity on $Y^-$.

\subsection{Line C: Hecke-character spectral cover}
The character-source maps $Q_\omega$ must be native to a carrier, not selected after the target is known.  A Bost--Connes/Cuntz--Pimsner style realization is the proposed structural way to make Hecke characters appear as a source spectrum of construction rather than a post-hoc test family.

\subsection{Line D: CCM semilocal Hardy--Titchmarsh arena}
The semilocal Hardy--Titchmarsh transform gives the common response geometry in which finite local Euler factors deform the archimedean measure and commute with the functional-equation grading.  This is the arena in which $\Delta_S$ and the boundary-packet sector are defined.

\section{Operator-valued lift obligation}
The genuine open construction is not the finite character identity
\[
  \sum_{\chi\in\widehat G}P_\chi=I_{\ell^2(G)}.
\]
It is the completed lower-frame lift
\[
  F_n\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}-R_n,
  \qquad \Lambda_n\to\infty,
\]
or at least the boundary-sector version
\[
  \Pi_{\mathcal B}F_n\Pi_{\mathcal B}
  \succeq
  \Lambda_n\Pi_{\mathcal B}(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}\Pi_{\mathcal B}-R_n^{\mathcal B}.
\]
The Heap--Soundararajan mollifier machinery is the scalar template for producing divergent lower strength.  The new requirement is an operator-valued lift that sees every boundary-packet direction.

\section{No-smuggling gates}
The theorem is invalid if any of the following occur:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The source family $\mathcal X_n$ is chosen after seeing the failing zero ledger.
\item Only large-sieve upper bounds are supplied.  The theorem needs lower frame bounds.
\item Finite character windows are used without Plancherel/exhaustivity tails.
\item The boundary-packet sector is replaced by a trace or scalar public shadow.
\item A zero-simplicity assumption is used to bypass source coverage.
\end{enumerate}
