\section{Purpose}
Steps 104--105 showed that the raw shifted archimedean Sonin block
\[
  P_\infty\tau_a(I-P_\infty)
\]
is noncompact for nonzero shifts and that compact or finite-rank perturbations of
\(P_\infty\) cannot repair its Calkin class.  Therefore the semilocal compactness
shortcut for
\[
  \Delta_S=q_{\rm Weil,S}-q_{\rm pos,S}
\]
is blocked unless the actual semilocal prolate operator supplies an essential
(non-compact) cancellation mechanism.  This note formulates the safer route:
source absorption of the surviving boundary-packet sector.

\section{Boundary-packet obstruction form}
Let \(Y^-\) be the completed anti-invariant response carrier.  In the present route
this is to be identified with a Burnol-type Sonine or extended Sonine carrier and
transported into the semilocal Connes--Consani--Moscovici response space.  Let
\(P_\infty\) be the archimedean Sonin/prolate projection, and let
\(\tau_\ell\) denote the log-shift generated by a finite-place mode \(\ell\).

\begin{definition}[shifted boundary-packet blocks]
For each finite-place log-shift mode \(\ell\in\mathcal L_S\), define
\[
  B_\ell:=P_\infty\tau_\ell(I-P_\infty).
\]
For positive weights \(\omega_\ell\), define the boundary-packet obstruction form
\[
  \mathsf B_S:=\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal L_S}\omega_\ell B_\ell^*B_\ell.
\]
\end{definition}

The role of \(\mathsf B_S\) is to record the noncompact sector discovered by the
shifted Sonin compactness test.  It is not a scalar error.  It is a positive response-side
currency on the directions where finite local factors mix the Sonin sector with its
complement.

\section{Source records}
A Hecke/Dirichlet source record is a tuple
\[
  s=(\omega,Q_\omega,\Theta_\omega,\lambda_{\omega,n},D_\omega),
\]
where \(Q_\omega:Y^-\to Y_\omega\) is a declared upstream-visible source readout,
\(\Theta_\omega\succ0\) is its response budget, and \(\lambda_{\omega,n}\ge0\) is its
stage weight.  The response-side source frame is
\[
  F_n:=\sum_{\omega\in\mathcal X_n}
  \lambda_{\omega,n}Q_\omega^*\Theta_\omega^{-1}Q_\omega.
\]

\section{Boundary absorption theorem}
\begin{theorem}[boundary-packet source absorption]
Suppose the positive semilocal cross-term satisfies
\[
  \Delta_S^+\preceq c_B\mathsf B_S+K_S+E_S,
\]
where \(c_B\ge0\), \(K_S\succeq0\) is compact or finite-window-exhaustible, and
\(E_S\succeq0\) is a declared defect.  Suppose also that the source frame satisfies
\[
  F_n\succeq \Lambda_n\mathsf B_S.
\]
If \(\Lambda_n\ge c_B\), then
\[
  \Delta_S^+\preceq F_n+K_S+E_S.
\]
If, in addition, \(K_S\preceq K_{S,m}+R_{S,m}\) with finite-window \(K_{S,m}\) and
\(R_{S,m}\to0\) in the fixed/exhaustive ledger sense, then the boundary-packet
cross-term is absorbed up to a vanishing compact/tail record.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from the two Loewner inequalities:
\[
 \Delta_S^+\preceq c_B\mathsf B_S+K_S+E_S
 \preceq \Lambda_n\mathsf B_S+K_S+E_S
 \preceq F_n+K_S+E_S.
\]
The compact-tail statement is the standard finite-rank approximation of compact
positive operators, promoted only with a fixed/exhaustive ledger record.
\end{proof}

\section{Full RH-scale collapse}
Boundary absorption alone is not yet RH.  The stronger completed lower-frame gate is
\[
  F_n\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1},
  \qquad \Lambda_n\to\infty.
\]
When this holds with vanishing source, bridge, and tail defects, the native anti-invariant
currency collapses:
\[
  K_n^-\preceq \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_0^-+E_{\rm src,n}\to0.
\]
Together with the fixed/exhaustive zero-ledger squeeze and the separating readout
\(\operatorname{Re}(s)-1/2\), this is the duality-confinement route.

\section{Partial coverage no-go}
\begin{proposition}[partial source coverage fails]
If there exists \(0\ne y\in\overline{\operatorname{Ran}\mathsf B_S}\) such that
\[
  Q_\omega y=0\qquad\forall\omega\in\bigcup_n\mathcal X_n,
\]
then no choice of weights \(\lambda_{\omega,n}\) can give a boundary lower frame on
\(\overline{\operatorname{Ran}\mathsf B_S}\).  In particular, if
\(\langle y,\Delta_S^+y\rangle>0\), source absorption fails in the direction \(y\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For such \(y\), \(\langle y,F_ny\rangle=0\) for every \(n\).  A lower frame
\(F_n\succeq \Lambda_n\mathsf B_S\) would imply
\(0\ge \Lambda_n\langle y,\mathsf B_Sy\rangle\), forcing
\(\langle y,\mathsf B_Sy\rangle=0\).  This contradicts \(y\) being a nonzero
boundary-packet direction if the obstruction form is nondegenerate there.
\end{proof}

\section{Arithmetic lift obligation}
The Heap--Soundararajan dual-mollifier method supplies a scalar lower-bound mechanism built
from short Dirichlet polynomials.  In this framework it becomes useful only after an
operator-valued lift:
\[
  \sum_{\omega\in\mathcal X_n}\lambda_{\omega,n}
  Q_\omega^*\Theta_\omega^{-1}Q_\omega
  \succeq \Lambda_n\mathsf B_S
  \quad\hbox{or}\quad
  \succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}.
\]
Scalar lower moments are evidence for source strength.  They are not yet lower frames.
The missing theorem is a vector-valued dual-mollifier inequality on the completed Sonine response
carrier.

Burnol Sonine carrier and semilocal CCM carrier, finite-window staging with tail, exact packaging,

\section{Nonclaim boundary}

zero simplicity, and does not promote finite character windows without Plancherel/exhaustivity.
It only turns the boundary-packet noncompact sector into a precise source-absorption obligation.
