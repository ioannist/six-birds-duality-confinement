# Nonclaim Boundary

This step does not prove GRH or a general theorem for `L(s, chi_4)`.

The decomposition uses the exact identity `g'(rho)=L''(rho)/(2L'(rho))`, the standard odd-character archimedean logarithmic derivative, and a residual term representing the regular far-zero Hadamard contribution plus tail/normalization. It diagnoses one finite zero, `j=30`.
