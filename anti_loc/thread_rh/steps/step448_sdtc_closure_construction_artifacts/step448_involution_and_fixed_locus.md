# Step 448 Functional-Equation Involution and Fixed Locus (Stage I.7)

For zeta, define

```math
J_L(s)=1-\overline{s}.
```

For self-dual Selberg-class extensions, the same involution acts on completed zeros. For non-self-dual cases, the involution is conjugate-dual and transports zeros of `L` to zeros of the dual `bar L` with the appropriate root-number record.

The fixed locus is

```math
Fix(J_L)={s: Re(s)=1/2}.
```

The anti-invariant readout is

```math
psi_-(s)=Re(s)-1/2.
```

This is lawful under needles.tex `def:main:involutive-ledger`: `X=Z_L^nt`, `J=J_L`, `mu=mu_L`, `Y=R`, `mathcal J=-1`, and `psi(s)=Re(s)-1/2` with anti-invariant projection equal to itself. The readout separates the fixed locus.
