\item \(K_a^\Gamma(\cdot,\rho)\) is the reproducing kernel in the completed
Mellin image \(\mathcal L_a^\Gamma=A_\infty\mathcal L_a\), with Burnol 2002
equation 1 and Theorem 8 now supplying the projected kernel.
\item \(J_a^*\) is only the adjoint of the declared transport
\(J_a:H_\infty\to L_a\).  The inherited records do not give a concrete
inclusion/projection formula.
\item \(M_\Gamma\) is identified through
\[
M(f)(s)=\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\widehat f(s),
\]
but \(M_\Gamma^*\) on \(B(E_a)\) reproducing kernels is not specified as a
pointwise multiplier or cancellation.
\item \(U_\infty\) is the archimedean Hardy--Titchmarsh/Mellin realization;
Step 173 uses it for the \(P_\infty\) operational identity, but not as a
pointwise formula for this composite.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Boundary Identity Test}

The desired identity is
\[
T_a^*K_a^\Gamma(\cdot,\rho)(1/2+i\tau)
=K_a^\Gamma(1/2+i\tau,\rho).
\]
The inherited records do not prove it.  They also do not determine a corrected
gamma-phase formula, because that would require the concrete adjoint action
\[
M_\Gamma^*K_a^\Gamma(\cdot,\rho)
\]
inside the completed de Branges kernel space and the concrete action of
\(J_a^*\).
