
# Step 103 nonclaim boundary

This step does not prove RH.

It does not prove that the semilocal cross-term \(\Delta_S\) is compact.

It proves a reduction: the finite-place semilocal metric off-diagonal block decomposes into shifted Sonin/prolate off-diagonal blocks. Compactness must be proved for those shifted blocks, after the legal quotient.

Raw bounded local Euler-factor multiplication is not compact evidence. Finite numerical sections are support-only unless they prove decay of singular values or a tail/exhaustivity record.
