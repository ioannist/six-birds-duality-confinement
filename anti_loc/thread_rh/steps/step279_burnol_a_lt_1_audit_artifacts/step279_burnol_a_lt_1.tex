\section*{Source}
The audited source is Burnol's Section 6 in \emph{On Fourier and Zeta(s)}
(Forum Mathematicum 16 (2004), 789--840), titled
``Sonine spaces of de Branges, novel spaces \(HP_\lambda\), vectors
\(Z^\lambda_{\rho,k}\), Krein string of the zeta function.''

\section*{Paper Statements}
Burnol supplies evaluator vectors:
\[
[f,Z^\lambda_{w,k}] = M(f)^{(k)}(w),
\]
with the corresponding linear forms continuous on \(K_\lambda\).

The decisive span statement is:
\[
Z^\lambda_{\rho,k}\ \text{span }K_\lambda
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\lambda\ge 1.
\]
For \(0<\lambda<1\), Burnol explicitly proves that the zeta-zero evaluator
vectors do not span \(K_\lambda\).  The main theorem instead gives a
decomposition with an additional \(W'_\lambda\) component.

\section*{Cascade Comparison}
The cascade-needed CAND1/CAND2 agreement requires a finite or countable
zero-evaluator linear-combination form for \(a<1\), or an equivalent theorem
showing that the full and compressed evaluator norms agree.  Burnol Section 6
does not state this and in fact rules out the full zero-evaluator span for
\(\lambda<1\).
