# Nonclaim boundary for Step 105

This step does not prove RH and does not prove compactness of the semilocal cross-term. It proves a conditional obstruction: if the actual semilocal prolate projection differs from the transported archimedean Sonin projection only by a compact perturbation, then the raw shifted Sonin noncompact sector from Step 104 survives.

The step also does not claim that the Connes--Consani--Moscovici semilocal prolate program fails. It says the actual semilocal prolate projection must do essential, Calkin-level repair work not supplied by the transported archimedean projection alone.

Finite-dimensional checks are sanity models only, not Six Birds simulations and not RH evidence.
