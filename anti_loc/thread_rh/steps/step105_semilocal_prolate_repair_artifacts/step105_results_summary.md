# Step 105: Semilocal prolate repair test

## Main result

Step 104 showed that the raw shifted archimedean Sonin block

\[
B_a=P_\infty\tau_a(I-P_\infty)
\]

has a noncompact boundary-packet sector for every nonzero log-shift \(a\). Step 105 proves that a compact or finite-rank perturbation of the archimedean projection cannot repair this obstruction.

If \(\widetilde P-P\) is compact, then

\[
\widetilde P U(I-\widetilde P)-PU(I-P)\in\mathcal K(H).
\]

Therefore the shifted off-diagonal obstruction has the same Calkin class. If the actual semilocal prolate projection repairs the finite-place shifted sector, it must change the projection at the essential level, not merely by finite-dimensional or compact correction.

## Semilocal repair equation

For finite-place shifts

\[
\ell=\sum_{p\in S_f}k_p\log p,
\]

the true semilocal prolate projection would have to satisfy

\[
[P_S][\tau_\ell](1-[P_S])=0
\]

in the Calkin algebra, for all nonzero finite-place Bohr shifts \(\ell\).

## Literature status

The semilocal Connes--Consani--Moscovici paper provides the right setting: semilocal Hardy--Titchmarsh transforms, local Euler-factor measures, an even cyclic pair, and a Hilbertian isomorphism \(\theta_S\) connecting semilocal Sonin spaces. But it does not yet supply the Calkin-level repair theorem needed here: the computation of semilocal Jacobi coefficients is deferred, and a further semilocal/metaplectic candidate is announced as future work.

## Verdict

\[
\boxed{\text{semilocal prolate repair is not certified yet.}}
\]

The compactness shortcut remains possible only if the genuine semilocal prolate projection performs essential, noncompact principal cancellation of the shifted boundary-packet sector.

## Consequence

There are now two serious routes:

1. prove the actual semilocal prolate projection satisfies the Calkin repair equation;
2. accept that the noncompact shifted sector survives and absorb it with the Hecke/Dirichlet source ladder.

Compact or finite-rank correction is ruled out.

## Layman interpretation

The raw prime shift creates infinitely many boundary-packet directions that do not shrink away.

A finite-dimensional correction cannot fix infinitely many surviving directions. A compact correction also cannot erase the essential obstruction.

So the semilocal prolate operator must either perform a genuinely new infinite-dimensional repair, or we must use the Hecke/Dirichlet source ladder to pay for the surviving sector.
