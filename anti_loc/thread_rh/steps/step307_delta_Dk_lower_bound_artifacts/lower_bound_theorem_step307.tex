\section*{Step 307: Attempted Lower Bound for \texorpdfstring{$|\delta_{D,k}(\rho_1,G_\star)|$}{delta}}

Let
\[
  M(G_\star)(z)=\int_0^\infty G_\star(t)t^{-z}\,dt,\qquad
  h(z)=\zeta(z)M(G_\star)(z),
\]
with the inherited Branch C convention \(t^{-z}\).  The coefficients of
\(G_\star\) enforce
\[
  M(G_\star)(1)=\int G_\star(t)t^{-1}\,dt=0
\]
to numerical precision, so the pole of \(\zeta\) at \(z=1\) is cancelled.
Also \(M(G_\star)(\rho_1)\neq 0\), so \(h\) has a simple zero at \(\rho_1\).

\subsection*{Finite-type route}

The requested route would use a finite order-one type \(\tau_h\) and the
Cauchy--Hadamard/Stirling heuristic
\[
  |h^{(k)}(\rho_1)|=k!|c_k|\approx \sqrt{2\pi k}\,\tau_h^k.
\]
This route is blocked.  Although \(M(G_\star)\) is an entire transform of a
compactly supported \(C^\infty\) function with exponential indicator bounded
by \(\log 3.7\), the zeta factor is not finite type in the order-one sense.
The functional equation gives, on left-half-plane directions away from trivial
zeros,
\[
  \log|\zeta(-R+i\eta)| = R\log R + O(R),
\]
so \(\log|\zeta(z)|/|z|\to\infty\) along such rays.  After the removable pole
at \(1\) is cancelled, \(h\) remains an entire function of order one but
infinite type by this standard estimate.

\subsection*{Lower-bound obstruction}

Even if a finite type were available, growth/type data alone do not imply a
coefficient lower bound of the form
\[
  |h^{(k)}(\rho_1)|\ge A_0 b_0^k k^{\alpha_0}\qquad(k\ge k_0).
\]
Cauchy estimates are upper bounds.  Jensen, Phragmen--Lindelöf, and
Cauchy--Hadamard control maximal growth or limsup coefficient envelopes; they
do not prevent cancellations or lacunary coefficient patterns for a specific
Taylor expansion about \(\rho_1\).

\subsection*{Conclusion}

No theorem-grade lower bound with explicit constants
\((A_0,b_0,\alpha_0,k_0)\) is derived from the stated standard entire-function
inputs.  The certified values support strong empirical growth, but converting
them into a lower bound for all large \(k\) requires additional structure:
for example a signed/phase-controlled asymptotic for the Cauchy-circle Fourier
coefficient, not merely order/type.
