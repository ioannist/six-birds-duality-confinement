# Step 127 nonclaim boundary

Step 127 does not claim that any existing theorem already proves the residual source lower frame.

It only maps existing theorem platforms to the form required by the framework.

Not accepted:

1. Scalar moment lower bounds for one selected mollifier.
2. Large-sieve upper bounds.
3. Average positivity without a lower eigenvalue bound.
4. A coefficient asymptotic whose error is not uniform in the coefficient vector.
5. A finite conductor-window result without completed tail/exhaustivity promotion.
6. Target-selected character weights.
7. Zero-simplicity assumptions.
8. Collapse into de Branges/RKHS shift positivity.

Accepted target:

\[
G_{q,w}\succeq \gamma_{q,w}H_N
\]

uniformly on the residual coefficient class, followed by

\[
R_N^*G_{q,w}R_N\succeq \gamma_{q,w}c_{\rm hyb,N}G_{R,N}.
\]
