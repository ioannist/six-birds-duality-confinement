\section*{Operator Chain}

Burnol 2002 gives the projected de Branges kernel
\[
K_a^\Gamma(s,w)=\frac{E_\lambda(s)E_\lambda(w)-E_\lambda(1-s)E_\lambda(1-w)}{s+w-1}.
\]
Step 153 gives
\[
U_\infty(J_a^*Y^a_{w,k})=
T_a^*\partial_{\overline w}^kK_a^\Gamma(\cdot,w),
\]
and Step 152/153 gives
\[
T_a=M_\Gamma J_aU_\infty^{-1},\qquad
T_a^*=U_\infty J_a^*M_\Gamma^*.
\]
Thus
\[
\kappa_i(\tau)=T_{1/2}^*K_{1/2}^{\Gamma}(\cdot,\rho_i)(1/2+i\tau).
\]
The inherited records do not imply
\[
T_{1/2}^*K_{1/2}^{\Gamma}(\cdot,\rho_i)(1/2+i\tau)
=K_{1/2}^{\Gamma}(1/2+i\tau,\rho_i).
\]
That equality is an extra transport theorem, not a consequence of Burnol's
kernel formula alone.
