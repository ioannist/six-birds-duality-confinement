## Design-Class Declaration

The **constitutive-closure design space at the L-function substrate** is the class of finite typed packages on a declared L-function scope, usually a finite zeta zero-strip, whose structural shape conforms to the needles layer-dissolving framework and whose audit content may be located anywhere in the package.

A grammar belongs to this local design space iff its package has:

1. a predictive native currency component on the L-function substrate;
2. an adequacy residual obtained by the Schur-complement interface of needles.tex;
3. a dissolving currency, or analog, whose Loewner/Capacity bound is the closure conclusion;
4. optionally a separate audit-currency typed component, or alternatively an audit-implicit-in-conclusion semantics.

The class includes the four-component `G_constitutive_closure` packages `P=(M,A,R,T)` of steps 441-443 and the three-component `G_closure_only_no_audit` package `P'=(K^L,Xi,[K^D+S])` of step 445. ## Structural Argument

The evidence separates the local design class into two exhaustive audit-content placements:

1. **Audit as a typed component**. Steps 441-443 tested three structurally distinct mechanisms: height prediction, de Branges/kernel positivity, and Weil explicit-formula positivity. In each case the concrete branch was RH-distinguishing only by importing arithmetic content: zero heights, the target function `xi`, or prime/von-Mangoldt data. The formal branch avoided smuggling only by losing numerical or order-theoretic closure content.

2. **Audit as closure semantics**. Step 445 removed the separate audit component and used the Capacity-bound conclusion as the audit. The same tension re-fired at the semantic locus: formal Loewner and Schur numerics survived, but interpreting `K^D <= Theta^D` as zero-localization required an external audit or RH-equivalent `Theta^D` semantics.

Thus the tension is invariant under component relocation. It is not a defect of a particular audit-currency location. In this local design class, any concrete RH-distinguishing audit content must be sourced from arithmetic data at some locus, while any purely formal content lacks the numerical adequacy or Capacity semantics needed for closure.

## Conclusion: Local Mode B Saturation

`G_constitutive_closure` plus its closure-only successor are locally saturated **at the L-function substrate**. This is a bounded Mode B saturation result relative to the declared design class. It is **not global Mode B exhaustion** and it is **not** a proof of RH or RH-on-scope.

Further carrier attempts inside this design class are expected to reproduce the same smuggle-or-insufficient pattern at shifted loci unless they change the design class itself.
