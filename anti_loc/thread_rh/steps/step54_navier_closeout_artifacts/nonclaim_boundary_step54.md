# Nonclaim Boundary for the Navier Adequacy Closeout

Step 54 does not prove Navier-Stokes regularity.

It does not claim that energy or enstrophy exclude blow-up.

It does not claim that parabolic smoothing alone closes the blow-up blind spot.

It does not simulate Six Birds or a multi-layer Navier carrier.

It only states the formed-layer membrane obligation:

- declare the native flow probes,
- declare the layer-dissolving blow-up probes,
- compute or bound the adequacy residual Xi_C(D|L),
- prove predictive transport and source-admission records,
- pass the all-six audit gate.

A restricted regularity class may receive a scoped membrane claim, but only with an explicit nonclaim that the full Navier problem has not been solved.
