# Step 73: Weil Positivity versus the Douglas Feature Gate

This step compares the framework's central RH obligation

\[
\mathsf A_Z \preceq \mathsf K_{\mathrm{cmp}}
\]

with classical Weil positivity.

## Main conclusion

Full-domain Weil positivity is equivalent to the Douglas gate **only after** the Weil form has been identified with

\[
y\mapsto \langle y,(\mathsf K_{\mathrm{cmp}}-\mathsf A_Z)y\rangle
\]

on the exact completed anti-invariant response space.

Then:

\[
\mathsf A_Z\preceq\mathsf K_{\mathrm{cmp}}
\iff
\langle y,(\mathsf K_{\mathrm{cmp}}-\mathsf A_Z)y\rangle\ge0\ \forall y
\iff
V_Z=T W_{\mathrm{cmp}},\ \|T\|\le1.
\]

## The real gap

The gap is not the formal implication from positivity to domination. The gap is earning:

1. the exact anti-invariant response space;
2. the zero-side readout \(V_Z\);
3. the completed carrier feature map \(W_{\mathrm{cmp}}\);
4. equality of the Weil form with \(\mathsf K_{\mathrm{cmp}}-\mathsf A_Z\);
5. closure from test functions to the completed response space;
6. positive feature decomposition of prime/gamma/pole/tail pieces;
7. no-smuggling of zero-side data into the carrier features.

## Key warning

Classical signed explicit formula is not automatically a positive feature map. Trace equality, scalar positivity, and test-subspace positivity do not imply Loewner domination.

## Status

Weil positivity is the closest known analytic-number-theoretic shape to O1, but O1 requires an operator-valued, positive-feature, Douglas-contractivity upgrade.
