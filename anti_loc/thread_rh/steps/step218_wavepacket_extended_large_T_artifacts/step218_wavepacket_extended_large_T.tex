\section*{Step 218: Large-\(T\) Wavepacket Extension}

\subsection*{Construction}

We extend the Step 217 wavepacket test to
\[
T\in\{300,1000,3000,10000\}.
\]
For each \(T\), define
\[
u_T(\tau)=Z_T^{-1/2}\exp\!\left(-\frac{(\tau-T)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)
\mathbf 1_{\{|\tau-T|<3\sigma\}},
\qquad \sigma=1.
\]
The tested operator is
\[
C_\ell P_\infty=(I-P_\infty)M_{m_\ell}P_\infty,\qquad \ell=\log 2.
\]

The numerical method uses a moving local Gauss--Legendre window centered at \(T\), with half-width \(80\).  This resolves the width-\(1\) packet even at \(T=10000\).  The sinc part of \(P_\infty\) is translation invariant in local differences, while the finite PSWF correction is evaluated at the actual large ordinates.

\subsection*{Primary Values}

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
T & \|C_\ell u_T\|\\
\hline
300 & 0.2638416044\\
1000 & 0.2638455893\\
3000 & 0.2638458640\\
10000 & 0.2638458688
\end{array}
\]

The drift from \(T=300\) to \(T=10000\) is only
\[
4.2644\cdot 10^{-6},
\]
and the ratio is
\[
\|C_\ell u_{10000}\|/\|C_\ell u_{300}\|=1.00001616.
\]
With conservative error \(7.5\cdot 10^{-2}\),
\[
\delta\ge 0.1888416044.
\]

\subsection*{Robustness}

The bound persists under \(\sigma=0.5\) and under \(\ell=\log 3\).  At \(T=10000\), changing the local half-window from \(40\) to \(120\) gives norms from \(0.262169\) to \(0.264677\), the same numerical band.

\subsection*{Verdict}

The large-\(T\) extension is flat at displayed precision:
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{V\_wavepacket\_large\_T\_constant}}.
\]
This strengthens the essential-norm diagnostic for \(C_\ell P_\infty\), but it does not decide the narrower \(C_\ell P_\eta\) Branch A question.
