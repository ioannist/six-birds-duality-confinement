\section{Setup}

We refine the wavepacket essential-norm function
\[
  \Phi(\sigma,\ell)=\lim_{T\to\infty}\|C_\ell u_T\|,
  \qquad C_\ell=(I-P_\infty)M_{m_\ell}P_\infty,
\]
using the inherited step 217--220 numerical model:
\[
  K_\infty^{op}=\delta-\frac{\sin(\tau-\tau')}{\pi(\tau-\tau')}
  -\sum_n \Psi_n(\tau)\overline{\Psi_n(\tau')}.
\]
The packet is the same 3\(\sigma\)-truncated Gaussian used in the prior wavepacket steps.

\section{Grid}

The grid has \(10\times 9=90\) points with \(T=10000\), 24 PSWF terms, and 100-digit mpmath constants:
\[
\sigma\in\{0.1,0.2,\ldots,1.0\},
\]
\[
\ell\in\{0.5,\log2,1,\log3,1.5,\log5,2,\log(10)/4,2.5\}.
\]
The grid maximum is
\[
  \Phi(0.4,2.0)=0.4898488467749268,
\]
consistent with the inherited step 220 refined value near \(0.4904766190\).

\section{Candidate Closed Form}

The best theorem-shaped candidate is the ideal high-pass/low-pass overlap for an untruncated Gaussian:
\[
\Phi_{\rm band}(\sigma,\ell)^2 =
\frac12\left[
\operatorname{erf}(\sigma(1+\ell))-\operatorname{erf}(\sigma a(\ell))
\right],
\]
where
\[
a(\ell)=
\begin{cases}
1, & 0\le \ell\le 2,\\
\ell-1, & \ell>2.
\end{cases}
\]
This gives the right qualitative shape and explains why the maximum occurs near \(\ell=2\), but it is not numerically exact for the inherited truncated/PSWF pipeline:
\[
\operatorname{RMSE}=3.5846804717\times 10^{-3}.
\]
Allowing a scale and effective \(\sigma\) improves only to
\[
\operatorname{RMSE}=3.4233392285\times 10^{-3}.
\]
