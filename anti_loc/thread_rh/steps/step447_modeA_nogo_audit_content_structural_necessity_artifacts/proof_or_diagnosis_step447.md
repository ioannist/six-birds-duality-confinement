## Branch Dichotomy Argument

Let `O` be the audit-content of a finite typed package `P` on the L-function substrate. By definition, `O` is the part that gives the package RH-distinguishing force: it bridges non-arithmetic primitive data to zero-localization on `Re(s)=1/2`.

### Concrete Branch

If `O` is concrete, it must evaluate to a numerical or typed fact distinguishing the zeta target from off-critical controls. In all demonstrated placements this requires one of:

- nontrivial zero heights (`Step 441`);
- the target `xi`/`E_xi` or its positivity (`Step 442`);
- prime/von-Mangoldt data or zero-side sums in Weil positivity (`Step 443`);
- an RH-equivalent interpretation of `Theta^D` / Capacity semantics (`Step 445`).

These are arithmetic or target-side imports and violate `C_arithmetic_independence` and `C_no_tautology`.

### Formal Branch

If `O` is merely formal, it avoids importing arithmetic content. But then it cannot supply the evaluated residual, positivity, or Capacity semantics needed for closure. It provides a name for the bridge rather than a bridge.

### Relocation

Step 445 is decisive for structural necessity: removing the separate audit component did not remove audit-content. The content reappeared in the semantics of the closure conclusion. Thus the obstruction follows the role, not the component label.

## Literature-Anchored Support

Conrey 2003 and Bombieri 2000 frame the Hilbert-Polya gap as the absence of an arithmetic-independent construction with pointwise zeta-zero force. Lagarias 2004 documents the de Branges obstruction: concrete `xi`-based structure imports the target, while abstract structure is insufficient. Weil 1952 gives an RH-equivalent positivity form whose concrete sides use zeros or primes. de Branges 1968 supplies the Hilbert-space-of-entire-functions setting whose positivity predicates must be proved rather than named.

NDO/SAU makes the same requirement syntactic: the bridge must return an audited descendant. It cannot silently insert the target answer.

## Diagnosis Boundary

The proof is theorem-grade only for the demonstrated covered placements. For an unrestricted universal theorem over every possible semantic placement, the proof is diagnosis-grade because no closed category of all audit-content placements has been supplied.
