# Step 80 nonclaim boundary

This step proves a positive feature representation for the normalized gamma/digamma difference symbol. It does not prove RH, the full Weil--Douglas bridge, or exact matching of the classical signed gamma term to the positive feature under a chosen explicit-formula convention.

The archimedean slot is a paired renormalized difference form. Its separated diagonal diverges and cannot be used as finite completion payment without an audit/renormalization record.

Finite numerical checks only verify identities and asymptotics; they are not evidence for RH.
