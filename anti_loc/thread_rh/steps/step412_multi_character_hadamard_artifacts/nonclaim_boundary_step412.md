# Nonclaim Boundary

This step does not prove RH, GRH, or Selberg-class universality.

The computation covers finite samples: 30 zeros for `L(s, chi_4)` and 30 zeros for one real quadratic character modulo 5. The aggregate also uses finite inherited results from ζ and `L(s, chi_3)`.

The close-pair-only Hadamard contribution is tested as an exception detector, not as a complete formula for `Re L''`.
