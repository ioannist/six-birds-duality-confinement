# Step 138 nonclaim boundary

This step does not prove RH and does not prove the BPRZ source lower frame on the completed residual carrier.

It formulates and finite-tests the threshold optimization gate

\[
\delta_{R,K,N}\le L_{R,K}+T_{R,K}\sigma_{K,N}.
\]

The finite plots are algebraic sanity checks on Boolean squarefree models. They are not evidence about the actual Burnol/Müntz residual seed unless the actual seed-tail estimate and fixed/exhaustive promotion are supplied.

The threshold pair \((R,K)\) must be declared by an upstream rule, or optimized over a declared finite admissible grid. It may not be selected after inspecting off-critical-zero outcomes.
