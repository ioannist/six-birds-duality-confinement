\section*{Step 243: Hecke H5 Auxiliary Explicit-Formula Records}

\subsection*{H5 Specification}

For a Hecke character \(\chi\) over a number field \(K\), H5 asks for a
lawful explicit-formula record for \(L(s,\chi)\). Schematically,
\[
\widetilde f(0)\cdot \mathrm{pole/trivial}
  - \sum_{\rho:L(\chi,\rho)=0}\widetilde f(\rho)
  + \widetilde f(1)\cdot \mathrm{dual\ pole/trivial}
  = \sum_v W_{v,\chi}(f).
\]
The record must include conductor, gamma factors, root number, pole terms,
primitive/imprimitive handling, local prime-power terms, zero-sum convergence,
test-function class, and tail/null records.

\subsection*{No-Go Discipline}

H5 cannot assume auxiliary GRH. It can record zeros and explicit-formula
terms, but it cannot assert all auxiliary zeros lie on the critical line.
Records for selected characters remain support-only without full character
coverage. The trivial character's zeta explicit formula is not a carrier
identity.

\subsection*{Literature Audit}

Weil's explicit formula supplies the general explicit-formula framework for
global \(L\)-functions. Tate's thesis and Iwasawa-Tate theory supply adelic
local factors and functional equations for Hecke \(L\)-functions.
Iwaniec-Kowalski gives modern explicit-formula normalization. Explicit
zero-estimate papers for Hecke \(L\)-functions show the formula in use with
conductors and local data.

These sources make H5 externally supplyable, but inherited records do not yet
contain the normalized character-family record table needed by the cascade.

\subsection*{Verdict}

\]
