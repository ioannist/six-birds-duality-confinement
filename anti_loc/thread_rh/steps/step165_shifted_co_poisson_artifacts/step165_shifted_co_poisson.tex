\section*{Split External Theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Step 165 shifted co-Poisson split obligation]
In the inherited Burnol/Sonine pulled-evaluator context, no audited source in
Steps 114--115, Steps 118--122, \texttt{adequacy.tex}, or \texttt{needles.tex}
supplies a theorem-grade shifted factorization
\[
  \Mell(Bu_{\ell,a})(s)=\zetaF(s)\alpha_{\ell,a,u}(s)
\]
with the typed \(\alpha\)-properties needed to close \(\XiBC\). Therefore the
shifted co-Poisson branch has status
\[
  \boxed{\texttt{split\_external\_theorem}}.
\]
The missing theorem splits into the following obligations:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.5em]
\item[H1] shifted packet and strip context;
\item[H2] shifted zeta-factorization existence;
\item[H3] \(\alpha\) holomorphy, growth, carrier, and support properties;
\item[H4] endpoint, pole, trivial-zero, and multiplicity admissibility;
\item[H5] completed exact implication to \(\XiBC\) closure and the active
  Calkin object.
\end{description}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Steps 114--115 identify the exact sufficient route: a shifted packet in the
Burnol carrier would be killed by all zero evaluators if its Mellin transform
had a zeta factor with admissible quotient \(\alpha\). The same records state
that the raw log shift does not itself create this factor; the route is
unearned. Steps 118--122 move to Burnol-to-Dirichlet readability, regularized
Muntz shadows, and angular-gap estimates. These records are useful for source
absorption and lawful shadow construction, but they do not assert the exact
shifted boundary-packet factorization. The foundational adequacy and needles
records supply the residual calculus and transfer discipline, not the missing
domain analytic factorization. Hence Verdict A and Verdict B are unsupported.
The warning about raw log shifts is also not a theorem that every admissible
shifted packet fails to factor; therefore Verdict D is unsupported. The
remaining supported status is the split external theorem above.
\end{proof}
