\section*{Step 257: Rudnick--Sarnak as Type II}

\paragraph{Carrier.}
For a family of principal or cuspidal automorphic \(L\)-functions, with zeros
\(\rho_j(\pi)=1/2+i\gamma_j(\pi)\), define the normalized \(n\)-level zero
statistic \(R_n^{\mathrm{family}}(f;T)\) for a Schwartz test function \(f\).
The residual is
\[
\Xi_{\mathrm{RS}}(f;T)
=\left|R_n^{\mathrm{family}}(f;T)-R_n^{\mathrm{GUE}}(f)\right|.
\]

\paragraph{Known theorem range.}
Rudnick--Sarnak prove \(n\)-level GUE correlations for principal
\(L\)-functions in restricted Fourier support.  This is a theorem-grade
restricted-support result, not the full unrestricted conjecture.

\paragraph{Subtype classification.}
Single-\(\pi\) \(n\)-level statistics are Type Ib: single-\(L\) zero
correlations.  Family or multi-\(\pi\) \(n\)-level statistics are Type II:
multi-\(L\) or family zero correlations.

\paragraph{Subtype matrix.}
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{subtype} & \text{meaning} & \text{instances}\\
\hline
\text{Ia} & \text{multi-}L\text{ coefficient correlations}
& \text{SOC, Selberg orthonormality}\\
\text{Ib} & \text{single-}L\text{ zero correlations}
& \text{Montgomery}\\
\text{II} & \text{multi-}L\text{/family zero correlations}
& \text{Rudnick--Sarnak}
\end{array}
\]

\paragraph{Verdict.}
\[
\mathrm{V\_rudnick\_sarnak\_type\_II\_instance}.
\]
The Selberg-Class Cross-Correlation Extension is now verified on four
Selberg instances and all three subtypes are populated.  No GRH or RH closure
is claimed.
