# CDK 1995 Extract -- Step 281

Source: Eduardo Cattani, Pierre Deligne, and Aroldo Kaplan, *On the locus of Hodge classes*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 8 (1995), 483-506.

Primary theorem:

> S(K) is an algebraic variety, finite over S.

Local corollary:

> The germ of analytic subvariety of S where u remains of type (0, 0), is algebraic.

Global corollary:

> The set of points in S where some determination of u is of type (0, 0), is an algebraic subvariety of S.

Secondary confirmation from Charles-Schnell:

> the locus of Hodge classes and the Hodge locus ... are countable unions of closed algebraic subsets

Voisin target distinction:

> The Hodge conjecture asserts that any rational Hodge class is a combination with rational coefficients of such classes.

Audit reading: CDK supplies algebraicity of a Hodge locus.  The Hodge 7-layer cascade needs cycle realization and cycle-class-map closure for a class on a fiber.  Locus algebraicity is adjacent but does not construct the cycle.
