\section*{Step 281: Hodge Score-2 Audit of Cattani--Deligne--Kaplan}

\paragraph{Target.}
The step tests whether the Cattani--Deligne--Kaplan theorem on Hodge loci supplies the Hodge cascade's cycle-realization and cycle-class-map closure.

\paragraph{Published theorem type.}
CDK prove algebraicity of a Hodge locus.  In their notation, Theorem 1.1 states that
\[
  S(K) \text{ is an algebraic variety, finite over } S.
\]
Corollary 1.2 says that the germ of the analytic subvariety where a fixed integral class remains of type \((0,0)\) is algebraic.  Corollary 1.3 globalizes this as an algebraic subvariety of \(S\).

\paragraph{Cascade need.}
The Hodge seven-layer CTMT chain requires cycle realization:
\[
  \text{Hodge class on a fiber} \in \operatorname{im}(\mathrm{cl}_{\mathrm{alg}}).
\]
Equivalently, it needs the cycle-class map to span the relevant rational Hodge classes in the codimension-2 setting under audit.

\paragraph{Gap.}
The implication
\[
  \text{algebraic Hodge locus} \Rightarrow \text{algebraic cycle realizing the class}
\]
is not a CDK theorem.  CDK identifies where the Hodge condition persists in a family; it does not construct cycles realizing all classes on a specific fiber.  Therefore the H-18R/H-3R/H-8R source-to-status rule does not promote this bridge to known closure.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
\[
  \boxed{V_{\mathrm{CDK\_Hodge\_bridge\_blocked}}}
\]
The CDK score-2 bridge downgrades to score-1.  The cross-track empirical downgrade pattern is now 5-of-5 across RH, BSD, and Hodge.
