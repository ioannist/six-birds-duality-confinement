# Step 209 Nonclaim Boundary

Step 209 does not prove RH.

Step 209 does not claim that `Z(t)` being real-valued is equivalent to RH.
That statement is false: real-valuedness follows from the zeta functional
equation and the definition of the Riemann-Siegel theta phase.

Step 209 classifies the RH-equivalent Z-carrier residual as the zero-count
defect `Delta_Z(T)=N(T)-N_0(T)`, not as `||Im Z||`.

Step 209 does not weaken any retained no-go and does not affect the precision
limitations found in the Branch B Burnol/Sonine numerical chain.
