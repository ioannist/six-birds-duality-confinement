\section*{Step 267: Burnol Transport-Sampling Derivation Attempt}

\paragraph{Verdict.}
\[
\mathrm{V\_burnol\_transport\_sampling\_blocked}.
\]
The cascade-needed finite transport-sampling theorem is not derivable from
Burnol 2002 and Burnol 2004 by algebraic specialization.

\paragraph{Burnol 2002 formulas.}
Theorem 4 gives the Sonine projection
\[
\pi_\lambda(f)=f-(1-D_\lambda)^{-1}(P_\lambda f-F_\lambda\mathcal F_+f)
-\mathcal F_+(1-D_\lambda)^{-1}(P_\lambda\mathcal F_+f-F_\lambda f),
\]
where \(F_\lambda=P_\lambda\mathcal F_+P_\lambda\) and
\(D_\lambda=F_\lambda^2\).

Equation (1) gives the de Branges kernel
\[
K(z_1,z_2)=
\frac{E(z_1)E(z_2)-E(1-z_1)E(1-z_2)}{z_1+z_2-1}.
\]
Theorem 8 gives
\[
\mathcal E_\lambda(w)=
\pi^{-w/2}\Gamma(w/2)\left(
\lambda^{1/2-w}+\frac{\sqrt{\lambda}}{2}
\int_\lambda^\infty
(\psi_+^\lambda(t)-\psi_-^\lambda(t))t^{-w}\,dt
\right).
\]

\paragraph{Burnol 2004 formulas.}
Section 6 uses
\[
M(f)(s)=\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\int_\lambda^\infty f(t)t^{-s}\,dt
\]
and evaluator vectors \(Z^\lambda_{w,k}\) satisfying
\[
[f,Z^\lambda_{w,k}]=M(f)^{(k)}(w).
\]
It also records the Fourier action
\[
\mathcal F_+(Z^\lambda_{w,k})=(-1)^k Z^\lambda_{1-w,k}.
\]
The zero-evaluator result is a closed-span statement:
\[
K_\lambda=
\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{Z^\lambda_{\rho,k}:0\leq k<m_\rho\}
\quad(\lambda\geq1).
\]

\paragraph{Derivation attempt.}
Applying zeta multiplication and evaluation gives
\[
\operatorname{Eval}_w(M_\zeta\pi_\lambda f)
=\zeta(w)M(\pi_\lambda f)(w).
\]
Using Burnol's evaluator vector,
\[
M(\pi_\lambda f)(w)=[\pi_\lambda f,Z^\lambda_{w,0}]
=[f,\pi_\lambda^\ast Z^\lambda_{w,0}]
\]
up to the selected bilinear/Hilbert convention.

\paragraph{Required missing step.}
The cascade needs a finite sampling identity of the form
\[
\pi_\lambda^\ast M_\zeta^\ast Z_w
=\sum_{i=1}^N c_i Z^\lambda_{\tau_i,0}.
\]
Burnol's formulas give the representing vector on the left, but not a finite
expansion in sampled evaluators.  The Section 6 theorem gives a closed infinite
span for zero evaluators, not finite interpolation or quadrature for arbitrary
transported evaluators.

\paragraph{Status.}
The Branch B transport-sampling terminus is not score-3.  After this audit it
is score-1 framework-internal unless a new finite transported-evaluator
expansion theorem is supplied.
