# Nonclaim Boundary for Step 62

The adequacy-aware transfer theorem does not claim:

1. A native membrane transfers to a layer-dissolving membrane without adequacy.
2. A coarsened native response preserves adequacy.
3. A public-shadow \(\Xi\) bound promotes to intrinsic adequacy.
4. Bridge residuals are harmless.
5. A finite-stage transferred membrane is predictive without summable defect control.
6. A raw substrate has no hidden dissolving probes.

The theorem claims only that a formed native membrane plus an audited \(\Xi\)-adequacy bound transfers across a faithful bridge, with explicit defect budgets.
