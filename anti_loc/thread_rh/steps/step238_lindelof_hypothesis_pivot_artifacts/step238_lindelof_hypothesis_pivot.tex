\section*{Step 238: Lindelof Hypothesis Carrier}

\subsection*{Carrier}

The Lindelof carrier is the critical-line growth residual
\[
\Xi_{\mathrm{Lind}} =
\limsup_{t\to\infty}
\frac{\log |\zeta(1/2+it)|}{\log t}.
\]
The closure condition is
\[
\Xi_{\mathrm{Lind}} = 0,
\]
equivalently,
\[
|\zeta(1/2+it)| = O_\varepsilon(t^\varepsilon)
\quad \text{for every } \varepsilon>0.
\]

\subsection*{Relation to RH}

The Riemann Hypothesis implies Lindelof by standard consequences of RH and Phragmen-Lindelof type growth control. The converse is not an accepted theorem. In the cascade, Lindelof is therefore a strictly weaker zeta sub-target: closing $\Xi_{\mathrm{Lind}}$ does not close the Riemann residual.

\subsection*{Subconvexity Status}

Lindelof remains open. The audited best pointwise subconvexity exponent is Bourgain's
\[
\zeta(1/2+it) \ll_\varepsilon t^{13/84+\varepsilon}.
\]
This is a genuine subconvexity improvement but not exponent zero.

\subsection*{Zero-Count and Density Forms}

Backlund's short-interval zero-count form is recorded as Lindelof-equivalent: for every $\sigma>1/2$, the number of zeros in
\[
T \leq \Im(s) \leq T+1,\qquad \sigma \leq \Re(s) \leq 1
\]
grows slower than $\log T$.

The broader density hypothesis belongs to the same Lindelof-family of zero-density statements. This step records it as Lindelof-family and Lindelof-implied in the audited sources, not as an RH-equivalent bridge.

\subsection*{Classification}

Under the Riemann-RH Dichotomy, Lindelof is outside scope because it is not RH-equivalent. Under the generalized zeta sub-conjecture Dichotomy, it is generalized CRCFT-TE within the Lindelof family: closing the residual is exactly the Lindelof hypothesis.

\]
