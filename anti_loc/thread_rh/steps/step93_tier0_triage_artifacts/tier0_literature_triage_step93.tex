\item Maintain the fixed/exhaustive zero-ledger squeeze gate; finite spectral triples are support-only unless determinant/zero-ledger convergence is proved.

\section*{Conrey--Li survival test}
Any proposed positivity or feature package must pass the following test:
\begin{quote}
It must not imply the de Branges positivity conditions shown false by Conrey--Li for the Hilbert spaces naturally associated to $\xi(s)$ or $1/\xi(s)$.
\end{quote}
In framework language, the proposed carrier must not be a public-shadow RKHS positivity condition. It must either be semilocal/adelic, paired-feature, quotient-corrected, source-coercive, or explicitly scoped.

\section*{Yakaboylu bridge test}
Yakaboylu's positive intertwiner has the right duality-confinement shape:
\[
W R = R^\dagger W,\qquad W\ge0\quad\Rightarrow\quad \rho=1-\bar\rho.
\]
But in our framework the decisive questions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Is $W$ constructed upstream of the zero-confinement test, or is it zero-ledger selected?
\item Does the compression to the zero subspace already assume the zero ledger we need to prove exhaustive?
\item Can the simplicity assumption be represented as a lawful scoped hypothesis rather than a hidden gate?
\item Can the positive intertwiner be expressed as a Douglas bridge or source-frame budget on a carrier not indexed by the target conclusion?
\end{enumerate}
