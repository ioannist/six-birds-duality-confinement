\section{Burnol Kernel Jet}

Burnol's completed reproducing kernel is recorded as
\[
K_a^\Gamma(z,w)=
\frac{E_\lambda(z)\overline{E_\lambda(w)}
-E_\lambda(1-z)\overline{E_\lambda(1-w)}}{z+w-1}.
\]
Burnol's Theorem 8 formula is
\[
E_\lambda(w)=\pi^{-w/2}\Gamma(w/2)
\left[
\lambda^{1/2-w}
\frac{\sqrt{\lambda}}2
\int_\lambda^\infty (\psi_+^\lambda(t)-\psi_-^\lambda(t))t^{-w}\,dt
\right].
\]
Writing \(E=P B\), its derivative is
\[
E'(w)=P'(w)B(w)+P(w)B'(w),
\]
\[
\frac{P'(w)}{P(w)}=-\frac12\log\pi+\frac12\psi(w/2),
\]
\[
B'(w)=-\log(\lambda)\lambda^{1/2-w}
-\frac{\sqrt\lambda}{2}
\int_\lambda^\infty
(\psi_+^\lambda-\psi_-^\lambda)(t)t^{-w}\log t\,dt.
\]
Therefore, under the inherited \(z+w-1\) denominator convention,
\[
\partial_{\bar w}K_a^\Gamma(z,w)
=
\frac{
E(z)\overline{E'(w)}
+E(1-z)\overline{E'(1-w)}
}{z+w-1}.
\]
