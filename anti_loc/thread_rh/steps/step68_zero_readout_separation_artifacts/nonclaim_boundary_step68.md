# Nonclaim boundary for Step 68

Step 68 does not prove RH.

It proves only that, once a completed anti-invariant zero ledger has been squeezed to zero, a separating anti-invariant readout turns that ledger collapse into fixed-locus confinement.

It does not prove:

- the completed explicit-formula domination square;
- anti-invariant budget collapse;
- zero-ledger exhaustivity;
- finite-to-continuum tail control;
- RH for the zeta function.

A readout that controls only invariant/self-dual data has status `invariant_shadow_only`.

A finite zero window with no tail/exhaustivity bridge has status `moving_ledger_support_only`.

A separating readout on a partial zero ledger gives only `visible_only` confinement.
