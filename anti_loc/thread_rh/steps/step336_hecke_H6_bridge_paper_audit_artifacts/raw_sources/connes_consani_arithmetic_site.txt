Abstract
We show that the non-commutative geometric approach to the Riemann zeta function has an algebraic geometric
̂×
incarnation: the “Arithmetic Site”. This site involves the tropical semiring N̄ viewed as a sheaf on the topos N
dual to the multiplicative semigroup of positive integers. We realize the Frobenius correspondences in the square
of the “Arithmetic Site”.
