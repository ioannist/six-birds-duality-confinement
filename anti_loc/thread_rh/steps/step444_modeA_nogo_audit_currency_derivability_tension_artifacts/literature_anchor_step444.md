# Step 444 Literature Anchor

## Conrey 2003

Conrey, "The Riemann Hypothesis", Notices AMS 50(3), surveys the Hilbert-Polya program and the structural difficulty of finding a Hermitian operator whose spectrum is the zeta-zero ordinates. In the framing used here: known constructions either import arithmetic structure or fail to distinguish the zeta spectrum.

## Bombieri 2000

Bombieri's Clay Millennium statement, "The Riemann Hypothesis", lists Hilbert-Polya as a central hope but emphasizes that no construction has produced the zeta-zero spectrum from arithmetic-independent primitives.

## Lagarias 2004

Lagarias, "Hilbert spaces of entire functions and the Riemann hypothesis", analyzes de Branges-type Hilbert spaces of entire functions and the obstruction history around applying them to zeta. In this step's language: the structure function is either concretely tied to xi or too abstract to prove the required positivity.

## Weil 1952 and Yoshida 1992

Weil's 1952 explicit formula gives the positivity criterion whose zero side and prime side are equivalent under the formula. Yoshida 1992 gives a clean proof of the same explicit-formula structure. The positivity form is RH-equivalent, but concrete evaluation uses zero ordinates or prime/von-Mangoldt data.
