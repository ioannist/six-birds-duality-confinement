# Step 451 Schur-Complement Route

## Objective

Attempt to derive completed domination records for `Xi_SDTC_domination_records` from adequacy.tex Schur calculus plus needles.tex section 5, using no substrate-specific analytic-number-theory theorem as accepted input.

Required target:

```text
exists B_n such that A_Z(zeta) <= B_n, B_n >= 0, and tr B_n -> 0.
```

## Candidate construction

For stage `n`, take:

- `L_n`: the lawful current trace-observable subspace of `Sel^!_{zeta,tr}` generated by completed gamma-factor/conductor/functional-equation records visible at level `n`.
- `D_n`: the dissolving anti-invariant zero-ledger probes whose readout is `psi_-(rho)=Re(rho)-1/2`, restricted to the stage `n` comparison quotient.
- Gram blocks `K_LL,n`, `K_DL,n`, `K_DD,n` built from the instrument-relative pairings on these finite probe families.

Adequacy.tex gives the residual

```text
Xi_n = K_DD,n - K_DL,n K_LL,n^dagger K_LD,n.
```

By `def:xi:adequacy-residual` and `thm:xi:projection-identity`,

```text
Xi_n = T_D,n (I-P_L,n) T_D,n^* >= 0.
```

This supplies positivity and the correct Schur geometry. It also supplies the formal candidate shape:

```text
B_n = Phi_n^* Xi_n Phi_n + E_tail,n
```

where `Phi_n` would be the Douglas bridge sending the global anti-invariant ledger into the stage-`n` dissolving coordinates and `E_tail,n` would account for omitted zero-ledger mass.

## Where the route stalls

The Schur calculus does **not** by itself prove either of the two load-bearing facts:

1. `A_Z(zeta) <= Phi_n^* Xi_n Phi_n + E_tail,n` for a contraction/factorization attached to `Sel^!_{zeta,tr}`.
2. `tr(Phi_n^* Xi_n Phi_n + E_tail,n) -> 0`.

The first is a Douglas-domination content claim; the second is a trace-decay claim. `thm:main:douglas-domination` tells what a domination record means once the factorization exists, but it does not produce the factorization or its decay. Adequacy.tex likewise states exact adequacy as a condition, not as a generic theorem.

## Named missing input

`Xi_SDTC_trace_decay_input`: a substrate-specific trace-decay theorem for the completed zeta anti-invariant ledger, strong enough to show the Schur residual and tail terms vanish in trace.

## Route verdict

Partial only. The route constructs positive residuals and a lawful candidate budget shape, but framework primitives alone do not provide `tr B_n -> 0`.
