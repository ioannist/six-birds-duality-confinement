# Step 213 Results Summary

## Carrier Declaration

Bagchi universality is modeled as the vertical-translation orbit
\[
  \tau \mapsto \zeta(\cdot+i\tau)
\]
in the compact-open topology on compact sets \(K\subset\{1/2<\Re(s)<1\}\).
For admissible \(K\) and nonvanishing holomorphic target \(f\), define
\[
  \Xi_{\rm Bagchi}(K,f)
  =\inf_\tau \sup_{s\in K}|\zeta(s+i\tau)-f(s)|.
\]

Bagchi/Voronin universality closes this residual unconditionally, with
positive lower density of good translates.

## CRE Audit

The carrier is not CRE. Its closure is a value-distribution theorem, not RH.
Since the theorem holds unconditionally, it cannot be logically equivalent to
RH.

## Classification

`V_bagchi_outside_dichotomy`.

This is a statistical/dynamical carrier analogous to the RMT carrier from
Step 188. It is not a non-CRE RH-analog with a zero-location theorem like
Selberg or Weil-Deligne; it is a zeta value-distribution theorem.

## Derivative Carrier Check

Joint universality and Selberg-class variants may interact with broader
statistical questions about L-functions, but no inherited RH-equivalent
derivative residual is exposed. A derivative CRE carrier would need a separate
explicit closure condition equivalent to zero-location; Bagchi universality
itself does not supply one.

## Dichotomy Implication

Bagchi refines the Dichotomy domain: not every theorem about ζ is an RH-closure
carrier. It does not refute coverage because it lies outside the in-scope
carrier class.
