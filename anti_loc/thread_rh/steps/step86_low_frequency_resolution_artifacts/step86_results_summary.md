# Step 86: Low-Frequency Resolution Gate for the Paired Weil Carrier

This step sharpens the RH construction target after the paired CND/shift-feature gate.

A paired shift feature has symbol

\[
\Psi(\xi)=c\xi^2+2\int(1-\cos(a\xi))\,\mu(da),\qquad c\ge0,\ \mu\ge0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\Psi(0)=0.
\]

So paired positivity is not coercivity. The completed carrier may be positive while still having a legal low-frequency soft mode.

## Main result

If near a legal zero

\[
\Psi(\xi)\gtrsim \rho^{2r},
\]

and a probe/readout has

\[
|\widehat g(\xi)|\lesssim \rho^s,
\]

then the local capacity is finite in dimension \(d\) when

\[
\boxed{s>r-\frac d2.}
\]

For the one-dimensional shift-zero case \(r=1,d=1\), this is

\[
\boxed{s>\frac12.}
\]

## Four lawful low-frequency records

To use a paired Weil carrier as an RH membrane, one must supply one of:

1. **Quotient/gap record**: restrict or quotient away the legal low mode with an explicit nonclaim/tail record.
2. **Cancellation record**: prove the zero-side readout vanishes fast enough at the legal zero.
3. **Source-coercivity record**: prove strict-extension source growth charges the whole low-frequency anti-invariant sector.
4. **Defect/nonclaim record**: keep the unresolved low-frequency piece as an explicit budget or scoped nonclaim.

## RH meaning

Prime and gamma paired features are plausible positive carrier slots. But because paired shift/CND carriers naturally vanish at low frequency, the RH route still needs a legal-zero resolution record.

Thus the active RH obstruction is no longer only:

\[
\text{construct positive paired features}.
\]

It is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{construct positive paired features and resolve their low-frequency legal zero.}
}
\]

## Nonclaim

This step does not prove RH, does not prove a spectral gap, and does not prove low-frequency cancellation for the zeta zero-side readout. It states the exact low-frequency gate that any such proof must pass.
