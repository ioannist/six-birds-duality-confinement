# Step 90: Candidate zeta source-family audit

\[
F_n=\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_n}\lambda_sQ_s^*\Theta_s^{-1}Q_s\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1},\qquad \Lambda_n\to\infty.

If accepted, this gate gives

\[

## Main verdict

- Prime-by-prime paired shifts are native and positive, but not a full-sector source frame by themselves because the paired symbol vanishes at zero frequency and finite prime sets have near-aliasing/soft directions.
- Dirichlet/Hecke character ladders are the most natural root-composite source candidate, but require a conductor-complete lower-frame record and a bridge from character sectors to zeta off-critical displacement.
- Selberg/trace-formula and automorphic/representation source families may provide a true carrier-level frame, but only after exact carrier and continuous-spectrum/tail/null records are built.
- Functional-equation parity is necessary for separation but does not price the anti-invariant sector.
- Public trace/scalar sources are support-only and cannot certify full anti-invariant coercivity.

## Counter-gates

Partial character coverage leaves hidden anti-invariant directions. Bounded source strength gives only finite nonzero budget. Moving finite windows are support-only unless they are tied to a fixed/exhaustive response ledger.

## Bottom line

The viable source route is not “more prime terms” in isolation. It is a full anti-invariant character/source lower frame, declared upstream, bridged into the completed zeta carrier, with \(\Lambda_n\to\infty\).
