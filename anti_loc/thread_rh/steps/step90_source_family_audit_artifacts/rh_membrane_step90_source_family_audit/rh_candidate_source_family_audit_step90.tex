\section{Purpose}
The paired Weil carrier has a low-frequency legal zero.  Steps 85--89 showed that paired positivity is not enough for the RH root-composite route: one still needs a lawful record that prices the anti-invariant sector.  This note audits candidate zeta source families against the source-coercivity gate
\[
F_n=\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_n}\lambda_s Q_s^*\Theta_s^{-1}Q_s
\succeq
\Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1},
\qquad
\Lambda_n\to\infty.
\]
If this gate is accepted, then the anti-invariant currency collapses:
\[
K_n^- = L^- C_n^\dagger (L^-)^* \preceq \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_0^-.
\]
The audit question is not whether a source family is mathematically interesting; it is whether it is upstream-visible, full-sector, divergent, fixed/exhaustive, and connected to the completed zeta carrier.

\section{Source-frame gate}
\begin{definition}[Source record]
A source record is a tuple
\[
s=(Q_s,\Theta_s,\lambda_s,D_s)
\]
where \(Q_s:Y^-\to Z_s\) is an upstream-visible anti-invariant character or source readout, \(\Theta_s\succ0\) is its source budget, \(\lambda_s>0\) is its strength, and the audit contribution \(D_s\succeq0\) obeys
\[
D_s\succeq \lambda_s(Q_sL^-)^*\Theta_s^{-1}(Q_sL^-).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Source frame]
The response-side source frame at stage \(n\) is
\[
F_n=\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_n}\lambda_s Q_s^*\Theta_s^{-1}Q_s.
\]
It is accepted at scale \(\Lambda_n\) if
\[
F_n\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}
\]
on the full declared anti-invariant response sector \(Y^-\).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Source-frame collapse]
Assume the exact packaged audit after adding accepted source records satisfies
\[
C_n\succeq \sum_{s\in\mathcal S_n}D_s.
\]
If the source frame obeys
\[
F_n\succeq \Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1},
\]
then
\[
K_n^-=L^-C_n^\dagger(L^-)^*\preceq \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_0^-.
\]
Consequently, if \(\Lambda_n\to\infty\), the anti-invariant budget collapses in Loewner order.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The source condition implies
\[
C_n\succeq \sum_s \lambda_s (Q_sL^-)^*\Theta_s^{-1}(Q_sL^-)
= (L^-)^*F_nL^-.
\]
By the lower-frame hypothesis,
\[
C_n\succeq \Lambda_n (L^-)^*(\Theta_0^-)^{-1}L^-.
\]
The standard coercive-capacity lemma gives
\[
L^-C_n^\dagger(L^-)^*\preceq \Lambda_n^{-1}\Theta_0^-.
\]
\end{proof}

\section{Candidate families}
\subsection{Prime-by-prime paired shifts}
The prime-power shift features are upstream-visible and positive:
\[
\Psi_{{\rm pr},L}(\xi)=2\sum_n w_{n,L}(1-\cos(\xi\log n)),\qquad w_{n,L}\ge0.
\]
They are excellent paired carrier features.  They do not by themselves give a full anti-invariant lower frame because \(\Psi_{{\rm pr},L}(0)=0\), and finite prime sets have near-aliasing directions.  Infinite prime sources may contribute to source growth, but they still require a low-frequency or fixed/exhaustive-tail record.

\subsection{Dirichlet/Hecke character ladders}
Dirichlet and Hecke characters are the most natural root-composite source family.  In finite cyclic models, the complete nontrivial character family gives a lower frame on the mean-zero sector, whereas incomplete character subsets leave hidden directions.  Thus a character ladder is plausible only if the conductor/character system is declared upstream and the bridge from character sectors to the zeta anti-invariant displacement is proved.

\subsection{Trace formula and automorphic sources}
Trace-formula or automorphic/representation-theoretic source families are carrier-level candidates.  They may provide a Plancherel-style full-sector frame if the carrier is exact and continuous spectrum, tails, and null modes are audited.  They are analytically heavy and cannot be inserted as external target-selected sources.

\subsection{Functional-equation parity}
The functional equation supplies the anti-linear symmetry and the separating readout.  It does not price the anti-invariant sector.  Thus it is necessary for RH's fixed-locus formulation but insufficient for source coercivity.

\section{No-go alternatives}
\begin{proposition}[Partial character coverage fails]
If there is \(0\ne y\in Y^-\) with \(Q_sy=0\) for all source readouts \(s\), then \(F_ny=0\) for all \(n\).  Hence no lower frame with \(\Lambda_n>0\) holds on the full sector.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Bounded source strength is not collapse]
If \(\sup_n\Lambda_n<\infty\), then the source-frame theorem gives only a nonzero budget.  It may support a scoped estimate but cannot yield exact fixed-locus confinement by budget collapse.
\end{proposition}

\begin{warning}[Moving source windows]
If \(F_n\) acts only on a moving finite source window and there is no fixed or exhaustive response ledger, then the status is \texttt{moving\_window\_support\_only}.  This is support evidence, not a completed source ladder.
\end{warning}

\section{Conclusion}
The strongest non-gating RH source route is a character/source lower frame on the full anti-invariant response sector:
\[
F_n\succeq\Lambda_n(\Theta_0^-)^{-1},\qquad \Lambda_n\to\infty.

The most plausible source families are Dirichlet/Hecke or broader automorphic character ladders, but they require a concrete bridge into the completed zeta carrier. Prime-by-prime shifts remain crucial paired carrier features, not by themselves a full source-coercivity proof.
