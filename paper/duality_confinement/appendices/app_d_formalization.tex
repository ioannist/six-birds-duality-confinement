\subsection*{Typed positive-cone encoding}

The formalization uses a mathlib-free typed positive cone as the
common substrate for \(\AX\), the bounds \(B_n\), the carrier currency
\(\Kminus\), and the tail terms \(T_n\).  This cone records exactly
the primitives used by the arguments in the paper: an order
\(\preceq\), a trace functional \(\trace\), trace monotonicity under
the order, and the positivity-to-zero principle that a positive
element with zero trace is the zero element.  The trace identity for
the anti-invariant ledger is encoded at this level: the Gram-type
construction of \(\AX\) is bundled with the assertion that
\(\trace\AX\) is the integral of \(\|\psim(x)\|^2\).

This abstraction is narrower than a full Hilbert-space operator
theory; it is the formal substrate for the membrane argument
because the proof of the master theorem uses only order, trace
monotonicity, scalar squeezing, and positivity-to-zero, and it does
not formalize trace-class operators, Hilbert-Schmidt maps, Schatten
ideals, or trace-norm topology.  Realizing the same theorem inside the
full positive trace-class cone remains an operator-theoretic extension
of the present work, not a hidden claim of the Lean development.

\subsection*{Representation notes}

\paragraph{Douglas factorization.}
The domination section uses the classical Douglas factorization as
operator-theoretic motivation.  In the mathlib-free typed-cone Lean
encoding, however, the factorization theorem is represented by a
typed \texttt{DouglasData} carrier.  The carrier records the
operator-square-root presentation \(A=V^*V\), \(K=W^*W\), the
domination relation \(A\preceq K\), and the equivalent contractive
factor witness \(T\) satisfying \(V=TW\).  Downstream theorems read
the implication between domination and factorization from this typed
carrier.

Thus the paper's disclosure is: Lean derives Douglas factorization at
the typed-cone level.  This is faithful to the classical Douglas
factorization (Douglas 1966) as the underlying mathematical content,
but it is a typed-cone packaging rather than a Lean development of the
full Hilbert-space operator theorem.

\paragraph{AM--GM in the scalar trace budget.}
The optimized scalar trace budget depends on the elementary
optimization
\[
  \inf_{t>0}\bigl((1+t)a+(1+t^{-1})b\bigr)
    = \bigl(\sqrt a+\sqrt b\bigr)^2 .
\]
In ordinary real arithmetic this follows from the AM--GM inequality,
or equivalently from non-negativity of
\((\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^2\).  The abstract scalar encoding used here is
weaker: it provides the scalar interface needed by the trace-budget
statement but does not supply enough ordered real arithmetic to derive
AM--GM substantively.

Accordingly, the Lean-side statement uses an explicit
\texttt{am\_gm\_optimization} hypothesis.  The optimized scalar trace
budget is tracked by the formalization harness as a typed projection
over that hypothesis; it is not advertised as a substantive Lean
derivation of AM--GM.  A future strengthening would add sufficient
typed real-arithmetic structure to express square roots,
non-negativity of squares, and the derivation of
\((\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^2\geq0\).

\paragraph{Anti-invariant readout as typed field.}
The body definition of the anti-invariant readout is
\(\psim(x)=\Pminus\psi(x)\), and equivariance of \(\psi\) under the
involution then forces \(\psim(Jx)=-\psim(x)\).  In the
mathlib-free typed-cone encoding the response-side projector
\(\Pminus\) is not represented, so \(\psim\) is realized in Lean as
an independent typed field of the separating-readout carrier and of
the anti-invariant-ledger carrier.  The two occurrences are linked
by a coherence hypothesis that they agree pointwise on the carrier,
and downstream theorems consume \(\psim\) only through these typed
fields.

Accordingly, the projection definition
\(\psim=\Pminus\psi\) and the resulting equivariance identity
\(\psim(Jx)=-\psim(x)\) are paper-side mathematical content and are
not derived inside Lean from the ledger's \(\psi\) and \(\Jiso\)
fields.  A future strengthening would add a response-side
\(\Pminus\) primitive (or an anti-action on the readout codomain)
sufficient to derive the projection identity and equivariance
internally; in the present typed-cone scope these are carried as
typed-interface data.
