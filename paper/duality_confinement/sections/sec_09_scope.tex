\section{Scope and non-claims}
\label{sec:scope}

Several scope boundaries deserve explicit statement so the paper's
main claim remains sharp.  The point of this section is not to
weaken the apparatus developed above, but to identify exactly which
structural content is being invoked, which encoding choices are
being made, and which extensions remain outside the present paper.

\paragraph{SDTC is a named structural law, not a derived theorem.}
Self-Dual Trace Confinement is named as recognition source:
structural content supplied by formed-layer closure per Foundations
I, not derived from framework primitives alone.  Thus this paper
does not present SDTC as a theorem derived from earlier Six Birds
primitives.  The duality-confinement membrane theorem of
\Cref{sec:master_theorem} is the apparatus theorem proved here: it
takes domination records as input and extracts fixed-locus
confinement.  The load-bearing structural-law content is the
recognition source that supplies those records on a formed
self-dual layer.  Likewise, the paper establishes the forward
apparatus implication from domination records to confinement; it
does not establish a bidirectional equivalence between the SDTC
structural claim and the existence of those records.

\paragraph{The mechanized theorem is at the typed-cone level.}
The master theorem is mechanized at the abstract positive-cone
level with an explicit typed trace functional.  The cone records
the order \(\preceq\), the trace functional \(\trace\), monotonicity
of trace, and the principle that a positive element with trace zero
is the zero element.  Operator-theoretic generality remains an open
extension: trace-class operators on Hilbert space, Hilbert--Schmidt
operators, Schatten norms, and trace-norm topology are not
developed here as a full analytic model.  The classical
trace-class setting is the motivating model for the abstraction,
but the present formal theorem is the typed positive-cone squeeze
theorem.

\paragraph{The scalar budget uses an AM--GM hypothesis.}
The AM--GM step in the optimized scalar trace budget of
\Cref{prop:duality_confinement:optimized-trace-budget} is tracked
by the formalization harness as a typed-Scalar axiom; a substantive
derivation requires introducing typed real-arithmetic structure.
Accordingly, this paper does not claim that the AM--GM inequality
has been derived inside the abstract scalar encoding.  The
proposition records the optimized budget as the scalar projection
needed by the defected-ledger apparatus, with the arithmetic
limitation disclosed explicitly.

\paragraph{Cross-substrate generalizations are structural predictions.}
The discussion of CPT symmetry, gauge invariance, Hermitian
conjugation, particle--antiparticle symmetry, quantum
self-adjointness, and function-field RH is predictive rather than
theorem-level.  These are structural predictions that genuine
involutive self-duality at the formed-layer level implies
fixed-locus confinement, and their plausibility comes from
consistency with observed physical and mathematical symmetries.
They are not established results in this paper; each remains a
single-substrate validation problem outside the abstract apparatus
developed here.

\paragraph{No novelty is claimed for standard operator theory.}
Douglas factorization, trace functionals, Loewner-style order,
Markov-type estimates, AM--GM, and standard rank-one trace
identities are treated as classical mathematical constructions.
The contribution of this paper is not to rebrand those results as
new.  The contribution is the structural packaging: the
involutive object ledger, the anti-invariant ledger, the
completed-domination bridge, the exhaustive moving ledger, and the
master theorem that converts vanishing trace budgets into
fixed-locus confinement.  Classical operator-theoretic references
are background for the operator model, not novelty claims of this
paper.

These boundaries identify which framework content is supplied as
recognition source, which typed abstractions are used in the
formalization, and what would count as an extension of the present
result.  \(\GamSDTC\) is the named structural law, and the master
theorem is its apparatus theorem.
