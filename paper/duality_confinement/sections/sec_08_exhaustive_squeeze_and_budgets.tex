\section{Exhaustive squeeze and defected budgets}
\label{sec:exhaustive_squeeze_and_budgets}

The master theorem is stated for a sequence of ambient domination
records \(\AX\preceq B_n\) whose traces vanish.  In applications,
the available records often arrive in a less direct form.  One has
finite-window ledgers \(A_{X,n}\), transports \(\iota_n\) that
place those finite objects into the completed ambient cone, and
tail terms \(T_n\) measuring what the window has not yet captured.
The exhaustive moving ledger formalizes exactly this
finite-window-plus-tail situation.

\begin{definition}[Exhaustive moving ledger]
\label{def:duality_confinement:exhaustive-moving-ledger}
An \emph{exhaustive moving ledger} over an involutive object ledger
\(\IOL\) consists of a sequence of positive cone elements
\(A_{X,n}\), inclusion transports \(\iota_n\), and positive tails
\(T_n\) with \(0\preceq T_n\), such that, for every \(n\),
\[
  \AX\preceq \iota_n A_{X,n}\iota_n^*+T_n.
\]
It is \emph{vanishing-exhaustive} when there are positive bounds
\(B_n\) satisfying
\[
  A_{X,n}\preceq B_n
\]
and
\[
  \trace(\iota_n B_n\iota_n^*)+\trace T_n\to0
  \qquad (n\to\infty).
\]
\end{definition}

The finite-window term \(A_{X,n}\) should be read as a truncated
approximation to the completed anti-invariant ledger \(\AX\).  The
transport \(\iota_n\) moves that truncated positive element into
the ambient cone where it can be compared with \(\AX\), and the
tail \(T_n\) records the residual positive contribution not
captured by the window.  The point of the definition is not to
choose a particular analytic truncation; it is to isolate the
trace-theoretic data needed for the same squeeze mechanism used in
\Cref{sec:master_theorem}.

Vanishing-exhaustiveness says that the transported upper bound and
the tail become invisible to the trace in the limit.  This is the
right abstraction for progressively finer finite windows,
increasing carrier subspaces, or any approximation scheme in which
the completed ledger is controlled by a transported finite piece
plus a tail whose trace can be driven to zero.

\begin{theorem}[Exhaustive ledger squeeze]
\label{thm:duality_confinement:exhaustive-squeeze}
Let \(\IOL\) be an involutive object ledger with anti-invariant
ledger \(\AX\).  If \(\IOL\) admits a vanishing-exhaustive moving
ledger, then \(\AX=0\).  If, moreover, the anti-invariant readout
\(\psim\) is qualitatively separating, then
\[
  \mu\bigl(X\setminus\Fix(J)\bigr)=0.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For each \(n\), exhaustiveness gives
\[
  \AX\preceq \iota_n A_{X,n}\iota_n^*+T_n.
\]
The transported domination bound \(A_{X,n}\preceq B_n\) gives
\[
  \iota_n A_{X,n}\iota_n^*
  \preceq
  \iota_n B_n\iota_n^*.
\]
By trace monotonicity in the typed positive cone,
\[
  0\leq\trace\AX
  \leq
  \trace(\iota_n B_n\iota_n^*)+\trace T_n.
\]
The right-hand side tends to zero by vanishing-exhaustiveness, so
\(\trace\AX=0\).  Positivity-to-zero in the cone gives
\(\AX=0\).  If \(\psim\) is qualitatively separating, then
\Cref{thm:duality_confinement:separation-confinement} gives the
displayed fixed-locus confinement.
\end{proof}

Lean proves the exhaustive ledger squeeze in this typed-cone
formulation; the formalization appendix records the
declaration-level correspondence.  The remainder of this section treats the
case where the finite-window-plus-tail approximation comes with a
structured defect budget optimized by a scalar parameter.

The exhaustive squeeze covers the case where the domination
records vanish after transport and tail control.  A common
refinement keeps track of two separate defects: one from the source
or membrane side, and one from the bridge or domination side.  A
positive scalar parameter then re-weights the two defects against
one another before the trace is minimized.

\begin{definition}[Defected obstruction budget]
\label{def:duality_confinement:defected-budget}
A \emph{defected obstruction budget} at level \(n\) is a domination
record of the form
\[
  \AX
  \preceq
  (1+t)\bigl(\Kminus_{n}+E_{\mathrm{src},n}\bigr)
  +(1+t^{-1})E_{\mathrm{br},n},
\]
where \(t>0\), where \(E_{\mathrm{src},n}\) is a source defect, and
where \(E_{\mathrm{br},n}\) is a bridge defect.  Both defects are
positive cone elements.  The unparameterized budget is obtained by
setting \(t=1\), which adds the two defect contributions without
optimization.
\end{definition}

The parameter \(t\) allocates weight between the source-side
contribution \(\Kminus_{n}+E_{\mathrm{src},n}\) and the bridge
defect \(E_{\mathrm{br},n}\).  The reciprocal factor
\(t^{-1}\) reflects the usual two-term balancing: increasing the
weight on one side decreases the weight available to the other.
This budget shape is motivational; the load-bearing scalar result
is the optimized trace estimate that follows.

\begin{proposition}[Optimized scalar trace budget]
\label{prop:duality_confinement:optimized-trace-budget}
For a defected obstruction budget at level \(n\), set
\[
  a_n=\trace(\Kminus_{n}+E_{\mathrm{src},n}),
  \qquad
  b_n=\trace E_{\mathrm{br},n},
\]
with \(a_n,b_n\geq0\).  Then
\[
  \inf_{t>0}
  \bigl[(1+t)a_n+(1+t^{-1})b_n\bigr]
  =
  \bigl(\sqrt{a_n}+\sqrt{b_n}\bigr)^2.
\]
In particular, if \(a_n\to0\) and \(b_n\to0\), then the optimized
trace budget tends to zero.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For \(a_n,b_n>0\), minimize
\[
  (1+t)a_n+(1+t^{-1})b_n
  =
  a_n+b_n+t a_n+\frac{b_n}{t}
\]
over \(t>0\).  The derivative vanishes at
\[
  t=\sqrt{\frac{b_n}{a_n}}.
\]
Substitution gives
\[
  a_n+b_n+2\sqrt{a_n b_n}
  =
  \bigl(\sqrt{a_n}+\sqrt{b_n}\bigr)^2.
\]
Equivalently, the AM--GM inequality applied to
\(t a_n\) and \(b_n/t\) gives
\[
  t a_n+\frac{b_n}{t}\geq2\sqrt{a_n b_n},
\]
with equality at the displayed value of \(t\).  If one of
\(a_n,b_n\) is zero, the same value is obtained as an infimum by
letting \(t\) tend to \(0\) or to \(+\infty\), as appropriate.
\end{proof}

In our typed-Scalar encoding, the AM--GM step in the proof of the
optimized scalar trace budget is taken as a typed-Scalar hypothesis
rather than derived from a quadratic-non-negativity axiom on a
typed real-arithmetic structure.  The optimized scalar trace budget
is thus tracked by the formalization harness as a typed projection
over the AM--GM hypothesis; a substantive Lean derivation would
require extending the abstract-Scalar encoding with sufficient
arithmetic to express the inequality
\((\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b})^2\geq0\).
