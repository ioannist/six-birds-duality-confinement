\section{The conditional landing chain}
\label{sec:landing_chain}

The previous section identified the structural input needed by the RH
closure: \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) supplies domination records for
\(\AZ\).  The landing chain imports the abstract theorem from
\cite{TsiokosSDTC2026} that acts on such records, reproduced verbatim
here for reference.

\begin{theorem*}[Duality-confinement membrane theorem, \cite{TsiokosSDTC2026}\footnote{Imported in Lean as a cross-paper reference to \texttt{masterTheorem} from \nolinkurl{DualityConfinement.MasterTheorem} in \cite{TsiokosSDTC2026}; the bridge to the RH ledger is recorded in App~\ref{app:formalization}.}]
Let \(\IOL\) be an involutive object ledger with carrier \(X\),
involution \(J\), measure \(\mu\), anti-invariant readout
\(\psim\), and anti-invariant ledger \(\AX\).  Assume that
\(\psim\) is qualitatively separating.  Suppose there is a sequence
of positive cone elements \(B_n\) such that
\[
  \AX\preceq B_n
  \qquad (n=1,2,\ldots)
\]
and
\[
  \trace B_n\to0
  \qquad (n\to\infty).
\]
Then \(\AX=0\) in the typed positive cone.  Consequently,
\[
  \mu\bigl(X\setminus\Fix(J)\bigr)=0.
\]
\end{theorem*}

For the RH application, the theorem needs an order \(\preceq\), a
trace functional \(\trace\), and a separating anti-invariant readout.
It consumes domination records; their source here is
\(\GamSDTCSelberg\), introduced in \Cref{sec:recognition_source}.

For the Selberg-class instance, the abstract ledger is \(\Sel\)
restricted to its typed zero ledger: \(X\) is the zero-carrier
underlying \(\Znt\), \(J\) is \(\JL\), \(\mu\) is the
multiplicity-weighted zero measure, \(\psim\) is \(\psimRH\), and
\(\AX\) is \(\AZ\).

\begin{theorem}[Duality-confinement master theorem, applied to \(\Sel\)\footnote{Verified in Lean as \nolinkurl{RH.DCMasterApplied.dcMasterApplied} via typed-bridge composition over the recognition-source carrier; see App~\ref{app:formalization}.}]
\label{thm:rh:dc-master-applied}
Let \(\Sel\) be the saturated completed Selberg trace closure with
functional-equation involution \(\JL\), separating anti-invariant
readout \(\psimRH\), and anti-invariant zero ledger \(\AZ\).  Suppose
there is a sequence \(B_n\) of positive cone elements such that
\[
  \AZ\preceq B_n
  \qquad (n=1,2,\ldots)
\]
and
\[
  \trace B_n\to0
  \qquad (n\to\infty).
\]
Then \(\AZ=0\) in the typed positive cone associated to \(\Sel\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
This is the duality-confinement membrane theorem of
\citet{TsiokosSDTC2026} applied under the substitutions just listed.
The qualitative-separation hypothesis is the concrete calculation of
\Cref{def:rh:psi-minus-rh}: \(\psimRH(s)=0\) exactly on
\(\Fix(\JL)\), and \(\psimRH(\JL(s))=-\psimRH(s)\).  The domination
records are precisely the records supplied by the recognition source
of \Cref{obl:rh:gamma-sdtc-selberg}.  Once the records give
\(\AZ\preceq B_n\) and \(\trace B_n\to0\), the typed-cone squeeze
forces \(\AZ=0\).
\end{proof}

The hypotheses of the membrane theorem are satisfied under these
substitutions.  Qualitative separation follows from
\Cref{def:rh:psi-minus-rh}: \(\psimRH(s)=0\) iff \(\Real(s)=1/2\),
and \(\psimRH(\JL(s))=-\psimRH(s)\).  The domination records are
those supplied by \Cref{obl:rh:gamma-sdtc-selberg}.  The passage from
``\(\psim\) vanishes \(\mu\)-almost everywhere'' to the shell equality
\(\AZ=0\) is a coherence assumption of the recognition source.

Thus the conditional input from \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) becomes
\(\AZ=0\), and Theorem T turns this into
\(\forall\,\rho\in\Znt,\ \Real(\rho)=1/2\).

\begin{theorem}[Conditional landing chain\footnote{Verified in Lean as \nolinkurl{RH.RHConditional.rhConditional}, taking a value of \nolinkurl{GammaSdtcSelberg shell} as an explicit hypothesis; see App~\ref{app:formalization}.}]
\label{thm:rh:conditional}
Let \(\Sel\) be a saturated completed Selberg trace closure.  Under
the standing assumption that \(\Sel\) is Foundations-II admissible,
and given the recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\)---the
Selberg-class instance of Self-Dual Trace Confinement supplied by
\citet{TsiokosSDTC2026}---the Riemann hypothesis holds on the typed
zero ledger \(\Znt\) of \(\Sel\): every typed nontrivial zero
\(\rho\in\Znt\) satisfies
\[
  \Real(\rho)=\frac{1}{2}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The proof is a direct three-step chain.  First, the recognition
source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\), as stated in
\Cref{obl:rh:gamma-sdtc-selberg}, supplies a sequence \(B_n\) of
positive cone elements with
\[
  \AZ\preceq B_n,
  \qquad
  \trace B_n\to0.
\]
Second, \Cref{thm:rh:dc-master-applied} applies the
duality-confinement master theorem to this Selberg-class instance and
therefore gives \(\AZ=0\) in the typed positive cone associated to
\(\Sel\).  Third, the forward direction of translation theorem T,
\Cref{thm:rh:translation-T-forward}, converts \(\AZ=0\) into
\[
  \forall\,\rho\in\Znt,\ \Real(\rho)=\frac{1}{2}.
\]
\end{proof}

The conditional structure is essential.  The hypotheses are the
standing Foundations-II admissibility of \(\Sel\)
(\Cref{def:rh:sat-sel-shell}), the closure-content discipline that
treats \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) as supplied structural content, and the
typed real-coordinate identification of
\Cref{sec:involution_and_ledger}.  Outside the Six Birds framing the
theorem reads as the conditional implication
\[
  \GamSDTCSelberg \Longrightarrow \mathrm{RH},
\]
not as an unconditional proof of RH in standard ZFC.
