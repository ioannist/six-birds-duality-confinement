\section{The conditional landing chain}
\label{sec:landing_chain}

The previous section identified the structural input needed by the
RH closure: the recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) supplies a
sequence of domination records for the anti-invariant zero ledger
\(\AZ\).  The landing chain begins by importing the one abstract
theorem from \cite{TsiokosSDTC2026} that acts on such a sequence,
reproduced verbatim here for reference.

\begin{theorem}[Duality-confinement membrane theorem]
Let \(\IOL\) be an involutive object ledger with carrier \(X\),
involution \(J\), measure \(\mu\), anti-invariant readout
\(\psim\), and anti-invariant ledger \(\AX\).  Assume that
\(\psim\) is qualitatively separating.  Suppose there is a sequence
of positive cone elements \(B_n\) such that
\[
  \AX\preceq B_n
  \qquad (n=1,2,\ldots)
\]
and
\[
  \trace B_n\to0
  \qquad (n\to\infty).
\]
Then \(\AX=0\) in the typed positive cone.  Consequently,
\[
  \mu\bigl(X\setminus\Fix(J)\bigr)=0.
\]
\end{theorem}

Two points about the imported theorem matter for the RH application.
First, it is a typed positive-cone statement: it needs an order
\(\preceq\), a trace functional \(\trace\), and a separating
anti-invariant readout, but it does not ask for an independent
classical analytic proof of RH.  Second, the theorem consumes
domination records; it does not supply their source.  In the present
paper that source is \(\GamSDTCSelberg\), introduced in
\Cref{sec:recognition_source}.

Applying the theorem to the Selberg-class instance, the abstract
involutive object ledger becomes the saturated completed Selberg
trace closure \(\Sel\) restricted to its typed zero ledger.  The
abstract carrier \(X\) is the zero-carrier underlying \(\Znt\); the
involution \(J\) becomes the functional-equation involution
\(\JL\); the measure \(\mu\) becomes the multiplicity-weighted zero
measure; the separating readout \(\psim\) becomes the signed
real-part displacement \(\psimRH\); and the anti-invariant ledger
\(\AX\) becomes the anti-invariant zero ledger \(\AZ\).

\begin{theorem}[Duality-confinement master theorem, applied to \(\Sel\)]
\label{thm:rh:dc-master-applied}
Let \(\Sel\) be the saturated completed Selberg trace closure with
functional-equation involution \(\JL\), separating anti-invariant
readout \(\psimRH\), and anti-invariant zero ledger \(\AZ\).  Suppose
there is a sequence \(B_n\) of positive cone elements such that
\[
  \AZ\preceq B_n
  \qquad (n=1,2,\ldots)
\]
and
\[
  \trace B_n\to0
  \qquad (n\to\infty).
\]
Then \(\AZ=0\) in the typed positive cone associated to \(\Sel\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
This is the duality-confinement membrane theorem of
\citet{TsiokosSDTC2026} applied under the substitutions just listed.
The qualitative-separation hypothesis is the concrete calculation of
\Cref{def:rh:psi-minus-rh}: \(\psimRH(s)=0\) exactly on
\(\Fix(\JL)\), and \(\psimRH(\JL(s))=-\psimRH(s)\).  The domination
records are precisely the records supplied by the recognition source
of \Cref{obl:rh:gamma-sdtc-selberg}.  Once the records give
\(\AZ\preceq B_n\) and \(\trace B_n\to0\), the typed-cone squeeze
forces \(\AZ=0\).
\end{proof}

A disclosure about the Lean formulation belongs here.  In Lean the
application is not realized by re-instantiating the abstract
involutive object ledger of \citet{TsiokosSDTC2026} on the shell;
instead it is realized as a typed-bridge composition over the
recognition-source carrier
\(\gamma:\GamSDTCSelberg\,\mathit{shell}\) of
\Cref{obl:rh:gamma-sdtc-selberg}.  The carrier supplies the
duality-confinement apparatus (an involutive object ledger, a
separating readout, an anti-invariant ledger, completed-domination
records, and the typed-cone primitives) together with explicit
bridge propositions linking that apparatus to the shell: a
same-readout coherence between the separation and
anti-invariant-ledger readouts, a visible-support refinement, and
the implication \(\mathtt{mu\_zero\_of\_ae}\) translating
``\(\psim\) vanishes \(\mu\)-almost everywhere'' into the shell
equality \(\AZ=0\).  These bridge propositions are typed hypotheses,
not derivations: the DC apparatus and its readout-side data are
supplied alongside the assertion that they cohere with the shell,
not constructed from the shell.  The Lean theorem composes the
imported master theorem with these bridge fields to obtain
\(\AZ=0\); the formalization appendix records the declaration-level
correspondence and the bridge-field signatures.

The bridge theorem is the point at which the conditional input from
\(\GamSDTCSelberg\) becomes the concrete ledger equality \(\AZ=0\).
In the duality-confinement notation, the fixed-locus consequence
says that the typed zero-ledger mass is confined to \(\Fix(\JL)\),
the critical line.  In the notation of the present paper, Theorem T
gives the sharper pointwise formulation on the finite typed ledger:
every \(\rho\in\Znt\) has \(\Real(\rho)=1/2\).

The headline conditional theorem composes \(\GamSDTCSelberg\), the
bridge theorem above, and the forward direction of Theorem T.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional landing chain]
\label{thm:rh:conditional}
Let \(\Sel\) be a saturated completed Selberg trace closure.  Under
the standing assumption that \(\Sel\) is Foundations-II admissible,
and given the recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\)---the
Selberg-class instance of Self-Dual Trace Confinement supplied by
\citet{TsiokosSDTC2026}---the Riemann hypothesis holds on the typed
zero ledger \(\Znt\) of \(\Sel\): every typed nontrivial zero
\(\rho\in\Znt\) satisfies
\[
  \Real(\rho)=\frac{1}{2}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The proof is a direct three-step chain.  First, the recognition
source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\), as stated in
\Cref{obl:rh:gamma-sdtc-selberg}, supplies a sequence \(B_n\) of
positive cone elements with
\[
  \AZ\preceq B_n,
  \qquad
  \trace B_n\to0.
\]
Second, \Cref{thm:rh:dc-master-applied} applies the
duality-confinement master theorem to this Selberg-class instance and
therefore gives \(\AZ=0\) in the typed positive cone associated to
\(\Sel\).  Third, the forward direction of translation theorem T,
\Cref{thm:rh:translation-T-forward}, converts \(\AZ=0\) into
\[
  \forall\,\rho\in\Znt,\ \Real(\rho)=\frac{1}{2}.
\]
\end{proof}

The same composition admits an equivalent audit-closure rendering.
In the language of \citet{TsiokosAOR2026}, the recognition source
\(\GamSDTCSelberg\), the bridge theorem
\Cref{thm:rh:dc-master-applied}, and translation theorem T jointly
certify the shell as a refinement-stable
audited-operational-realisability instance,
\[
  \Sel\in\mathrm{RefStableAOR}^{\mathrm{ref}}_{S_{\mathrm{RH}}},
\]
of which the critical-line statement on \(\Znt\) is the
typed-zero-ledger readout.  \Cref{sec:rh-aor-instance} unfolds this
form: the eight-stratum / twelve-type residual atlas of the AOR
meta-theory is consumed by the literal classification record
assembled from the shell, the recognition source, and the bridge
fields, and the AOR-instance theorem composes the seven discharge
atoms through the local refinement-stable predicate.  The
membership reading is not an additional theorem about \(\Sel\); it
is the formed-layer certification that the conditional landing
chain delivers its conclusion through an audit-closed apparatus,
with the critical-line statement \(\Real(\rho)=1/2\) on \(\Znt\) as
the readout.

The conditional structure of this result is essential.  Three
hypotheses are in force: the standing Foundations-II admissibility
of \(\Sel\) (\Cref{def:rh:sat-sel-shell}); the Six Birds
closure-content discipline that treats \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) as
supplied structural content of the formed self-dual Selberg trace
layer; and the typed real-coordinate identification described in
\Cref{sec:involution_and_ledger}.  Outside the Six Birds framing the
theorem reads as the conditional implication
\[
  \GamSDTCSelberg \Longrightarrow \mathrm{RH},
\]
not as an unconditional proof of RH in standard ZFC.  The
formalization preserves this conditional structure: the landing
chain takes a value of the recognition-source carrier as an
explicit hypothesis parameter.

In the BirdInt judgment vocabulary of
\cite{TsiokosFoundationsIII2026}, the same conditional theorem is
the accepted judgment
\(\GamSDTCSelberg;\,\Sel;\,\Itr \vdash
  (\forall\,\rho\in\Znt,\ \Real(\rho)=1/2) : \mathrm{accepted}\),
where \(\Itr\) is the saturated Selberg trace instrument of
\Cref{def:rh:sat-sel-shell}; this rendering is decorative and not
used in the proof above.

Lean composes the conditional landing chain (per the formalization
appendix): the formal realization takes a value of the
typed-structure carrier representing \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) as input,
applies \Cref{thm:rh:dc-master-applied} through that carrier's
bridge fields to obtain \(\AZ=0\) on the shell, and then applies
\Cref{thm:rh:translation-T-forward}.
