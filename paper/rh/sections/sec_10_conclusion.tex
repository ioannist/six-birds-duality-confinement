\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The paper has established the RH closure in its conditional form.
Under the standing Foundations-II admissibility assumption on
\(\Sel\), and with the Selberg-class recognition source
\(\GamSDTCSelberg\) supplied by the SDTC structural law of
\citet{TsiokosSDTC2026}, the conditional landing chain certifies
\(\Sel\in\mathrm{RefStableAOR}^{\mathrm{ref}}_{S_{\mathrm{RH}}}\)
in the sense of \citet{TsiokosAOR2026}.  Its typed-ledger readout is
\(\rho\in\Znt \Rightarrow \Real(\rho)=1/2\).  The recognition source
supplies domination records for \(\AZ\); the duality-confinement
membrane theorem converts those records into \(\AZ=0\); translation
theorem T converts \(\AZ=0\) into the critical-line statement; and
\Cref{thm:rh:aor-instance} records that the same composition
discharges the audit-closure residual atlas.

The conditional structure is essential.  Outside the Six Birds
framework, the result reads as
\(\GamSDTCSelberg\Longrightarrow\mathrm{RH}\) on the typed zero
ledger; it is not an unconditional proof of RH in standard ZFC.
Acceptance turns on two supplied pieces of formed-layer structure:
the recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\), and the admissibility of
\(\Sel\) recorded by the \(\mathrm{Audit}_L\) field.  The
AOR-realisability framing gives the audit-closure reading of that
conclusion, but does not collapse these hypotheses into unmentioned
background assumptions.

Three extension targets remain.  First, the admissibility of
\(\Sel\) from the seven Foundations-II schemas would be derived
rather than carried as a standing hypothesis.  Second, the typed
real-coordinate parameter would be connected to the classical
function \(\Real(\rho)\) through analytic-number-theoretic
infrastructure, lifting the real-part representation choice disclosed
earlier.  Third, the analogous program for primitive Selberg-class
\(L\)-functions would require a separate typed shell, admissibility
verification, and recognition-source provenance argument for each
\(L\).  The paper accordingly defers unconditional standard-ZFC RH,
any claim to circumvent classical RH attack barriers in their native
sense, and GRH for general primitive \(L\)-functions.
