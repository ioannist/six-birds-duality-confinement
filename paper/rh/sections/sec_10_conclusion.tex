\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The paper has established the RH closure in its conditional form.  If
\(\Sel\) is a saturated completed Selberg trace closure satisfying
the standing Foundations-II admissibility assumption, and if the
Selberg-class recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) is supplied by
the SDTC structural law of \citet{TsiokosSDTC2026}, then every
typed nontrivial zero in \(\Znt\) lies on the critical line:
\[
  \rho\in\Znt \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Real(\rho)=\frac12 .
\]
The proof is the two-step landing chain developed above.  The
recognition source supplies domination records for \(\AZ\); the
duality-confinement membrane theorem of \citet{TsiokosSDTC2026}
converts those records into \(\AZ=0\); and translation theorem T
converts \(\AZ=0\) into the critical-line statement on the typed
zero ledger.

The conditional structure is essential.  Outside the Six Birds
framework, the result should be read as the implication
\[
  \GamSDTCSelberg \Longrightarrow \mathrm{RH}
\]
on the typed zero ledger, not as an unconditional proof of RH in
standard ZFC.  Acceptance of the conclusion turns on two supplied
pieces of formed-layer structure: the recognition source
\(\GamSDTCSelberg\), which is closure content of the formed self-dual
Selberg trace layer, and the admissibility of \(\Sel\), recorded by
the \(\mathrm{Audit}_L\) field of the shell.  The theorem is therefore
sharp as a conditional theorem, and these hypotheses are not
collapsed into unmentioned background assumptions.

Three extension targets remain.  First, a substantive Lean derivation
of the admissibility of \(\Sel\) from the seven Foundations-II schemas
would replace the construction-time audit field by derived predicates.
Second, the typed real-coordinate carrier should be connected inside
the formal system to the classical function \(\Real(\rho)\) by
analytic-number-theoretic infrastructure, lifting the real-part
abstraction disclosed earlier.  Third, the analogous program for
primitive Selberg-class \(L\)-functions would require a separate
typed shell, admissibility verification, and recognition-source
provenance argument for each \(L\).  Thus the paper explicitly defers
unconditional standard-ZFC RH, any claim to circumvent classical RH
attack barriers in their native sense, and GRH for general primitive
\(L\)-functions.
