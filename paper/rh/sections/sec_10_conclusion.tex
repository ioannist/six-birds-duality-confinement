\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The paper has established the RH closure in its conditional form.
If \(\Sel\) is a saturated completed Selberg trace closure
satisfying the standing Foundations-II admissibility assumption, and
if the Selberg-class recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) is
supplied by the SDTC structural law of \citet{TsiokosSDTC2026}, then
the conditional landing chain certifies the shell as a
refinement-stable audited-operational-realisability instance in the
sense of \citet{TsiokosAOR2026},
\[
  \Sel\in\mathrm{RefStableAOR}^{\mathrm{ref}}_{S_{\mathrm{RH}}},
\]
of which the critical-line statement on the typed zero ledger
\(\Znt\) is the readout:
\[
  \rho\in\Znt \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Real(\rho)=\frac12 .
\]
The proof is the two-step landing chain developed above.  The
recognition source supplies domination records for \(\AZ\); the
duality-confinement membrane theorem of \citet{TsiokosSDTC2026}
converts those records into \(\AZ=0\); translation theorem T
converts \(\AZ=0\) into the critical-line statement on the typed
zero ledger; and the AOR-instance theorem
(\Cref{thm:rh:aor-instance}) certifies that the same composition
discharges every atom of the eight-stratum / twelve-type residual
atlas of the audit-closure meta-theory, with the seven discharge
records assembled literally from the shell, the recognition source,
and the bridge fields.

The conditional structure is essential.  Outside the Six Birds
framework, the result reads as the implication
\[
  \GamSDTCSelberg \Longrightarrow \mathrm{RH}
\]
on the typed zero ledger, with \(\mathrm{RH}\) delivered as the
typed-ledger readout of the audit-closed realisability instance.
It is not an unconditional proof of RH in standard ZFC.  Acceptance
turns on two supplied pieces of formed-layer structure: the
recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\), which is closure content of
the formed self-dual Selberg trace layer, and the admissibility of
\(\Sel\), recorded by the \(\mathrm{Audit}_L\) field of the shell.
The theorem is therefore sharp as a conditional theorem; the
AOR-realisability framing names the audit-closure stratum at which
the closure delivers its conclusion, but does not collapse these
hypotheses into unmentioned background assumptions.  In the Lean
development the realisability-instance theorem is recorded as
partial because its conclusion is the local syntactic membership
predicate over the carrier fields rather than the full audit-closure
characterisation; the substantive recognition-source and
translation-interface discharges remain deferred to
\citet{TsiokosSDTC2026} and \Cref{sec:translation_theorem}.

Three extension targets remain.  First, a substantive Lean
derivation of the admissibility of \(\Sel\) from the seven
Foundations-II schemas would replace the construction-time audit
field by derived predicates.  Second, the typed real-coordinate
carrier should be connected inside the formal system to the
classical function \(\Real(\rho)\) through analytic-number-theoretic
infrastructure, lifting the real-part abstraction disclosed earlier.
Third, the analogous program for primitive Selberg-class
\(L\)-functions would require a separate typed shell, admissibility
verification, and recognition-source provenance argument for each
\(L\).  The paper accordingly defers unconditional standard-ZFC RH,
any claim to circumvent classical RH attack barriers in their native
sense, and GRH for general primitive \(L\)-functions.
