\section{The recognition source}
\label{sec:recognition_source}

\Cref{sec:translation_theorem} reduced RH on the typed zero ledger of
\(\Sel\) to the trace-side statement \(\AZ=0\).  The remaining
structural question is where that trace-side vanishing comes from.
The answer in this paper is a named recognition source: the
Selberg-class instance of Self-Dual Trace Confinement,
\(\GamSDTCSelberg\).  Paper 1 supplies the SDTC structural-law
framing; the present paper specializes that framing to the saturated
completed Selberg trace closure \(\Sel\) \cite{TsiokosSDTC2026}.

A recognition source is not a calculation internal to the proof of
Theorem T.  It is structural content of the formed layer: typed data
which the formed closure recognizes as present.  In this case, the
content is precisely the completed domination-record sequence needed
by the duality-confinement master theorem.  Once that sequence is
available, the master theorem will force \(\AZ=0\), and Theorem T
will then convert \(\AZ=0\) into the critical-line statement.

\begin{definition}[The recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\)]
\label{obl:rh:gamma-sdtc-selberg}
On the saturated completed Selberg trace closure \(\Sel\), the
recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) supplies a sequence of
completed domination records \(B_n\) in the typed positive cone of
\(\Sel\) such that
\[
  0\preceq B_n,\qquad
  \AZ\preceq B_n,\qquad
  \trace(B_n)\to 0
  \quad\text{as } n\to\infty.
\]
Equivalently, \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) asserts that the
duality-confinement domination hypothesis holds for the RH ledger
inside \(\Sel\), with \(\JL\), \(\Znt\), and \(\AZ\) as the
specialized involution, zero ledger, and anti-invariant ledger.
\end{definition}

The definition records the load-bearing field of the recognition
source: the existence of the domination sequence.  The provenance of
that field is also part of the source.  It is the Selberg-class
instance of the SDTC structural law; it sits in the V-Differential
trace-state-only column; it is attached to the shell \(\Sel\), its
trace instrument, its functional-equation involution \(\JL\), its
zero ledger \(\Znt\), and its anti-invariant ledger \(\AZ\).  These
provenance components matter because the source is not an unnamed
black box.  It is a typed recognition object whose mathematical role
is exactly to provide the domination records that the abstract
duality-confinement apparatus requires.

An important encoding disclosure is required here.  In the Lean
formalization, \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) is realized as a typed structure
carrier: a record whose field encodes the existence of the completed
domination-record sequence on the typed shell, with trace tending to
zero.  The carrier is declared inline in the conditional-landing-chain
module, following the placement convention that a type lives next to
its only consumer.  It is not a Lean axiom.  The project's
forbidden-tokens rule bans the declarations \texttt{axiom},
\texttt{opaque}, \texttt{constant}, \texttt{sorry}, and
\texttt{admit} throughout the source tree.  Consequently, the
recognition source is supplied as a structural hypothesis encoded as
a typed record, not as a postulated logical axiom.  The conditional
landing-chain theorem takes a value of this carrier as an explicit
hypothesis parameter,
\[
  \gamma : \texttt{GammaSdtcSelberg}\ \texttt{shell}.
\]

This encoding reflects the Six Birds closure discipline.  A formed
layer may carry structural content that is not re-derived from lower
framework primitives every time it is used.  The formalization
therefore records what the source supplies, and the later conditional
theorem is explicit about taking that source as a hypothesis.  The
result is a typed structural assumption with named fields, not a
free-standing theorem that the paper claims to derive and not an
unrestricted metatheoretic postulate.

The development history includes a three-route derivation sweep
testing whether \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) could be obtained directly from
framework primitives.  The three candidate paths were the framework's
primitive role inventory, the audited interaction calculus, and the
closure-formation discipline.  Each route was found insufficient to
produce the trace-vanishing domination sequence as an output.  This
is paper-prose due diligence, not a Lean-mechanized premise: it
supports the recognition-source framing by documenting that the SDTC
content is closure content of the formed layer, not a separately
derivable consequence of the primitive vocabulary.

This conclusion matches the one-way import from Paper 1.  Paper 1
names Self-Dual Trace Confinement as the structural law for
involutive self-dual formed closures and supplies the abstract
duality-confinement apparatus \cite{TsiokosSDTC2026}.  Paper 2 does
not rederive that structural law.  It instantiates the Selberg-class
recognition source on \(\Sel\), exposes the domination records it
carries, and then uses those records in the conditional landing chain.

With \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) in place, the remaining composition is
straightforward: apply the duality-confinement master theorem to the
domination records to obtain \(\AZ=0\), then apply Theorem T in the
forward direction to obtain the critical-line statement on \(\Znt\).
That composition is the subject of \Cref{sec:landing_chain}.
