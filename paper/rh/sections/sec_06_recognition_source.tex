\section{The recognition source}
\label{sec:recognition_source}

\Cref{sec:translation_theorem} reduced RH on the typed zero ledger of
\(\Sel\) to the trace-side statement \(\AZ=0\).  The remaining
structural question is where that trace-side vanishing comes from.
The answer in this paper is a named recognition source: the
Selberg-class instance of Self-Dual Trace Confinement,
\(\GamSDTCSelberg\).  \cite{TsiokosSDTC2026} supplies the SDTC structural-law
framing; the present paper specializes that framing to the saturated
completed Selberg trace closure \(\Sel\) \cite{TsiokosSDTC2026}.

The recognition source supplies structural content of the formed
layer rather than a calculation internal to the proof of Theorem T.
In this case the content is the completed domination-record sequence
required by the duality-confinement master theorem.  Once that
sequence is available, the master theorem forces \(\AZ=0\), and
Theorem T converts \(\AZ=0\) into the critical-line statement.

\begin{definition}[The recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\)]
\label{obl:rh:gamma-sdtc-selberg}
On the saturated completed Selberg trace closure \(\Sel\), the
recognition source \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) supplies a sequence of
completed domination records \(B_n\) in the typed positive cone of
\(\Sel\) such that
\[
  0\preceq B_n,\qquad
  \AZ\preceq B_n,\qquad
  \trace(B_n)\to 0
  \quad\text{as } n\to\infty.
\]
Equivalently, \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) asserts that the
duality-confinement domination hypothesis holds for the RH ledger
inside \(\Sel\), with \(\JL\), \(\Znt\), and \(\AZ\) as the
specialized involution, zero ledger, and anti-invariant ledger.
\end{definition}

The definition records the load-bearing field of the recognition
source: the existence of the domination sequence.  The provenance of
that field is also part of the source.  It is the Selberg-class
instance of the SDTC structural law; it sits in the V-Differential
trace-state-only column; it is attached to the shell \(\Sel\), its
trace instrument, its functional-equation involution \(\JL\), its
zero ledger \(\Znt\), and its anti-invariant ledger \(\AZ\).  These
provenance components matter because the source is not an unnamed
black box.  It is a typed recognition object whose mathematical role
is exactly to provide the domination records that the abstract
duality-confinement apparatus requires.

An important encoding disclosure is required here.  In the Lean
formalization, \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) is realized as a typed structure
carrier: a record packaging, in one place, the entire
duality-confinement apparatus needed by the bridge theorem
(involutive object ledger, separating anti-invariant readout,
anti-invariant ledger, completed-domination records, and the
typed-cone primitives \(\preceq\), \(\trace\), positivity-to-zero)
\emph{together with} the bridge propositions that link this
apparatus to the shell's anti-invariant zero ledger.  The bridge
propositions---in particular the implication from the abstract
``\(\psi_-\) vanishes \(\mu\)-a.e.''  to
``\(\AZ = 0\) on the shell''---are
typed hypotheses, not derivations: the apparatus data is supplied
together with the assertion that it is coherent with the shell, not
constructed from the shell.  The carrier is declared inline in the
conditional-landing-chain module, following the placement convention
that a type lives next to its only consumer.

The carrier is not a Lean axiom.  The project's forbidden-tokens
rule bans \texttt{axiom}, \texttt{opaque}, \texttt{constant},
\texttt{sorry}, and \texttt{admit} throughout the source tree.  The
recognition source is therefore supplied as a typed structural
hypothesis, not a postulated logical axiom, and the conditional
landing-chain theorem takes a value of this carrier as an explicit
hypothesis parameter,
\[
  \gamma : \texttt{GammaSdtcSelberg}\ \texttt{shell}.
\]

The development history includes a three-route derivation sweep
testing whether \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) could be obtained directly from
framework primitives.  The three candidate paths were the
framework's primitive role inventory, the audited interaction
calculus, and the closure-formation discipline; each was found
insufficient to produce the trace-vanishing domination sequence.
This is paper-prose due diligence, not a Lean-mechanized premise,
and it supports the recognition-source framing by documenting that
the SDTC content is closure content of the formed layer rather than
a separately derivable consequence of the primitive vocabulary.
\cite{TsiokosSDTC2026} names Self-Dual Trace Confinement as the
structural law for involutive self-dual formed closures and
supplies the abstract duality-confinement apparatus; the present
paper instantiates the Selberg-class recognition source on \(\Sel\)
and
uses the domination records it carries in the conditional landing
chain.

With \(\GamSDTCSelberg\) in place, the remaining composition is
straightforward: apply the duality-confinement master theorem to the
domination records to obtain \(\AZ=0\), then apply Theorem T in the
forward direction to obtain the critical-line statement on \(\Znt\).
That composition is the subject of \Cref{sec:landing_chain}.
