This file gives citation candidates grouped by the requested topic list. I have included DOI fields only where they could be reasonably verified from publisher or stable metadata sources, and I have omitted fields that remained uncertain rather than guessing. Where a source is canonical but old or difficult to cite precisely, I have supplied a stable URL or a clear note.
